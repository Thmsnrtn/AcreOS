# AcreOS Product Hunt Launch Plan

**Target timing:** Month 2-3 post-deploy, when at least 3-5 testimonials exist from beta users.

---

## Timing

**Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
**Launch time:** 12:01 AM Pacific Time (to maximize the full 24-hour voting window)

---

## Listing

**Tagline:** The AI operating system for land investors

**Description:**

AcreOS replaces the 5 tools land investors use to find, analyze, buy, and manage properties. It pulls from 18 free government data sources to generate instant due diligence reports, scores every parcel with a proprietary Land Credit Score (300-850), and handles everything from campaigns to seller-financed note management with built-in Dodd-Frank compliance.

Built for land investors who are tired of toggling between Pebble, spreadsheets, Mailchimp, and county GIS sites. One platform. Full lifecycle. AI that matches your communication style.

**Topics:** SaaS, Real Estate, Artificial Intelligence, PropTech

**First comment from maker:**

> I started investing in land two years ago and immediately hit the tool fragmentation problem. I needed a CRM, a campaign tool, a data platform, a spreadsheet for DD, another spreadsheet for note tracking, and I was still missing follow-ups.
>
> So I built AcreOS. 400K lines of TypeScript, 276 database tables, 18 free government data sources, and a Land Credit Score that rates parcels on a FICO-like 300-850 scale.
>
> The thing I'm most proud of: the AI matches your writing style. When it generates an offer letter or campaign copy, it sounds like you wrote it — because it learned from your previous messages.
>
> I'm a solo founder. I built every line. Looking for honest feedback and early adopters.
>
> Happy to answer any questions about the architecture, the data sources, or land investing in general.

## Pre-Launch Checklist (Week Before)

- [ ] Notify list of 20 people who will upvote and comment on launch day
  - Beta users (5-10)
  - Friends and family who understand the product (5)
  - Community contacts from land investing groups (5-10)
- [ ] Prepare 5 tweets/posts for launch day (schedule in advance)
- [ ] Blog posts published and ready to link: "18 Free Data Sources", "How I Automate My Pipeline", "Seller Finance Guide"
- [ ] Demo video uploaded and embeddable
- [ ] All screenshots captured and optimized (< 1MB each)
- [ ] GIF recorded and tested on Product Hunt preview
- [ ] First comment drafted and ready to paste immediately after launch
- [ ] Maker profile updated with photo and bio

## Launch Day Protocol

### 12:01 AM PT
- Submit the listing (queued the night before if possible)
- Post the first comment immediately
- Verify all images and links render correctly

### 6:00 AM PT
- Send email to the 20-person notify list: "AcreOS is live on Product Hunt! Here's the link: [link]. An upvote and comment would mean a lot."
- Post on personal Twitter/X: "Launching AcreOS on Product Hunt today. I built the platform I wish existed when I started land investing. [link]"
- Post on LinkedIn: longer-form version of the maker comment

### 9:00 AM PT
- Share in 1-2 land investing communities (where appropriate and not spammy): "I launched the tool I've been building on Product Hunt. Would love feedback from fellow investors."
- Check for comments — respond to every single one within 2 hours

### 12:00 PM PT
- Second tweet: "Halfway through launch day. The #1 question so far: [common question]. Here's the answer: [brief answer + link]"
- Check comments again — respond to all new ones

### 3:00 PM PT
- Third tweet: share a specific feature or data point that resonated with commenters

### 6:00 PM PT
- Final push: DM anyone in the notify list who hasn't engaged yet (gentle nudge)
- Thank everyone who commented

### End of Day
- Post a thank-you comment on the Product Hunt listing regardless of ranking
- Send a thank-you email to the notify list: "Here's how we did: [ranking]. Thank you for the support."

---

## Post-Launch (Days 2-7)

- Respond to any late comments (Product Hunt engagement continues for a few days)
- Follow up with anyone who expressed interest (DM on PH or email)
- Write a brief retrospective: total upvotes, comments, signups from PH, key feedback
- Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to the landing page (if applicable)
- Retarget PH visitors with the blog content and demo video
