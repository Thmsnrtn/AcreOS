# AcreOS Community Engagement Strategy

## Facebook — Land Investing (43K members)

**How to engage:** Answer questions about due diligence, data sources, county analysis, and note management. Provide genuine, detailed answers — not "use my tool." Share screenshots of free government data sources with explanations. Be the person who shows up with useful information consistently.

**What to share:**
- The "18 Free Data Sources" post (pure value, CTA only at the very bottom of the blog post)
- Deal analysis breakdowns (anonymized): "Here's how I evaluate a 10-acre parcel in Hudspeth County"
- Quick tips: "Always check FEMA NFHL before making an offer — here's how..."
- Once trust is established (2+ weeks of value): casual mention of AcreOS when directly relevant

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't post "check out my new app" — it will be deleted and you'll be flagged
- Don't reply to every question with "AcreOS does that" — it reads as spam
- Don't DM people unsolicited about the product
- Don't argue with moderators. Ever.

**Cadence:** 3-5 helpful comments per week. 1 original value post per 2 weeks.

## Reddit — r/LandInvesting

**How to engage:** Reddit rewards depth and honesty. Write detailed, specific answers with numbers. Share your methodology, not just conclusions. Reference specific data sources, calculations, and reasoning. Reddit users can smell marketing instantly — lead with substance.

**Best post types:**
- Case studies with real numbers (anonymized): "Analyzed 50 parcels in [county] — here's what the data showed"
- How-to guides: "How I run due diligence using free government data"
- Data analysis: "County-by-county land value trends in Texas (2023-2026)"
- Compliance deep dives: "Dodd-Frank for seller-financed land: what actually applies"

**Link back:** Only when directly relevant and after providing substantial value in the comment itself. Format: "I ended up building a tool that automates this — [link] — but the manual process works fine too." Never make the link the point of the comment.

**Cadence:** 2-3 comments per week. 1 original post per month. Quality over quantity.

## Facebook — REtipster Community

**Who's here:** Seth Williams' audience — more sophisticated investors, many with established businesses. They've seen every tool pitch. They respond to genuine expertise and data-driven insights.

**Engagement approach:**
- Share insights about data quality, compliance nuances, note management best practices
- Reference specific government data sources with URLs
- Share seller finance compliance information (this is Seth's audience — many do seller finance)
- Goal: get noticed as a knowledgeable contributor, ideally catch Seth's attention

**Why Seth matters:** A mention from Seth Williams (blog review, podcast guest spot, or community endorsement) would reach 10,000+ qualified leads. Don't pitch him directly — earn it by being useful in his community first.

## General Engagement Principles

1. **Value first, always.** Every post and comment should teach something useful. If it doesn't stand on its own without mentioning AcreOS, don't post it.

2. **Be specific.** "Use government data" is useless. "Query the FEMA NFHL API with lat/lng coordinates to get the flood zone designation" is useful.

3. **Be honest about limitations.** If someone asks about a feature AcreOS doesn't have, say so. Credibility compounds.

4. **Track what works.** Note which posts drive profile visits, DMs, or signups. Double down on those topics.

5. **Don't scale before it works.** Master one community before expanding to the next. Better to be known in one group than invisible in five.
