# AcreOS Competitive Teardown

## 1. Pebble (formerly PebbleREI)

**Pricing:**
- Starter: $49/mo (1 user, basic CRM)
- Growth: $99/mo (3 users, campaigns, automation)
- Scale: $199/mo (unlimited users, API access)

**Core Strength:** Purpose-built for land investors. Good CRM with lead management, campaign sending (email + direct mail), and basic deal tracking. The most recognized brand in the land investing software space.

**Key Weakness:** No due diligence automation. No data enrichment beyond what the user manually enters. No seller-financed note management. No AI features. Users must manually research every parcel using external tools, then enter the data. Campaign analytics are basic — open/click rates but no response attribution.

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Automated DD from 18 sources vs. manual research
- Land Credit Score (no equivalent in Pebble)
- Full note lifecycle management (Pebble has none)
- AI-generated offer letters and campaign copy matching user voice
- Deal Feed with daily scored opportunities (Pebble has no deal sourcing)
- Lower price point ($20-49 vs $49-199)

**User Sentiment:** Users frequently mention: "It works but it's expensive for what it does." "Wish it had built-in data." "Campaign builder is clunky." "No note tracking is a dealbreaker for seller finance." "Support is responsive but slow to ship new features."

## 2. PropTrac

**Pricing:**
- Solo: $39/mo
- Team: $79/mo
- Enterprise: Custom

**Core Strength:** Property tracking and portfolio management. Good at managing a portfolio of owned properties with value tracking, document storage, and basic financial reporting. Simpler UI than most competitors.

**Key Weakness:** Very limited acquisition tools. No campaign functionality. No offer generation. Focused on managing what you already own rather than finding and closing new deals. Effectively a post-acquisition tool only.

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Full acquisition pipeline (Deal Feed → offers → closing) vs. portfolio-only
- Campaign system for lead outreach
- Negotiation copilot for active deal-making
- Note management with compliance (PropTrac has basic financial tracking only)
- AI intelligence throughout vs. static data entry

**User Sentiment:** "Good for tracking my portfolio but I still need other tools for finding deals." "Simple and clean." "Wish it did more on the acquisition side." "No automation — everything is manual entry."

---

## 3. REsimpli

**Pricing:**
- Basic: $99/mo
- Pro: $299/mo
- Enterprise: $499/mo

**Core Strength:** Full CRM with strong marketing automation. Built for wholesalers and flippers with robust campaign tools, skip tracing integration, and lead routing. Good team management features for larger operations.

**Key Weakness:** Not built for land investing specifically. Missing land-specific data (soil, flood zones, elevation). No seller-finance note management. Expensive — the Pro tier ($299/mo) is required for most useful features. No AI scoring or property intelligence.

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Land-specific intelligence (LCS, soil, flood zones, environmental)
- 80% lower price point ($49 vs $299 for comparable features)
- Seller-financed note management (REsimpli has zero)
- 18 free data sources vs. paid skip tracing only
- BYOK model lets users bring their own data provider keys

**User Sentiment:** "Powerful but expensive." "Overkill for land — it's really built for houses." "Skip tracing is useful but I pay per lookup on top of the monthly fee." "Learning curve is steep." "Reporting is good once you figure it out."

## 4. InvestorFuse

**Pricing:**
- Starter: $147/mo
- Professional: $297/mo
- Scale: $497/mo

**Core Strength:** Lead management and follow-up automation. Strong at ensuring no lead falls through the cracks with automated task creation, follow-up sequences, and lead scoring based on engagement.

**Key Weakness:** House-focused, not land-focused. No property data enrichment. No due diligence tools. No note management. Very expensive for what it does. Lead scoring is based on engagement (opens, clicks, replies) rather than property quality or seller motivation.

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Land-specific intelligence vs. generic lead management
- 90% lower price point ($49 vs $497 for comparable features)
- Property-level scoring (LCS) vs. engagement-only scoring
- Full deal lifecycle vs. lead management only
- Note management, compliance, borrower portal (none in InvestorFuse)

**User Sentiment:** "Good at follow-ups but that's about it." "Way too expensive for a glorified to-do list." "Works well for house flippers, not great for land." "Integrations are limited." "The automation is genuinely useful if you have enough leads to justify the cost."

## 5. PropStream

**Pricing:**
- Standard: $99/mo
- Team: $199/mo (3 users)

**Core Strength:** Data. PropStream's core value is property data access — ownership records, tax assessments, mortgage info, pre-foreclosure lists, and skip tracing. The best data platform in the space for property research.

**Key Weakness:** It's a data tool, not an operating system. No CRM, no campaign sending, no deal tracking, no note management. Users export data from PropStream and import it into their CRM (usually Pebble or REsimpli). No AI features. The data is good but it's a research tool, not a workflow tool.

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Full workflow (CRM + campaigns + deals + notes + analytics) vs. data-only
- 18 free data sources provide comparable coverage for land DD
- Deal Feed surfaces opportunities proactively vs. manual search
- Land Credit Score synthesizes data into an actionable score vs. raw data display
- Campaign and offer tools eliminate the export-import workflow

**User Sentiment:** "The data is the best in the business." "But I still need 3 other tools to actually do anything with it." "Skip tracing works well." "Expensive for data I could get from county websites." "UI is dated but functional."

---

## 6. DealMachine

**Pricing:**
- Starter: $49/mo
- Growth: $99/mo
- Business: $249/mo

**Core Strength:** Driving for dollars and direct mail. The app lets you photograph properties while driving, instantly look up owner info, and send a direct mail piece. Strong mobile experience for field acquisition.

**Key Weakness:** Very narrow use case. Only useful for driving-for-dollars acquisition strategy. No due diligence, no deal management, no note servicing, no portfolio tracking. Once you've identified a lead, you need other tools for everything else. Not designed for land investing (house-focused).

**AcreOS Advantage:**
- Full platform vs. single-strategy tool
- Land-specific intelligence vs. house-focused
- Multiple acquisition channels (Deal Feed, campaigns, direct mail, manual) vs. driving only
- Complete post-acquisition workflow (deals, notes, portfolio)
- Lower price with more functionality ($49 vs $249 for full features)

**User Sentiment:** "Great for driving for dollars, nothing else." "The direct mail feature is convenient." "Not worth it if you don't drive for dollars regularly." "Owner lookup data is hit or miss." "Good companion app but not a primary tool."

## AcreOS Positioning

AcreOS sits in a space no competitor currently occupies: **the full-lifecycle intelligent operating system for land investors.**

**Horizontal breadth:** AcreOS covers the entire workflow from deal discovery to note payoff. Competitors either handle acquisition (Pebble, DealMachine) OR portfolio management (PropTrac) OR data (PropStream) — never all three.

**Vertical depth:** Within each workflow stage, AcreOS goes deeper than competitors. Due diligence pulls from 18 sources (vs. 0). Scoring uses a proprietary 6-dimension model (vs. basic engagement metrics). Note management includes compliance checking (vs. spreadsheets).

**AI intelligence:** No competitor has AI that learns the user's communication style, generates context-aware content, or autonomously handles operational tasks. AcreOS's AI isn't a chatbot — it's integrated into every workflow decision.

**Price advantage:** At $49/mo for full Pro access, AcreOS costs less than the cheapest plan of most competitors while delivering more functionality. This isn't a race to the bottom — it's possible because the 18 free data sources eliminate the per-lookup costs that force competitors to charge more.

**The network effect moat:** Every transaction processed improves the Land Credit Score model and market intelligence for all users. This is a compounding advantage that grows with adoption and cannot be replicated by a competitor starting from zero.
