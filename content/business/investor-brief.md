# AcreOS — The Operating System for Real Estate Investors

## One-Page Investor Brief

---

### The Market

The US real estate investor technology market exceeds $12 billion annually. Over 500,000 active land and real estate investors in the US rely on fragmented tools — separate CRMs, campaign platforms, spreadsheets, and manual processes. Existing solutions (Pebble, REsimpli, PropStream, InvestorFuse) each serve a single segment of the workflow with no AI intelligence, no cross-platform data sharing, and no compliance automation. No product owns the full investor lifecycle.

### The Product

AcreOS is an AI-powered platform covering the complete investor workflow: deal discovery → property analysis → offer generation → negotiation → closing → seller-financed note servicing → portfolio management. It supports 7 investor types (land flipper, buy-and-hold, wholesaler, developer, commercial, fix-and-flip, seller finance) from a single codebase.

Key capabilities:
- **Deal Feed:** AI-scored daily opportunities across target counties
- **Due Diligence:** One-click reports from 18 free government data sources
- **Land Credit Score:** Proprietary 300-850 parcel rating (6 dimensions)
- **Offer Wizard:** AI-generated letters matching user communication style
- **Note Management:** Full lifecycle with borrower portal and compliance
- **Freedom Meter:** Passive income tracking against financial independence goals

### The Moat

**Data network effect:** Every transaction processed improves market intelligence for all users. Anonymized deal data (county, acreage range, price range) feeds county-level pricing models. More users = better predictions = more users.

**Land Credit Score calibration:** LCS accuracy improves with every closed deal outcome. The scoring model adjusts weights based on which dimensions best predicted profitable transactions. This is a compounding advantage that cannot be replicated without transaction volume.

**Voice learning:** AI output personalized to each user's communication style creates switching costs. The system learns from months of correspondence — that history doesn't transfer to a competitor.

### Competitive Advantage

- Only platform with a parcel-level investment quality score (LCS 300-850)
- Only platform integrating 18 free government data sources into automated DD
- Only platform with end-to-end seller-finance note management + Dodd-Frank compliance
