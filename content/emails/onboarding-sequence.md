**Body:**

Want to work with your existing data too? Two ways to get leads into AcreOS:

**CSV Import** — Go to Leads → Import. Upload any CSV with at minimum: first name, last name, and one of [email, phone, address]. AcreOS maps the columns automatically.

## Day 4 — First Campaign

**Subject:** Your leads are waiting

**Body:**

You have leads in AcreOS now. Time to reach out.

Go to Campaigns → New Campaign. Here's what happens:

1. **Select your audience** — filter by county, status, score, or any custom criteria.
2. **Write your message** — or click "Generate Copy" and the AI will draft a message that matches your communication style.
3. **Choose your channel** — email, SMS, or direct mail.
4. **Send** — the campaign goes out, and every response comes back into AcreOS attached to the right lead.

No more toggling to Mailchimp. No more responses lost in Gmail.

The AI-generated copy isn't generic — it learns from your previous messages and matches your tone. Most users can't tell the difference from what they'd write themselves.

Quick link: https://app.acreos.com/campaigns

— Thomas

## Day 10 — Features You Haven't Tried

**Subject:** You're using 40% of AcreOS — here's what you're missing

**Body:**

Based on your activity so far, here are features you haven't explored yet:

**Due Diligence Reports** — Click any property → "Run DD Report." You get flood zones, soil data, environmental checks, elevation, road access, and 13 more data points in one click. Free on your Pro trial.

**Land Credit Score** — Every property gets a 300-850 score based on 6 dimensions. It's like a FICO score for land parcels. Look for the LCS badge on any property page.

**Negotiation Copilot** — When you're in a deal and the seller counters, the copilot suggests your next move based on the seller's motivation profile and comparable sales.

**Dodd-Frank Checker** — If you seller-finance, go to Finance → any note → Compliance. It checks your note terms against state regulations in real time.

**Freedom Meter** — Dashboard → Freedom Meter. It tracks what percentage of your monthly expenses are covered by your passive income. It updates automatically from your note payments.

Each of these is one click away. Let me know if you want a walkthrough of any of them.

— Thomas
