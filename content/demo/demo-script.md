# AcreOS Demo Script (3–5 Minutes)

## 1. Opening (15 sec)

**[Screen: AcreOS login page]**

> "Let me show you AcreOS — I built this to replace the 5 tools I was using for land investing. One platform: find deals, analyze them, make offers, manage notes, track everything."

---

## 2. Onboarding (30 sec)

**[Screen: Onboarding wizard]**

> "When you sign up, you tell AcreOS what kind of investor you are. I'm a land flipper, so I'll pick that."

**[Click 'Land Flipper' → set 3 target counties → see sample data populate]**

> "It sets up your workspace with sample data so you can explore before importing your own. Three target counties selected — the Deal Feed starts scanning immediately."

---

## 3. Deal Feed (45 sec)

**[Screen: Deal Feed page with opportunity cards]**

> "Every morning, the AI scans my target counties and finds opportunities ranked by quality. Each card shows a composite score based on four factors: acquisition radar, seller motivation signals, county market strength, and the Land Credit Score."

**[Hover over a high-scoring card → click into it]**

> "This 10-acre parcel in Hudspeth County scored 84 out of 100. High motivation — the owner is out of state and behind on taxes. County market is trending up. Let me click in."

---

## 4. Property Intelligence (45 sec)

**[Screen: Property detail page with enrichment data]**

> "When I click in, I see data from 18 free government sources — flood zones from FEMA, soil type from USDA, elevation from USGS, road access from OpenStreetMap, wetlands, endangered species, fire risk. All pulled automatically."

**[Point to Land Credit Score badge]**

> "And here's the Land Credit Score — a 300 to 850 rating of this parcel's investment quality, like a FICO score for land. This one's a 720: good access, no flood risk, strong soil, near infrastructure. Six dimensions, all transparent — you can see exactly why it scored the way it did."

---

## 5. Make an Offer (45 sec)

**[Screen: Offer wizard]**

> "I can send an offer right from here. The system suggests three tiers based on market comps — aggressive, market, and conservative."

**[Select middle tier → show generated offer letter]**

> "It generates a professional offer letter that matches my writing style — I trained it on my previous correspondence so it doesn't sound like a robot. I can edit it, then send it as email or queue it for direct mail."

**[Click 'Send Offer']**

> "Done. The lead status updates automatically, the activity is logged, and if the seller replies, the response shows up right here in AcreOS — not buried in my Gmail."

---

## 6. Note Management (30 sec)

**[Screen: Finance/Notes page → click into a note]**

> "If I seller-finance the deal, AcreOS manages the entire note lifecycle. Here's an active note — amortization schedule, payment history, borrower portal where the buyer can see their balance and make payments."

**[Point to Dodd-Frank badge]**

> "And this green badge means the note is Dodd-Frank compliant. AcreOS checks the interest rate against state usury limits, verifies balloon restrictions, and monitors the ability-to-repay rules. If anything's out of compliance, it flags it before you finalize the note."

---

## 7. Freedom Meter (15 sec)

**[Screen: Dashboard with Freedom Meter progress ring]**

> "And this is why I do it. The Freedom Meter tracks my progress toward financial freedom. My note income currently covers 34% of my monthly expenses. Every deal that closes, every note payment that comes in, it updates automatically. That number going up is the whole point."

---

## 8. Close (15 sec)

**[Screen: Dashboard overview]**

> "AcreOS is in beta right now. Free to try for 90 days with full Pro access. If you're a land investor and you're tired of toggling between 5 different tools, DM me and I'll get you set up."
