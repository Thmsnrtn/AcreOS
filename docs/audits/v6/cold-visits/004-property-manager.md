# Cold Visitor Simulation 004: Property Manager

**Visitor profile:** Manages 50 rental units (residential, suburban). Uses AppFolio for tenant management and accounting. Looking for better portfolio analytics and maybe a CRM for tenant leads. Not a land investor. Not a wholesaler.

**Date:** 2026-04-18
**Pages visited:** Landing, Pricing, Auth

---

## Question 1: CATEGORY (60 seconds on landing page)

**Score: 2/5**

### What this visitor sees

Headline: **"The operating system for real estate professionals."** Subhead about replacing spreadsheets, managing leads, automating outreach, closing deals. Strategy badges: **Wholesaling, Fix & Flip, Buy & Hold, STR / Airbnb, Land, Multifamily, Commercial, Creative Finance, Notes.**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Operating system for real estate professionals.' Okay, that's me. I'm a real estate professional. Let me see..."

> "'Buy & Hold' -- that's sort of what my clients do. 'STR / Airbnb' -- I manage a few of those. 'Multifamily' -- I've got a couple small multifamily buildings. So maybe this is relevant?"

> "But the subhead says 'manages leads, automates outreach, and closes deals.' That's acquisitions language. I don't acquire properties -- I manage them. I deal with tenants, maintenance requests, lease renewals, and rent collection. 'Closing deals' is not my world."

> "Features: Portfolio Mapping -- could be useful for seeing my units on a map. AI Valuations -- I don't need to value my properties every day, I need to manage them. AI Deal Intelligence -- deals? I don't do deals. Campaign Automation -- outreach to whom? Tenants? Prospects?"

> "This is not property management software. This is investment software. There's a difference."

### Assessment

The strategy badges create a false hope -- "Buy & Hold" and "Multifamily" are categories this visitor recognizes. But the feature set immediately reveals the mismatch: this is about acquiring and financing properties, not managing tenants and maintenance. The visitor correctly identifies the gap within 60 seconds.

---

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 1/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Let me read the features more carefully to be sure..."

> "'Portfolio Mapping -- Visualize every parcel on interactive maps with property boundaries, flood zones, and zoning data.' Okay, I already know where my properties are. I don't need flood zone data on a daily basis. I need to know which units have open maintenance tickets."

> "'AI Valuations -- Instant property valuations powered by comps analysis and 18 open data sources.' I don't need instant valuations. I need to know if tenant #7B paid rent this month."

> "'Document Generation -- Auto-generate purchase agreements, contracts, and closing documents in seconds.' Purchase agreements? I need lease agreements, move-in checklists, and maintenance authorization forms."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks.' Do-not-call lists? I'm calling my own tenants. About their own maintenance request. I don't need DNC compliance for that."

> "There is literally nothing here about tenants. Nothing about rent collection. Nothing about maintenance. Nothing about leases. Nothing about inspections. This is acquisitions software, not management software."

> "I'm going back to AppFolio."

### Assessment

Complete misalignment. Property management requires tenant management, lease tracking, maintenance ticketing, rent collection, and accounting. AcreOS offers none of these. Every feature is oriented toward the deal lifecycle (find, evaluate, acquire, finance, sell) rather than the asset management lifecycle (lease, maintain, collect, renew). The visitor is out.

## Question 3: PRICING (2 minutes)

**Score: 2/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "I'll look at pricing just to understand the product model before I leave."

> "The limits are: Leads, Properties, Notes, AI Requests, Campaigns, Sequences. These are all acquisition metrics. There's no 'Units managed' or 'Tenants' or 'Lease agreements' as a limit category."

> "Properties: 3 on free, 50 on Starter, 100 on Pro, Unlimited on Scale. If 'properties' means my 50 rental units, I'd need at least Starter. But I still don't understand what I'd do with them in this tool."

> "'Stripe Connect Payments' -- for collecting rent? That would actually be interesting. But it says 'Payments' not 'Rent collection.' And it's only on Starter and above."

> "The pricing is clear but irrelevant to me. It's like reading the menu of a restaurant that only serves food I'm allergic to."

### Assessment

The pricing structure is technically clear but reveals the product's inapplicability to property management. The metering units (leads, campaigns, sequences, notes as financial instruments) confirm this is not a property management tool. Stripe Connect Payments is the one potentially relevant feature but is not positioned as rent collection.
