# R2-003: REsimpli Switcher (Wholesaler wanting land tools)

**Persona**: Active wholesaler, 2 years. Currently pays $299/mo for REsimpli. Does 3-5 deals/month in houses. Wants to add a land flipping vertical to diversify. Needs land-specific tools that REsimpli doesn't have.
**Device**: MacBook Pro, Chrome
**Referral**: Google search "land investing CRM software"

---

## 1. CATEGORY (60 seconds)

**Score: 5 / 5**

> "'The AI-Powered Platform for Land Investors.' Good. That's what I'm looking for. I need land-specific tools, not another generic RE CRM."

> "'Find motivated sellers. Analyze parcels. Send direct mail. Close deals.' That's the land flip workflow. REsimpli does sellers and mail for houses, but I need parcel analysis."

The category positioning is exactly right. This visitor is sophisticated enough to know that land investing has different tooling needs than house wholesaling, and they're looking for something purpose-built.

> "Adjacent Verticals section -- 'Wholesaling: Deal flow and motivated seller outreach -- Coming soon.' Huh. So they know wholesaling exists but aren't supporting it yet. Interesting. That tells me the land tools are probably deep."

They read the "Coming soon" for Wholesaling as a signal that AcreOS is focused on land. This is actually a positive for their use case -- they WANT a tool that goes deep on land, not one that tries to do everything poorly.

**Comparable**: REsimpli for houses, PropStream for data. They want AcreOS to be "REsimpli but for land."

**Verdict**: Perfect category match. The land-specific positioning is exactly what they were searching for.

---

## 2. VALUE PROP (60 seconds)

**Score: 4 / 5**

> "'AI Valuations' -- REsimpli doesn't do land comps. That's the whole reason I need a separate tool. If AcreOS can comp raw land, that's the killer feature."

> "'Campaign Automation -- SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' Okay so it's not just analysis -- it has outreach too. That means I might be able to run my entire land operation from here without touching REsimpli."

> "'18 free data sources' -- REsimpli charges me for skip tracing and data pulls on top of the $299. If AcreOS includes data, that's a significant cost saving."

Strong recognition of each feature's value because they have a direct comparison point. Every feature listed solves a problem they currently solve with a different (more expensive) tool.

Credibility concern: "500+ Properties managed" is weak social proof for someone managing 50+ active deals.

> "No mention of driving for dollars, no mention of seller financing for the outbound side. But I see 'Documents' and 'Blind Offer Wizard' -- those are very land-specific. This wasn't built for houses."

> "No integrations mentioned on the landing page. I use Zapier, CallRail, and a VA team. Can AcreOS connect to my existing stack?"

**Verdict**: Strong value match. Missing information about integrations and team workflow creates some uncertainty.

---

## 3. PRICING (2 minutes)

**Score: 5 / 5**

> "Free: $0. Starter: $20. Pro: $49. Scale: $79. ... I'm paying $299 for REsimpli. Even the most expensive AcreOS plan is $79."

> "Let me look at what Pro gives me: 500 leads, 100 properties, unlimited campaigns, BYOK data, SMS/voice, 2 seats for $49/mo. That's... that's absurd. REsimpli charges $299 for roughly the same thing without land comps."

This is the moment the visitor starts seriously considering switching their entire workflow.

> "Scale at $79 gives unlimited everything plus 10 seats. My VA could have their own login. At REsimpli that's an add-on."

> "14-day free trial. Annual saves 20%. I could run Pro for a year at $470 total -- that's less than two months of REsimpli."

> "'Team Seats: Pro 2 (add more at $20/seat), Scale 10 (add more at $40/seat).' Clear. No hidden costs."

The pricing page is doing the selling at this point. The feature comparison table is exactly what a CRM switcher needs -- a line-by-line comparison to mentally map against their current tool.

One question unanswered: "What are 'AI Requests / day'? At 1,000 on Pro, will I hit that limit? What counts as a request?"

**Verdict**: Pricing is a massive competitive advantage against REsimpli. This visitor is price-shocked (in a good way).

---

## 4. SIGNUP READINESS (5 minutes total)

**Score: 4 / 5**

> "I'm signing up. $0 risk. I just want to see the CRM before I commit to importing my data."

They click "Get Started Free" and hit the auth page.

> "'The operating system for real estate professionals.' That works for me. I'm a RE professional."

The auth page tagline doesn't create friction for this persona -- they self-identify as a professional.

> "Google SSO. Done in 10 seconds."

Hesitations:
1. "Can I migrate my data from REsimpli? Is there a CSV import?"
2. "Will the land comps actually work or is it vaporware?"
3. "If I start on Free (10 leads), that's useless for real testing. I need to import at least 50 leads to evaluate. Do I need to start a trial immediately?"

> "I wish there was a migration guide. 'Switching from REsimpli?' with a step-by-step. That would close me instantly."

> "No FAQ on the pricing page. No 'How does the free trial work?' No 'What happens when I exceed limits?' Those are basic questions I need answered."

**Verdict**: Signing up without hesitation due to free tier. But they need more information about data migration and limit behavior to commit to paid.

---

## 5. FIRST-RUN MENTAL MODEL (5 min post-signup)

**Score: 4 / 5**

Onboarding wizard: they pick "active" path.

> "'Import Your Existing Portfolio' -- CSV upload. This is the first thing I needed. I can export my REsimpli leads as CSV and bring them here."

> "'Set Your Target Counties' -- good, this is land-specific. REsimpli is address-based. Land deals are county-based. They understand the difference."

> "Instant Deal Hunt showing motivation scores for real owners in my target county -- REsimpli doesn't do this. This is legitimately different."

They land on Today page. The sidebar navigation:

> "CRM section: Leads, Skip Tracing, Properties, Portfolio Map, Deal Pipeline, Marketplace, Listings, Documents, Blind Offer Wizard. Okay, this is a full CRM. Comparable to REsimpli's lead management."

> "Campaigns: Campaigns, Direct Mail, Sequences. Same as REsimpli but with direct mail built in -- REsimpli uses third-party integrations for that."

> "Intelligence section: Insights, AI Valuations, Land Credit, Markets, Counties, Acq. Radar -- none of this exists in REsimpli. This is the differentiation."

> "Finance: Seller-financed notes, Cash Flow, Portfolio, Capital Markets. Wait -- seller financing management? That's a land investing need that REsimpli completely ignores."

> "The landing page promised 'all in one platform' and the nav confirms it. CRM + Outreach + Intelligence + Finance. That's four product categories in one."

Concerns:
- "Will the CRM part be as polished as REsimpli, which has been around for years?"
- "The nav has a LOT of items. Some of these might be half-built."
- "I see 'Marketplace' and 'Listings' -- are those functional or placeholders?"

**Verdict**: The first-run experience validates the landing page promise. The feature set is broader than expected. The active investor onboarding path (import -> counties -> deal hunt) is well-sequenced. Main risk is whether feature depth matches feature breadth.

---

## TOTAL SCORE: 22 / 25

**Summary**: AcreOS is extremely well-positioned to capture REsimpli switchers who want land tools. The pricing alone is a compelling switching argument ($49 vs $299). The land-specific features (comps, county targeting, motivation scoring, blind offer wizard, seller financing) are things generic RE CRMs don't offer. Friction points: (1) no migration guide or competitor comparison page, (2) no FAQ about limits and trial mechanics, (3) social proof is thin for someone coming from an established platform. This visitor will run both tools in parallel for a month before deciding.
