# R2-004: RE Agent -- Should Filter Out

**Persona**: Licensed real estate agent, 5 years. Works residential resales in suburban markets. NOT a land investor. Looking for a CRM to manage listings and client relationships.
**Device**: iPad, Safari
**Referral**: Google search "real estate CRM with AI"

---

## 1. CATEGORY (60 seconds)

**Score: 5 / 5 (for filtering)**

> "'The AI-Powered Platform for Land Investors.' -- Oh. This is for land people. Not me."

The headline does its filtering job in under 3 seconds. "Land Investors" is specific enough that a residential agent immediately knows this is not their tool.

> "'Find motivated sellers. Analyze parcels. Send direct mail.' -- Yeah, I don't send direct mail to find sellers. My sellers come from referrals and Zillow leads. 'Parcels' is land language."

> "How It Works: 'Import or Find Parcels,' 'AI Analyzes Each Parcel,' 'Launch Direct Mail Campaigns,' 'Close Deals & Track Returns.' Every step is about land acquisition. I list homes. This isn't for me."

They scroll to the Adjacent Verticals section:

> "'Expanding to Serve More Investors' -- investors, not agents. 'Wholesaling,' 'Fix & Flip,' 'Buy & Hold,' 'STR / Airbnb'... no 'Residential Agent' or 'Listing Agent' option. I'm not an investor."

> "I do see it says 'Coming soon' for these other verticals. Maybe one day. But right now, no."

**Comparable**: They were looking for something like Follow Up Boss, KV Core, or LionDesk. AcreOS is clearly not in that category.

**Verdict**: Headline filtering works perfectly. This visitor self-selects out within 10 seconds. The landing page does NOT waste an agent's time.

---

## 2. VALUE PROP (60 seconds)

**Score: 5 / 5 (for filtering)**

> "'AI Valuations powered by comps analysis' -- I use the MLS for comps. I don't need an AI to pull public data for residential properties."

> "'Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers' -- my leads are buyers and sellers calling me from Zillow. I don't need lead scoring for inbound calls."

> "'Campaign Automation -- SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' -- I use Mailchimp for my newsletter. Direct mail is a land investor thing."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- phone compliance, do-not-call list checks.' I don't cold call. My broker's compliance department handles this."

Every feature is correctly positioned for land investing and correctly NOT positioned for residential brokerage. There's nothing misleading that would pull an agent into the funnel.

**Verdict**: Clean filter. The value prop is specific enough that the wrong audience disengages immediately.

## TOTAL SCORE: 10 / 10 on applicable questions (filtering success)

**Summary**: The v6 headline "The AI-Powered Platform for Land Investors" works exactly as intended for filtering out non-land professionals. A residential agent recognizes within 3 seconds that this is not their tool. The landing page copy, feature descriptions, How It Works steps, and Adjacent Verticals section all reinforce the land-specific positioning. The only weakness is the auth page tagline ("The operating system for real estate professionals") which is broader than the landing page, but this is only visible AFTER clicking through, and by that point the landing page has already filtered. The filter works.

**Caveat**: If this agent ALSO does land deals on the side (increasingly common), the Adjacent Verticals "Wholesaling -- Coming soon" might disappoint them. But for a pure agent, the filter holds.
