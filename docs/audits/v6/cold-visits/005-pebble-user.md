# Cold Visitor Simulation 005: Frustrated Pebble User

**Visitor profile:** Current Pebble user (land investing CRM). Frustrated with Pebble's limitations -- slow UI, limited automation, clunky document generation, no AI features. Actively searching for alternatives. Does 15-20 deals per year, team of 3 (self + 2 VAs).

**Date:** 2026-04-18
**Pages visited:** Landing, Pricing, Auth

---

## Question 1: CATEGORY (60 seconds on landing page)

**Score: 4/5**

### What this visitor sees

Headline: **"The operating system for real estate professionals."** Strategy badges including **Land, Creative Finance, Notes.** Features: Portfolio Mapping, AI Valuations, AI Deal Intelligence, Document Generation, Campaign Automation, Compliance.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Operating system for real estate professionals.' Okay, that's a step up from Pebble's positioning. Let me see if this actually covers what I need."

> "Land, Creative Finance, Notes -- those are my categories. Check."

> "'Replaces your spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and guesswork with a single platform.' That's exactly the promise Pebble made when I signed up three years ago. But Pebble became one of the disconnected tools I'm trying to replace."

> "Portfolio Mapping with property boundaries -- Pebble has a map but it's slow and doesn't show boundaries. AI Valuations -- Pebble doesn't have this at all, I use LandGlide separately. AI Deal Intelligence with 'Sophie' -- Pebble has zero AI anything. Document Generation -- Pebble's doc gen is terrible. Campaign Automation -- Pebble's campaign feature is basic."

> "This checks every box Pebble doesn't. The question is whether it actually works."

### Assessment

This is the visitor most likely to convert. They have the exact pain points AcreOS addresses, they have a frame of reference (Pebble) to compare against, and they are actively looking to switch. The landing page speaks directly to their frustrations. The only weakness: the headline says "real estate professionals" when this visitor would respond more strongly to specific land investing language.

---

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 4/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities.' I've been wanting something like this. In Pebble I manually score every lead based on my own criteria. If this AI can learn my criteria and auto-score, that alone is worth switching."

> "'18 Free data sources' -- Pebble charges for most data lookups. If AcreOS includes 18 sources for free, that saves me money."

> "'Campaign Automation with SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' Pebble's campaigns are email-only. I use a separate service for SMS and I hand-address direct mail. Multi-channel in one platform would be huge."

> "'Compliance Built-In' -- Pebble doesn't handle DNC at all. I've been worried about this since the TCPA changes."

> "But... '500+ Properties managed' is concerning. That's less than what I've managed personally in Pebble over three years. Is AcreOS actually production-ready?"

> "'Now in Public Beta' -- that's a red flag. I'm running a real business. I can't afford beta-quality software when I have VAs depending on me, deals in progress, and notes being serviced."

> "Also, there's no mention of data migration. Can I import from Pebble? If I have to manually re-enter 1,500 leads and 200 properties, that's a dealbreaker."

### Assessment

The value proposition is very strong for this visitor -- every feature addresses a specific Pebble limitation. The concerns are operational: beta stability, data migration, and team readiness. The landing page does not address any of these switching concerns, which is a missed opportunity given that tool-switchers are probably the highest-intent audience.

## Question 3: PRICING (2 minutes)

**Score: 4/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Let me compare this to what I'm paying for Pebble."

> "Free: 10 leads, 3 properties. Not enough to evaluate seriously -- I need to import at least 100 leads to see if the system works."

> "Starter: $20/mo, 250 leads, 1 seat. But I have a team of 3. So Starter is out."

> "Pro: $49/mo, 500 leads, 2 seats with additional seats at $20 each. So for my team of 3, that's $49 + $20 = $69/mo. I'm paying $79/mo for Pebble with fewer features. This is cheaper AND better on paper."

> "Scale: $79/mo, 10 seats. Same as what I pay for Pebble but with 10 seats. But I only need 3 seats, so Pro + 1 seat at $69/mo is the better deal."

> "Annual pricing: Pro at $39/mo + $20/seat = $59/mo. Pebble is $79/mo. I'd save $240/year and get AI, better automation, and compliance. On paper, this is a no-brainer."

> "Feature comparison: SMS/Voice Outreach is Pro only -- good, I need that. BYOK Data Providers on Pro -- interesting, I could plug in my DataTree key. Stripe Connect Payments -- I use a separate payment processor for my notes, could this replace it?"

> "One thing missing from pricing: what about overage charges? If I hit 500 leads on Pro, what happens? Do I get blocked? Throttled? Charged per-lead? The pricing page doesn't say."

### Assessment

The pricing comparison against Pebble is very favorable for AcreOS. This visitor can do the math and see real savings. The feature comparison table helps them validate that Pro covers their needs. The main gaps are: no mention of what happens at limits, and no data migration support mentioned.

## Question 4: SIGNUP READINESS (5 minutes total)

**Score: 3/5**

### Would you sign up?

**Yes, but with significant anxiety. Would sign up for free to evaluate, not ready to commit or migrate.**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "The features are right. The pricing is right. The switching economics work. But..."

> "One: 'Public Beta.' I have active deals. I have notes being serviced monthly. I have VAs who need a reliable system. I can't move to a beta product for my primary business operations."

> "Two: Data migration. There is zero mention of importing data from another system. I have 1,500 leads, 200 properties, 40 active notes, and 3 years of activity history in Pebble. Can I bring that with me? CSV import? API? Pebble export?"

> "Three: No case studies or testimonials. Nobody has said 'I switched from Pebble to AcreOS and here's what happened.' I'd feel a lot better if I saw one success story."

> "Four: Team management. I see 'Team Seats' in pricing but the landing page says nothing about roles, permissions, or VA management. In Pebble I can restrict what my VAs see and do. Can I do that here?"

> "Five: The auth page uses Clerk. That's fine. But I notice there's no 'Book a demo' or 'Talk to sales' option anywhere. For a $50-80/mo commitment involving a full platform migration, I'd want to talk to someone first."

> "I'll sign up for the free tier to look around. But I won't move any real data until I've confirmed: (a) data import works, (b) the system is stable, (c) my VAs can use it, and (d) there's someone I can call if things break."

### Unanswered questions

- Can I import from Pebble (or any other system)?
- What happens when I hit tier limits?
- What are the team roles and permissions?
- Is there phone/chat support, or just email?
- How stable is the platform? Any uptime SLA?
- Is there a mobile app?
- Can I run Pebble and AcreOS in parallel during migration?
- Does Stripe Connect work for servicing seller-financed notes?

### Hesitations

- "Public Beta" is the biggest blocker -- cannot risk primary business on beta software
- No data migration path visible
- No demo or sales conversation available
- No testimonials from switchers
- No documentation or help center

## Question 5: FIRST-RUN MENTAL MODEL (post-signup)

**Score: 3/5**

### What the visitor sees

After signing up via Clerk, lands on `/today`. Sidebar: **Today, Pipeline, Money, AI Hub, Settings.**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Okay, similar structure to Pebble but cleaner. Today is like a dashboard. Pipeline is the CRM. Money is for notes. AI Hub is new -- Pebble doesn't have this."

> "Wait, where are Campaigns? The landing page made a big deal about Campaign Automation. It's not in the sidebar. Let me look... Settings? No. AI Hub? No."

> "Oh, I bet I can customize the sidebar. Let me look for a settings or customize option... okay, found it. I can add Campaigns, Sequences, Documents, etc. But why aren't Campaigns in the default view? That's one of the top-3 reasons I'd switch from Pebble."

> "Let me explore Pipeline. There are Leads, Deals, and Properties. Looks clean. Kanban-style deal board. Similar to Pebble's pipeline but with more stages."

> "Money has Notes, Portfolio, Cash Flow. 'Notes' here means seller-financed notes. Good. But I don't see where I'd set up recurring payment tracking for existing notes. In Pebble I can see upcoming payments. Where is that here?"

> "AI Hub -- let me try Sophie. 'Hi Sophie, I have a 5-acre parcel in Cochise County, AZ. What's it worth?' Let me see if the valuation works..."

> "This has potential. But the default sidebar hides too much. And there's no onboarding that says 'Welcome! You look like a land investor. Here are the 8 nav items most land investors use.' They're making me discover everything manually."

> "Also, where's the import button? I don't see how to get my Pebble data in here. That's a hard stop for me."

### Assessment

The first-run experience is clean but passive. There is no guided onboarding, no import wizard, no "What did you use before?" question, and no recommended sidebar configuration based on use case. The Pebble switcher -- AcreOS's highest-value prospect -- is left to wander and discover on their own. Features that motivated the switch (Campaigns, Document Generation, Compliance) are hidden behind sidebar customization.

## Summary

| Question | Score | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 1. Category | 4/5 | Immediately recognized as Pebble alternative; "Land" badge is the anchor |
| 2. Value Prop | 4/5 | Every feature addresses a Pebble pain point; no switching support shown |
| 3. Pricing | 4/5 | Favorable vs Pebble; overage handling unclear |
| 4. Signup Readiness | 3/5 | Would try free tier; "Public Beta" blocks real migration |
| 5. First-Run | 3/5 | Clean but passive; no onboarding, no import, key features hidden |

**Overall: 3.6/5** -- This is AcreOS's highest-value prospect: a frustrated Pebble user ready to switch. The product feature set and pricing both win. What prevents conversion from trial to migration are entirely addressable gaps: (1) remove or de-emphasize "Public Beta" if the product is stable, (2) add a data import wizard with Pebble/CSV support, (3) add a "switching from another tool?" onboarding path, (4) show the right sidebar items by default for land investors, and (5) provide a way to talk to a human (demo, chat, or call) for migration decisions.
