# Cold Visitor Simulation 008: Commercial Real Estate Broker

**Visitor profile:** Licensed commercial real estate broker in a mid-size metro. Handles office, retail, and industrial leasing and sales. Not a land investor -- has never bought land for personal investment. Evaluating RE tech broadly because their brokerage is considering a platform upgrade from a legacy system. Comfortable with tech, uses CoStar, LoopNet, and Salesforce CRM daily.

**Date:** 2026-04-18
**Pages visited:** Landing, Pricing
**Source URL:** https://acreos.fly.dev/ and https://acreos.fly.dev/pricing

---

## Question 1: CATEGORY (60 seconds on landing page)

**Score: 2/5**

### What this visitor sees

Headline: **"The operating system for real estate professionals."** Strategy badges including **Commercial** alongside Wholesaling, Fix & Flip, Land, Notes, etc. Features: Portfolio Mapping, AI Valuations, AI Deal Intelligence, Document Generation, Campaign Automation, Compliance.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Operating system for real estate professionals' -- okay, that's a big claim. Let me see if this actually applies to commercial brokerage."

> "I see 'Commercial' in the strategy badges. Good, they at least acknowledge commercial exists. But it's sandwiched between 'Land' and 'Creative Finance' and 'Notes' -- those are investor strategies. I'm a broker. I don't invest in properties, I represent clients who lease or buy them."

> "'Replaces your spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and guesswork with a single platform that manages leads, automates outreach, and closes deals.' We already have Salesforce for leads and outreach, and CoStar for property data. This would need to replace both."

> "'Portfolio Mapping -- Visualize every parcel on interactive maps with property boundaries, flood zones, and zoning data.' Zoning data is useful for commercial. Flood zones less so for office and retail, but relevant for industrial. Property boundaries... I need building footprints and square footage, not parcel boundaries."

> "'AI Valuations -- Instant property valuations powered by comps analysis and 18 open data sources.' Comps analysis for what? Vacant land? Residential? I need cap rate analysis, NOI calculations, rent comps per square foot, tenant credit analysis. Are any of those 18 sources commercial data providers like CoStar or REIS?"

> "I don't see anything about lease management, tenant tracking, lease abstraction, or commercial deal room functionality. This looks like a residential/land tool that added a 'Commercial' badge."

### Assessment

The "Commercial" badge gets this visitor's attention for exactly 5 seconds before the surrounding context reveals that the product is not built for commercial brokerage. None of the six feature cards address commercial-specific needs: no lease management, no tenant tracking, no cap rate analysis, no rent roll, no CoStar/LoopNet integration. The strategy badges overstate the product's breadth. Including "Commercial" when the features are entirely land/residential creates a credibility problem -- this visitor knows what commercial RE tech looks like and this is not it.

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 1/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities.' In commercial, 'leads' are tenants looking for space or owners looking to sell/lease. 'Drafts offers' -- does it draft LOIs? Commercial purchase agreements? Lease proposals? There's no specificity."

> "'Document Generation -- Auto-generate purchase agreements, contracts, and closing documents in seconds.' What kind of purchase agreements? Commercial PSAs have entirely different clauses than residential -- due diligence periods, environmental contingencies, tenant estoppels, SNDA agreements. I doubt this covers any of that."

> "'Campaign Automation -- Multi-channel outreach with SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' We do email campaigns for available properties, but we use Buildout for that. It integrates with CoStar and creates professional commercial property flyers. Does AcreOS create commercial-grade marketing materials? The landing page doesn't show any examples."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks.' We don't cold call. Our leads come from CoStar inquiries, referrals, and our brokerage network. DNC compliance is a non-issue for us."

> "'18 Free data sources' -- which sources? In commercial, the only data that matters is CoStar, REIS, Real Capital Analytics, and local MLS for smaller markets. If the 18 sources are county assessor records and Census data, that's not useful for valuing a Class A office building."

> "The social proof says '500+ Properties managed.' My brokerage has 200+ active listings alone. 500 managed properties total, across all AcreOS users, is not confidence-inspiring for a commercial operation."

### Assessment

The value proposition has zero alignment with commercial brokerage. Every feature description maps to land investing or residential wholesaling workflows. There is no mention of leases, tenants, cap rates, NOI, rent rolls, CoStar, LoopNet, or any commercial-specific concept. The visitor spends their 60 seconds confirming what they suspected at the category stage: this product does not serve their market.

> "Scale at $79/mo for 10 seats vs our current stack at $800/mo per broker would be incredible savings. But it's like comparing a bicycle to a car because the bicycle is cheaper -- it doesn't do what I need."

### Assessment

The pricing is irrelevant because the product is irrelevant to this visitor. The feature comparison confirms the mismatch: every row in the table is oriented toward land/residential investing. None of the 16 features listed map to commercial brokerage workflows. The visitor recognizes the price advantage but correctly identifies that cost savings on a non-functional tool are meaningless.
