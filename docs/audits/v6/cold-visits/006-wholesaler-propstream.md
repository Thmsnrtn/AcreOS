# Cold Visitor Simulation 006: Wholesaler (PropStream + Podio)

**Visitor profile:** Wholesaler with 3 years experience. Currently uses PropStream for data and Podio for CRM. Looking to consolidate into one platform. Does 1-2 wholesale deals per month, primarily vacant land and distressed residential. Comfortable with tech, has used Zapier integrations between PropStream and Podio.

**Date:** 2026-04-18
**Pages visited:** Landing, Pricing
**Source URL:** https://acreos.fly.dev/ and https://acreos.fly.dev/pricing

---

## Question 1: CATEGORY (60 seconds on landing page)

**Score: 4/5**

### What this visitor sees

Headline: **"The operating system for real estate professionals."** Strategy badges: **Wholesaling, Fix & Flip, Buy & Hold, STR / Airbnb, Land, Multifamily, Commercial, Creative Finance, Notes.** The "Now in Public Beta" badge sits above the headline.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Operating system for real estate professionals -- broad but okay. Let me look at the badges... Wholesaling is literally the first one. That's me."

> "Fix & Flip, Land, Creative Finance -- these are all strategies I use or have used. This isn't a generic CRM trying to be everything. They listed wholesaling first."

> "'Replaces your spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and guesswork with a single platform that manages leads, automates outreach, and closes deals -- powered by AI.' That's exactly what I'm trying to do. I'm paying $99/mo for PropStream and $50/mo for Podio plus another $20 for Zapier to glue them together. If one platform does all of it, I'm interested."

> "Portfolio Mapping with property boundaries and flood zones -- PropStream has this, but having it in the same system as my CRM would save me from bouncing between tabs."

> "Campaign Automation with SMS, email, and direct mail -- right now I export skip-traced numbers from PropStream into a dialer and run separate direct mail through Yellow Letters. Having it all in one tool would be massive."

### Assessment

This is a strong category match. The "Wholesaling" badge is the first thing this visitor latches onto, and the consolidation value proposition directly addresses their pain of running PropStream + Podio + Zapier + separate outreach tools. The visitor self-identifies within 15 seconds. The only reason this isn't a 5/5: the headline says "real estate professionals" instead of something more targeted that would make a wholesaler feel like this was built specifically for them.

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 4/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'AI Valuations -- Instant property valuations powered by comps analysis and 18 open data sources.' PropStream has comps but I pay for them per lookup. If AcreOS includes 18 free sources, I could cancel PropStream entirely."

> "'AI Deal Intelligence -- Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities.' I currently score leads manually in Podio based on motivation level and equity. If AI can automate that, I get hours back every week."

> "'Document Generation -- Auto-generate purchase agreements, contracts, and closing documents in seconds.' I use Dotloop for contracts right now. Another tool I could eliminate."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks, and audit trails.' This is important. I've been worried about TCPA. Right now I'm manually scrubbing my lists against the DNC registry."

> "'Campaign Automation -- Multi-channel outreach with SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' This is the big one. If I can run my entire outreach -- skip trace, SMS, ringless voicemail, direct mail -- from inside the CRM, that eliminates three separate subscriptions."

> "But I don't see any mention of skip tracing. PropStream's skip tracing is the main reason I keep it. If AcreOS doesn't skip trace, I still need PropStream for data and this just becomes another Podio."

> "Also no mention of driving for dollars, list stacking, or list building. Those are core wholesaling workflows. The badge says 'Wholesaling' but the feature descriptions feel more like land investing features."

### Assessment

The value proposition is strong on consolidation and AI differentiation. This visitor can count the tools they would eliminate (PropStream, Podio, Zapier, dialer, direct mail service, Dotloop). The gap is that core wholesaling workflows -- skip tracing, list stacking, driving for dollars, comping distressed residential -- are not explicitly mentioned. The features described lean toward land investing use cases. The "18 open data sources" claim is interesting but the visitor doesn't know which 18 or whether they include the data PropStream provides.

## Question 3: PRICING (2 minutes)

**Score: 4/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Let me do the math. Right now I'm spending: PropStream $99, Podio $50, Zapier $20, dialer $45, direct mail service $30 minimum. That's $244/mo before I send a single piece of mail."

> "AcreOS Free: $0, 10 leads, 3 properties. Not enough -- I pull 500+ leads per campaign. Starter: $20/mo, 250 leads, 5 campaigns. Closer but I'd blow through 250 leads in a week."

> "Pro: $49/mo, 500 leads, unlimited campaigns, BYOK Data Providers, SMS/Voice Outreach. That's interesting. 500 leads is still tight for a wholesaler -- I might pull 2,000 leads in a month -- but the features are right."

> "Scale: $79/mo, unlimited leads, 10 seats. That's more like it for volume. And $79/mo vs my current $244/mo is $165/mo in savings. Almost $2,000 a year."

> "'BYOK Data Providers' on Pro -- so I can bring my own API keys? Like my PropStream key? If I can plug PropStream data into AcreOS and use AcreOS as the CRM and outreach layer, that's interesting even as a transition step."

> "Feature comparison: SMS/Voice Outreach is Pro and up only. Direct Mail Integration is Starter and up. Stripe Connect Payments on Starter and up -- I don't need that, I use title companies."

> "One thing I notice: there's no mention of per-message costs for SMS or direct mail. $49/mo for unlimited campaigns with SMS sounds too good. Is SMS included in the price or is it usage-based on top? The pricing page doesn't say."

> "Annual pricing: Pro at $39/mo. Even better. But what happens when I hit 500 leads? Do they cut me off mid-campaign?"

### Assessment

The pricing is extremely competitive against this visitor's current stack. The math works clearly in AcreOS's favor. The main concerns are: (1) lead limits feel low for high-volume wholesaling, (2) SMS/direct mail unit costs are unclear, and (3) the BYOK option is interesting but unexplained. The visitor can see a path to saving $2,000/year, which is a strong motivator.

## Question 4: SIGNUP READINESS (5 minutes total)

**Score: 3/5**

### Would you sign up?

**Yes, for a free trial. Would not migrate from PropStream + Podio until testing confirms feature parity on data and outreach.**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "The economics are compelling. $49-79/mo vs $244/mo is a no-brainer IF the tool actually does what PropStream and Podio do together."

> "My biggest concern is data quality. PropStream's data comes from county assessor records, MLS, pre-foreclosure filings, lien data. The landing page says '18 open data sources' but doesn't tell me which ones. Are they the same quality as PropStream? Or are they lesser sources that'll give me incomplete property data?"

> "Second concern: 'Public Beta.' I run a real wholesaling business. I have deals in contract, earnest money deposits, assignment deadlines. If this system goes down or loses my data during a live deal, I'm out thousands of dollars."

> "Third: no integrations mentioned. Can I connect to my title company? To my dispo buyers list? To CallRail for call tracking? Podio has hundreds of integrations via Zapier. Does AcreOS?"

> "Fourth: I don't see any mention of a mobile app or mobile-friendly design. I'm in the field a lot -- driving for dollars, meeting sellers, inspecting properties. I need to pull up lead info and property data on my phone."

> "No testimonials from other wholesalers. No case studies. No 'I switched from PropStream and here's what happened.' I'd want to see that before committing."

> "I'll sign up for the free tier to kick the tires. Maybe import 10 leads and see if the data quality holds up. But PropStream stays active until I'm convinced."

### Unanswered questions

- Which 18 data sources are included?
- Is skip tracing available? What's the cost per record?
- Are SMS and direct mail included in the subscription or usage-based?
- What happens when I hit lead/property limits mid-campaign?
- Is there Zapier/webhook integration?
- Is there a mobile app?
- Can I import my Podio data (leads, notes, deal history)?
- Can I export if I want to leave?

### Hesitations

- "Public Beta" label creates risk for active deal flow
- No skip tracing mentioned -- that's a dealbreaker for many wholesalers
- Lead limits (500 on Pro) feel restrictive for high-volume wholesaling
- No clarity on per-message costs for SMS/direct mail
- No social proof from wholesaling users
- No integrations page or API documentation visible

## Question 5: FIRST-RUN MENTAL MODEL (post-signup)

**Score: 3/5**

### What the visitor sees

After signup via Clerk, the sidebar shows: **Dashboard, Leads, Properties, Deals, Campaigns, Inbox, AI Hub, Intelligence, Finance, Settings.** Expandable sub-items under each group.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Okay, this looks organized. Dashboard, Leads, Properties, Deals -- that maps to my workflow. Campaigns for outreach. Good."

> "Let me click Leads. I see a lead table. Where's the import button? I want to upload a CSV of skip-traced leads from PropStream... I see 'Data Export' under Settings but I don't see 'Data Import' anywhere obvious."

> "Deals has a pipeline view -- kanban style. The stages look like: New, Contacted, Negotiating, Under Contract, Closed. That works for wholesaling. But where's 'Assigned to Buyer'? In wholesaling, after I get a property under contract, I need to find a cash buyer and assign the contract. That's a critical stage missing from the default pipeline."

> "Campaigns -- okay, I can create email, SMS, and direct mail campaigns. Let me try to set up an SMS campaign... it asks me for a phone number. Do I need to bring my own Twilio number? Or does AcreOS provide one?"

> "AI Hub -- let me try Sophie. 'Sophie, I have a list of 200 vacant land parcels in Maricopa County. Can you score them by investment potential?' This is the use case that would save me the most time."

> "Properties has a Maps view -- nice, I can see parcels on a map with boundaries. But I need to comp properties. Where's the comp tool? In PropStream I can pull comps for any address. Here I see 'AI Valuations' mentioned on the landing page but I don't see a 'Run Comps' button on the property detail page."

> "Intelligence section has AVM, Land Credit, Markets, Counties, Acq. Radar. This is a lot of analytical tools. More than PropStream in some ways. But it's all land-focused -- Land Credit, Counties (USDA + Census). Where's the distressed residential analysis? Foreclosure data? Lien data?"

> "There's no onboarding flow. No 'What kind of deals do you do?' question. No guided setup. I'm just dropped into an empty dashboard and told to figure it out. PropStream had a tutorial video. Podio had a template marketplace. Here it's a blank canvas."

### Assessment

The first-run experience has the right structural bones -- the sidebar navigation maps reasonably well to a wholesaling workflow. But the details reveal a land-investing bias: the intelligence tools are land-focused, the deal pipeline lacks wholesaling-specific stages (assignment, double-close), and there's no comping workflow equivalent to PropStream. The lack of an import wizard is a significant friction point for someone trying to migrate from PropStream + Podio. No onboarding or guided setup means the visitor is left to discover features by clicking around.
