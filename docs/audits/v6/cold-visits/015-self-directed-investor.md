Wants AI-powered tools (uses Copilot for code, ChatGPT for research). Found AcreOS via a Bigger Pockets forum thread.

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 4/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Everything you need to run your land business.' Bold claim. Let me check the feature cards."

> "'Portfolio Mapping -- Visualize every parcel on interactive maps with property boundaries, flood zones, and zoning data.' Maps are good. I want to see if they're using Mapbox or Google Maps. > "'AI Valuations -- Instant property valuations powered by comps analysis and 18 open data sources.' '18 open data sources' -- they're aggregating public data (county assessor records, USDA data, Census data, probably FEMA flood maps). The word 'open' suggests these are free government/public sources, not paid APIs. Smart -- keeps costs down and passes savings to users."

> "'AI Deal Intelligence -- Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities.' Okay, this is where I get skeptical. What model is Sophie running on? Is it GPT-4? Claude? A fine-tuned model? What's the scoring methodology? 'Scores leads' could mean anything from a simple weighted average to a real ML model trained on deal outcomes. The landing page doesn't say."

> "'Document Generation -- Auto-generate purchase agreements.' Template-based or actually intelligent? I've seen too many 'AI-powered' document tools that are just mail merge. But for land deals, even good templates would save me time since I'd be starting from zero."

> "'Campaign Automation -- Multi-channel outreach with SMS, email, and direct mail.' Standard CRM stuff but important. I'd need this because I have zero connections in the land world -- my only option is cold outreach."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks.' Important and often overlooked. Shows the team thinks about regulatory risk, not just features. Another positive technical signal."

> "'18 Free data sources' and '$0 to get started.' I like free data sources -- as an engineer I know that data provider costs can balloon. '14 day free trial' -- standard SaaS. '500+ Properties managed' -- that's a small number. I manage more positions than that in my stock portfolio. But this is clearly a young product."

> "Two gaps I notice: (1) No API mentioned. I'd want to integrate this with my own tools or scripts. (2) No mention of analytics or ROI tracking beyond 'Track Returns.' For a self-directed investor, I want detailed return analytics -- IRR, cash-on-cash, annualized returns -- like I get from my brokerage for stocks."

### Assessment

This visitor evaluates the value proposition through a technical lens. The positive signals are: data source aggregation (shows engineering depth), compliance features (shows regulatory awareness), and map integration (shows GIS capability). The skepticism is around AI claims -- a tech-savvy visitor wants to know what "AI" actually means under the hood. The gaps they identify (API access, detailed return analytics) reflect their background as a self-directed investor who is accustomed to rich analytical tools in the stock/crypto world. The value prop scores well but not perfectly because it lacks the technical depth signals a software engineer looks for.
