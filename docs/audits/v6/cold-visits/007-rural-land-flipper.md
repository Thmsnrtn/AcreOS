# Cold Visitor Simulation 007: Rural Land Flipper (Low Tech)

**Visitor profile:** Rural land flipper based in Arkansas. Does 2-3 deals per year, buying cheap rural parcels and reselling at a markup. Currently uses paper files, a spiral notebook for tracking leads, phone calls for outreach, and his wife does bookkeeping in a paper ledger. Very low tech comfort -- uses a smartphone for calls and texts but has never used a CRM, spreadsheet, or business software. Found AcreOS through a land investing Facebook group.

**Date:** 2026-04-18
**Pages visited:** Landing, Pricing
**Source URL:** https://acreos.fly.dev/ and https://acreos.fly.dev/pricing

---

## Question 1: CATEGORY (60 seconds on landing page)

**Score: 3/5**

### What this visitor sees

A badge that says "Now in Public Beta." The headline: **"The operating system for real estate professionals."** Below it: strategy badges including **Land.** Below that: feature cards with terms like Portfolio Mapping, AI Valuations, AI Deal Intelligence, Campaign Automation, Compliance.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'The operating system for real estate professionals.' I don't know what an operating system is. Like Windows? This is a website though."

> "I see 'Land' in those little badge things. That's what I do. And 'Creative Finance' -- I've done a couple seller-financed deals."

> "'Replaces your spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and guesswork.' I don't have spreadsheets or tools. I have a notebook. But 'guesswork' -- yeah, I do a lot of that. I just drive around looking at county records and calling people."

> "'Manages leads, automates outreach, and closes deals -- powered by AI.' I don't know what 'automates outreach' means. AI like ChatGPT? I've heard of that but never used it."

> "'Portfolio Mapping -- Visualize every parcel on interactive maps with property boundaries, flood zones, and zoning data.' That actually sounds useful. I spend hours at the county courthouse looking up plat maps and flood zone info. If I could see that on my phone..."

> "'AI Valuations -- Instant property valuations powered by comps analysis and 18 open data sources.' I comp properties by calling local agents and looking at recent sales in the county clerk records. This might be faster."

> "But this whole page looks... complicated. There's a lot of jargon. 'CRM,' 'pipeline,' 'sequences,' 'BYOK.' I don't know what any of that means."

### Assessment

The visitor recognizes "Land" and understands that the mapping and valuation features could help them. But the page is written for tech-comfortable operators who already understand CRM concepts. Terms like "operating system," "pipeline," "outreach automation," "BYOK," and "sequences" are meaningless to this visitor. The value is there but it's buried under jargon. A 2-3 deal/year flipper using paper thinks in terms of "I need to find a cheap parcel, figure out what it's worth, and find a buyer" -- the landing page never frames it that simply.

## Question 2: VALUE PROP (next 60 seconds)

**Score: 2/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "'Sophie, your AI copilot, scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities.' What's a copilot? Like in an airplane? And 'scores leads' -- what does scoring a lead mean? I either talk to someone who wants to sell or I don't."

> "'Document Generation -- Auto-generate purchase agreements, contracts, and closing documents in seconds.' Now THAT I understand. I currently write purchase agreements by hand or use a template my attorney gave me in Word. If this can fill it in automatically, that saves time."

> "'Campaign Automation -- Multi-channel outreach with SMS, email, and direct mail sequences.' I don't do campaigns. I drive around, I talk to people, I send maybe 20 letters a month by hand. Multi-channel? I have one channel -- my phone."

> "'Compliance Built-In -- Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks.' I didn't even know I was supposed to check a do-not-call list. Maybe I should look into that."

> "'18 Free data sources' and '$0 to get started' -- okay, free is good. But '500+ Properties managed' -- there's only 500 properties in the whole system? That doesn't inspire confidence."

> "I keep looking for something simple like 'Keep track of your parcels and what you paid' or 'See what a property is worth instantly.' Those are on this page but they're mixed in with so much other stuff I almost missed them."

### Assessment

The two features that genuinely excite this visitor -- document generation and AI valuations -- are presented alongside many features that are either confusing or irrelevant to them. The page assumes a baseline understanding of CRM/marketing automation concepts that this visitor does not have. The social proof stat of "500+ Properties managed" does not reassure someone who is evaluating whether to trust software for the first time. There is no "simple mode" messaging that says "Even if you've never used software like this before, you can start in 5 minutes."

## Question 3: PRICING (2 minutes)

**Score: 3/5**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Free: $0, 10 leads, 3 properties. I only do 2-3 deals a year. So 3 properties might actually be enough. And 10 leads -- I usually only talk to maybe 15-20 people before I find a deal. So 10 is tight but the free tier is where I'd start."

> "Starter: $20/mo. I spend about $300 a year on stamps and envelopes for my letters. $20/mo is $240/year. If it replaces my postage and helps me find deals faster, it's a wash."

> "Pro: $49/mo, $588/year. That's a lot of money for 2-3 deals a year. My average profit on a flip is $8,000-12,000. So $588 is 5-7% of my annual profit. I need to be sure it's worth it."

> "What's 'BYOK Data Providers'? I have no idea what that means. And 'AI Requests / day' -- I don't know what I'd use AI requests for. 25 per day on the free plan... is that a lot or a little?"

> "'Team Seats' -- it's just me and my wife. 1 seat on Free and Starter. Can my wife see stuff too? She does the bookkeeping. Do I need to pay for a second seat?"

> "'Stripe Connect Payments' -- I don't know what Stripe is. I collect payments via cashier's checks or wire transfers through the title company."

> "The feature comparison table has 16 rows and I understand maybe half of them. 'Sequences,' 'BYOK Data Providers,' 'SMS/Voice Outreach' -- I know what SMS is but the rest of this is jargon."

### Assessment

The free tier is surprisingly well-matched for this visitor's volume -- 10 leads and 3 properties could cover a typical deal cycle. The $20/mo Starter tier is in their budget if they can see the value. But the pricing page is written for an audience that already understands what CRM features are. Half the feature comparison table is meaningless to this visitor. There is no "perfect for beginners" or "just getting started" positioning on any tier.

## Question 4: SIGNUP READINESS (5 minutes total)

**Score: 2/5**

### Would you sign up?

**Maybe, but with significant reluctance and confusion. Would need encouragement from someone in the Facebook group who has actually used it.**

### What the visitor says out loud

> "I can see that the mapping and property valuation stuff would help me. Instead of driving to the courthouse, I could look things up on my phone. That alone might be worth it."

> "But I'm worried I'll sign up and then have no idea what to do. There's no tutorial on this page. No video showing how it works. No 'Here's what it looks like inside.' I'd be going in blind."

> "The signup button says 'Get Started Free.' Free is good. But what if I get in there and it's all complicated dashboards and I can't figure out how to just add a property?"

> "'Now in Public Beta' -- I don't know what beta means. Is this a test version? Am I a guinea pig? Will my data be safe?"

> "There's no phone number anywhere. No chat. No way to talk to a real person. If I get stuck, who do I call? I'm not going to figure out a complicated website by myself."

> "I wish someone from the Facebook group had posted screenshots. Then I'd know what I'm getting into."

> "Honestly, I'll probably show this to my son next time he visits. He's good with computers. Maybe he can help me set it up."

### Unanswered questions

- What does it look like inside? Are there screenshots?
- Is there a tutorial or video walkthrough?
- Can I just track properties without learning all the other features?
- Is there a phone number for support?
- What does "beta" mean -- is my data safe?
- Can my wife access the account without paying for a second seat?
- Does it work on my phone?
- Can I type in an address and see property info immediately?
- How do I add a property I'm looking at buying?

### Hesitations

- Overwhelming amount of features and jargon for a first-time software user
- No screenshots or video of the actual product
- No human support channel visible (no phone, no chat)
- "Public Beta" is alarming to someone who doesn't understand tech terminology
- No mobile app mentioned (this visitor is phone-only)
- No onboarding or "beginner" pathway communicated
- Fear of wasting time learning something too complex

## Question 5: FIRST-RUN MENTAL MODEL (post-signup)

**Score: 1/5**

### What the visitor sees

After signing up via Clerk (which itself requires entering an email, getting a verification code, etc.), the sidebar shows: **Dashboard, Leads, Properties, Deals, Campaigns, Inbox, AI Hub, Intelligence, Finance, Settings.** Many items expand into sub-menus with additional options.

### What the visitor says out loud

> "Okay, I'm in. There's a sidebar with a bunch of items. Dashboard, Leads, Properties... I see Properties, that's what I want. Let me click that."

> "There's a list... it's empty. How do I add a property? Is there an 'Add' button? Let me look... okay, I see a button. It wants an address, APN, county, state, acreage, purchase price. I can do that."

> "But wait -- I entered the address and nothing happened. It didn't pull up any info. I expected it to show me the parcel on a map with the flood zone and zoning like the landing page said. Do I have to do that manually?"

> "Now I see 'Leads' in the sidebar. What's a lead? I think that's a person who might sell to me? In my notebook I just write their name and phone number. Let me click Leads."

> "Another empty list. 'Add Lead' button. It asks for a lot of information -- name, phone, email, address, source, status, motivation, property APN... I just have a name and phone number. Do I really need to fill all this out?"

> "What is 'Deals'? 'Campaigns'? 'Intelligence'? 'AI Hub'? I don't know what to click. There are so many options."

> "'Finance' shows 'Seller-financed notes,' 'Cash Flow,' 'Portfolio,' 'Capital Markets.' I don't know what capital markets means. I just want to track what I bought and what I sold it for."

> "I'm lost. There's no guidance. No 'Start here' button. No 'Welcome, let's set up your first property.' I'm going to close this and go back to my notebook."

### Assessment

This is a catastrophic first-run experience for a low-tech user. The product dumps them into a feature-rich workspace with zero guidance, no progressive disclosure, and no simplified "beginner mode." The sidebar alone has 10+ top-level items with expandable sub-menus. There is no wizard, no tutorial, no contextual help, and no way to call someone for assistance. A user who has never touched CRM software will be overwhelmed within 60 seconds and abandon. The product assumes a baseline of software literacy that this visitor simply does not have.

## Summary

| Question | Score | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| 1. Category | 3/5 | Sees "Land" badge; mapping/valuations resonate; jargon is alienating |
| 2. Value Prop | 2/5 | Doc gen and valuations interest; rest is confusing or irrelevant |
| 3. Pricing | 3/5 | Free tier fits volume; half the feature table is incomprehensible |
| 4. Signup Readiness | 2/5 | Reluctant; no screenshots, no video, no phone support, no tutorials |
| 5. First-Run | 1/5 | Completely lost; no onboarding, too many options, no guidance |

**Overall: 2.2/5** -- This visitor represents a real segment of the land market: small-volume rural flippers who have never used business software. They are not dumb -- they successfully find, evaluate, buy, and sell land using paper and phone calls. But AcreOS is built for a tech-comfortable operator and makes zero accommodation for beginners. The two features that would change this visitor's life (interactive parcel maps on their phone and instant AI valuations) are present but buried under CRM jargon they do not understand. Actionable fixes: (1) add a product screenshot or demo video to the landing page, (2) add a "New to software?" or "Getting started" pathway, (3) implement a first-run onboarding wizard that asks "What do you want to do first?" and guides them through adding their first property, (4) provide a phone number or live chat for support, and (5) create a simplified "essentials" view that hides advanced features until the user is ready.
