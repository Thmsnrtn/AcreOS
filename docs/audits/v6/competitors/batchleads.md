## 5. Core Workflows

### 5a. Build Lead Lists
Use 140+ filters to create targeted lists from on-market and off-market property data nationwide. One-click list generation. BatchRankAI scores and ranks leads by deal potential. Save properties to multiple lists simultaneously.

### 5b. Skip Trace for Free
Retrieve owner phone numbers and email addresses included with all plans (no per-record charge). Skip trace on add-to-list or in bulk. Results include all-inclusive phone numbers and emails.

### 5c. Analyze Deals with AI
Reia AI chatbot provides instant property insights: analyze comps, calculate offers, estimate ARV (after-repair value), and make strategic recommendations. Property evaluation tools for side-by-side comparison.

### 5d. Engage via Multi-Channel Outreach
Dialer AI provides live AI prompts and property data during calls. Direct mail campaigns (letters + postcards). Email campaigns. All outreach tracked within the platform CRM.

### 5e. Canvass in the Field
Mobile app for driving neighborhoods (Driving for Dollars). Virtual canvassing from desktop. Flag distressed properties, add to lists, and skip trace on the spot.

## 7. What BatchLeads Communicates Clearly That AcreOS Does Not

1. **"Free skip tracing" as a headline differentiator.** BatchLeads leads with "free skip tracing" in the subheadline. This is the single most expensive line item for real estate investors, and making it free is a powerful conversion lever. AcreOS does not mention skip tracing on its landing page at all.

2. **AI as a deal-closing tool, not a feature category.** BatchRankAI "scores leads by deal potential." Reia AI "analyzes deals and calculates offers." Dialer AI "provides live prompts mid-call." Each AI feature has a name, a specific job, and a clear outcome. AcreOS's "Sophie, your AI copilot" is vaguer — "scores leads, drafts offers, and surfaces the best opportunities" lacks the specificity of BatchLeads' three distinct AI products.

3. **Role-based navigation.** The Solutions dropdown includes explicit roles: "Real Estate Investor, Wholesaler, Agent/Broker, Title Companies, Mortgage Lenders, Home Services." This lets visitors self-identify immediately. AcreOS has no role-based entry points.

4. **Outcome-oriented headline.** "UNLOCK THE BEST DEALS" promises a result. AcreOS's "The operating system for real estate professionals" is a category claim, not an outcome promise.

5. **Specific filter count.** "140+ customizable lead filters" gives prospects a concrete sense of depth. AcreOS mentions no filter count.

6. **Social proof with specificity.** "Trusted by 10,000+ Real Estate Professionals" is a concrete user count. AcreOS shows "500+ Properties managed" which is a feature metric, not a trust signal.

## 8. Transferable Patterns AcreOS Should Adopt

1. **Name each AI capability separately.** BatchLeads has three distinct AI products (BatchRankAI, Reia AI, Dialer AI), each with a clear job. AcreOS should consider whether "Sophie" needs to be broken into named capabilities (e.g., Sophie Score, Sophie Offer, Sophie Insights) to communicate specificity.

2. **Lead with outcomes, not architecture.** "Unlock the best deals" beats "The operating system for..." in immediate comprehension. The hero headline should promise what the user gets, not what the product is.

3. **Make skip tracing a headline feature.** If AcreOS includes skip tracing, it should be foregrounded as aggressively as BatchLeads does — ideally with "free" or "included" language.

4. **Show lead volume, not lead count.** BatchLeads' tiers are defined by "10,000 monthly leads" / "35,000 monthly leads." This frames the product as a volume tool. AcreOS's "250 leads" tier feels like a ceiling rather than a capability.

5. **Add a role selector.** Even a simple "I am a..." dropdown or nav section (Investor / Agent / Wholesaler / Team) helps visitors self-qualify and see relevant messaging.

6. **Use social proof as a trust metric.** Replace "500+ Properties managed" with a user count or deal count. Properties managed is a feature stat; users trusting you is a credibility stat.

7. **Price AI add-ons separately.** BatchLeads charges $19/mo for Reia AI and $89/mo for Dialer AI. This signals that AI is valuable and premium. AcreOS bundles everything, which may undersell the AI capability or make it feel like a gimmick.
