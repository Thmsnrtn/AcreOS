## 5. Core Workflows

1. **List Build -> Stack -> Skip Trace**: Pull property lists by criteria, layer/stack multiple lists to find overlapping motivated sellers, then auto-skip-trace to get contact info. All happens inside one screen without external tools.

2. **Inbound Lead Capture -> Speed to Lead -> Qualification**: Seller fills out website form -> Speed-to-Lead AI calls within 15 seconds -> AI qualifies, asks pre-set questions, books appointment or transfers live to agent.

3. **Drip Campaign Automation**: Set up multi-channel sequences (SMS + email + RVM + direct mail + task reminders) with configurable time delays. Leads enter campaigns automatically based on status or list membership. The system maintains follow-up indefinitely without manual intervention.

4. **Deal Pipeline -> Accounting -> KPI**: Manage deal stages, track expenses and revenue per deal, then roll up metrics to KPI dashboards showing cost per lead, cost per deal, and campaign ROI. Financial data flows directly from deal records to reports.

5. **AI Agent Ecosystem**: Three distinct AI agents (VoiceFollow, CallAnswer, Conversational) handle outbound follow-up calls, inbound call answering, and two-way SMS qualification respectively. These operate autonomously and escalate to humans when needed.

## 7. What REsimpli Communicates Clearly That AcreOS Doesn't

1. **Named AI agents with specific jobs.** "VoiceFollow AI," "CallAnswer AI," and "Conversational AI" are branded, distinct agents. Each has a one-sentence job description. The user knows exactly what each agent does without reading documentation.

2. **"#1" authority claim in the headline.** Whether defensible or not, the "#1 AI-powered CRM" claim creates immediate category authority. It forces competitors to respond.

3. **Explicit "Compare" navigation tab.** REsimpli dedicates a top-level nav item to competitive comparisons. They publish detailed teardowns of PropStream, DealMachine, Podio, and others. This captures high-intent search traffic ("REsimpli vs X") and controls the narrative.

4. **Free skip tracing as a headline benefit.** Skip tracing is typically a per-lead cost ($0.10-0.25/record elsewhere). REsimpli bundles it free into every plan with volume tiers. This is stated prominently: "FREE Skip Tracing and Cash Buyer Searches Across All Plans."

5. **Speed-to-Lead with a specific time claim.** "Automatically calls new leads within 15 seconds." The specificity of "15 seconds" makes the value concrete and measurable, not abstract.

6. **Full accounting built into the CRM.** Most investor CRMs push financial tracking to QuickBooks or spreadsheets. REsimpli includes deal-level expense/revenue tracking and ROI calculation natively. This is a meaningful workflow consolidation.

## 8. Transferable Patterns AcreOS Should Adopt

1. **Brand and name AI capabilities individually.** REsimpli's three named AI agents (VoiceFollow, CallAnswer, Conversational) are more memorable and marketable than a generic "AI features" section. AcreOS should name its AI capabilities as distinct agents or tools with clear job descriptions.

2. **Publish competitive comparison pages.** The dedicated "Compare" nav tab captures searchers comparing tools. AcreOS should create structured comparison pages (AcreOS vs. Pebble, AcreOS vs. REsimpli) that honestly address differences and highlight strengths.

3. **Bundle skip tracing into the plan price.** Per-lookup pricing creates friction and unpredictable costs. REsimpli's "free skip tracing" positioning (even though it is volume-capped) removes a major objection. AcreOS should evaluate bundling data lookups into plan tiers.

4. **Lead response time as a KPI.** "Speed to Lead" is a named feature category, not just a metric. Framing fast response as a product capability -- not just a best practice -- makes the platform feel active rather than passive.

5. **Multi-channel drip sequences as a visual builder.** REsimpli's drip campaigns combine SMS + email + RVM + direct mail + task reminders in one configurable sequence. If AcreOS has or plans automated follow-up, it should present the multi-channel nature visually.

6. **Case studies and testimonials as top-level nav items.** REsimpli puts social proof in the main navigation, not buried in a footer. This signals confidence and gives prospects immediate access to validation stories.
