## 5. Core Workflows

### 5a. Find Leads
Search 160M+ properties using 165+ filters across 20 pre-built lead lists (Cash Buyers, High Equity, Foreclosures, Vacant, Tax Liens, Divorce, Free & Clear, Flippers). Save matching leads to custom lists. Lead Automator runs continuously in the background.

### 5b. Research & Analyze
Pull property reports with transaction history, tax records, foreclosure details, MLS data, and owner info. Run comps using public records + MLS. Use Rehab Calculator for flip projections. Use Heat Map to identify high-potential markets.

### 5c. Skip Trace Owners
Bulk skip trace saved leads to get phone numbers and emails. Results ranked by likelihood of connection. DNC and litigator flags included for compliance.

### 5d. Outreach & Marketing
Click-to-Dial calling, BatchDialer campaigns, email campaigns ($0.02/each), postcards ($0.48+), and direct mail. Dialer Campaigns track call outcomes.

### 5e. Mobile Scouting (Drive for Dollars)
Use PropStream Mobile to search properties in the field, pull comps, access owner contact info, run skip tracing, and start outbound marketing from phone.

## 7. What PropStream Communicates Clearly That AcreOS Does Not

1. **Scale and data authority.** "160+ Million Properties Nationwide" with daily MLS updates is stated within seconds. AcreOS says "18 free data sources" but never quantifies property coverage or freshness.

2. **Specific lead list taxonomy.** PropStream names 20 distinct lead lists (Cash Buyers, High Equity, Foreclosures, Vacant, Tax Liens, Divorce, Free & Clear, Flippers, etc.). AcreOS landing page has no equivalent vocabulary — "leads" is generic.

3. **Role-based landing pages.** PropStream has dedicated pages for Investors, Agents, Lenders, Property Managers. AcreOS lists strategy badges (Wholesaling, Fix & Flip, etc.) but has no role-specific messaging.

4. **Compliance as a feature.** DNC flags, litigator flags, and built-in compliance are prominently described. AcreOS mentions "Phone compliance, do-not-call list checks" but buries it in a feature card rather than foregrounding trust.

5. **Education ecosystem.** PropStream Academy, live webinars, video library, and community forums are top-level navigation items. AcreOS has no education presence.

6. **Mobile as a first-class workflow.** PropStream dedicates an entire page and nav item to mobile (Drive for Dollars scouting, field comps). AcreOS does not mention mobile at all.

## 8. Transferable Patterns AcreOS Should Adopt

1. **Quantify data coverage on the hero.** Replace vague "18 free data sources" with concrete property count and update frequency (e.g., "X million parcels, updated daily").

2. **Name your lead lists.** Create a visible taxonomy of lead types (Cash Buyers, High Equity, Motivated Sellers, etc.) rather than generic "leads" language. This helps prospects map their existing workflow onto AcreOS.

3. **Offer a consumption metric.** PropStream's "saves & exports" model gives users a clear mental model of what they're buying. AcreOS's "250 leads" tier feels restrictive without explaining what constitutes a lead vs. a property record.

4. **Add role-based navigation.** Even a simple "For Investors / For Agents / For Teams" section on the landing page helps visitors self-select.

5. **Lead with a free trial, not a free tier.** PropStream's "7-Day Free Trial With 50 Free Leads" creates urgency and a clear onboarding path. AcreOS's "Free" tier at "$0 / 10 leads, 3 properties" feels like a toy sandbox rather than a trial of the real product.

6. **Build an education nav item.** Webinars, templates, calculators, and how-to content are table-stakes for real estate SaaS. An "Academy" or "Resources" link signals depth.

7. **Feature the mobile app.** If AcreOS has or plans mobile support, it needs a dedicated mention — Drive for Dollars is a core investor workflow.
