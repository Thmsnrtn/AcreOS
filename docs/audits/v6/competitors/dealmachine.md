## 1. Landing Page -- First 5 Seconds

**Above-the-fold headline:** "Real estate data software that helps you find motivated leads"

**Subheadline:** "Search nationwide property and ownership data to identify opportunities and contact the right owner instantly."

**Primary CTA:** "Start Free" (prominent button in the hero section)

## 5. Core Workflows

1. **Drive -> Tag -> Contact**: Open the mobile app, drive a neighborhood, tap to tag distressed/vacant properties. The system auto-pulls owner info (skip trace) and adds the property to your pipeline. Route tracking ensures you never duplicate streets.

2. **List Build -> Filter -> Export**: Search 150M+ properties using 70+ filters (tax delinquent, absentee owner, free & clear, cash buyer, recently sold, etc.). Apply up to 700 data points to narrow results. Export to your pipeline or directly into a mail campaign.

3. **Mail Sequence -> Follow-Up -> Close**: Design a multi-touch direct mail sequence with personalized letters including property photos/street view. System auto-sends at configurable intervals (e.g., every 21 days). Track responses via dedicated call tracking numbers.

4. **Workflow Automation -> Task Assignment**: Define criteria-based rules (e.g., "properties 15+ years old with absentee owner"). When new leads enter from any source (driving, list builder, upload), the workflow auto-filters, auto-skip-traces, and auto-assigns qualified leads to team members. Non-matching leads are auto-discarded.

5. **AI Deal Analysis (Alma)**: Ask Alma to analyze a specific property. The AI evaluates based on investment strategy (wholesale, fix-and-flip, or buy-and-hold), estimates ARV and rental prices, generates cold call scripts, and answers general investing questions.

## 7. What DealMachine Communicates Clearly That AcreOS Doesn't

1. **Data scale as a headline number.** "150 million+ properties" appears repeatedly. The sheer number creates immediate credibility for lead generation. AcreOS does not communicate the scale of its data in a single, memorable number.

2. **"Unlimited" as a pricing strategy.** "Unlimited contact info" and "unlimited skip tracing" across all plans eliminates the fear of per-lookup costs. The word "unlimited" appears prominently in pricing and feature descriptions.

3. **Investment-strategy-specific AI.** Alma analyzes deals based on the user's stated strategy (wholesale, fix-and-flip, buy-and-hold). This is not generic AI -- it adapts its output to the user's business model.

4. **Solutions segmented by investor type.** The homepage has a section labeled "A solution for every investor" with distinct pathways for Wholesaling, Fix & Flip, and Buy & Hold. Each investor type sees themselves reflected.

5. **Mail pricing as a competitive lever.** Per-piece mail costs are displayed prominently per tier ($0.72, $0.67, $0.62, $0.57). This transparency lets prospects calculate actual campaign costs before signing up.

6. **"Start Free" as the primary CTA.** Not "Request Demo" or "Contact Sales." The immediate action is to start using the product for free (7-day trial). The friction is minimal.

7. **Corporate skip tracing depth.** "Trace LLC ownership up to five levels deep" is a specific, technical capability that experienced investors immediately recognize as valuable. It communicates depth of data, not just breadth.

## 8. Transferable Patterns AcreOS Should Adopt

1. **Lead a headline with a concrete data number.** "150 million+ properties" is DealMachine's most memorable stat. AcreOS should identify its equivalent anchor number (records, parcels, data points) and make it unavoidable on the homepage.

2. **Segment the homepage by investor type.** DealMachine's "Wholesaling / Fix & Flip / Buy & Hold" sections let each visitor self-select into a relevant pathway. AcreOS should consider segments relevant to its audience (e.g., by deal volume, by team size, by asset type).

3. **Show per-unit economics in pricing.** DealMachine displays per-piece mail costs alongside monthly subscription prices. This lets prospects build their own ROI model. AcreOS should expose per-unit costs for data lookups, mailings, or other variable-cost features directly in the pricing table.

4. **"Start Free" over "Book a Demo."** DealMachine's primary CTA is self-service. Pebble also offers a free plan. The pattern is clear: let prospects use the product immediately. If AcreOS requires a demo-first flow, it is adding friction that competitors have eliminated.

5. **Name the AI assistant.** DealMachine's AI is called "Alma." It has a personality descriptor ("friendly") and specific capabilities. A named AI entity is more trustworthy and marketable than "AI-powered features."

6. **Route tracking and "virtual driving" as mobile-first features.** DealMachine's driving-for-dollars workflow is inherently mobile. If AcreOS has or plans field-based features, leading with the mobile experience (not the desktop dashboard) would match how investors actually work.

7. **Transparent tiering by capacity, not by feature gates.** DealMachine tiers primarily differ on lead limits (20K/60K/120K), user counts, and export volumes -- not by locking out core features. This "more of the same" tiering model reduces upgrade anxiety compared to "you need Pro to get feature X" models.
