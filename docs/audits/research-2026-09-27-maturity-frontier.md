## What changed after the two reports met

The reports agree on the right destination: an evidence-grounded system that makes a real investor's decisions, authorized actions and obligations easier to carry, with a founder surface that compresses operating truth. The attached report adds important existing strengths that a superficial audit might miss: shared/evidence/claim.ts, shared/outcomes/calibration.ts, the founder stepAwayReadiness.ts, customer accounting, onboarding signals, and a command palette. Keep and compose these.

But the supplied report's ten sequential campaigns risk becoming the very recursive maturity program the product needs to escape. Do not require a universal Proof Lattice service, zero AI bypasses, a new Intent Compiler, a new certificate store, completed multi-strategy ontology and a reconstructed interface before a first customer can use the land loop. An end-state contract is useful; a first implementation should be an existing pathway with one real customer obligation and a receipt. The deepest architecture question is: which invariants must hold through a customer's consequential journey, and where do the present paths break them?

The strongest new design contribution is a decision-boundary contract that simultaneously governs evidence, economics, authority, external effect and attention. It can be adopted one path at a time. A broad institution is earned when these same contracts repeatedly lower the cost and risk of later strategies.

Further source verification on 26 September materially changes the immediate priority. Customer-reachable bookkeeping and tax-output paths have a more consequential contradiction than the cache issue. The financial qualification slice in the addendum below should be reconciled before any customer relies on those reports or exports.

An additional offer-path trace exposed the complementary acquisition-side boundary: a generated agricultural estimate can be promoted to a parcel-sale comparable and thereby bypass the calculator's no-comp refusal. If the blind-offer journey is being used before tax exports, this deserves equally immediate attention. The two paths test the same institutional rule: a consequential dollar amount must retain the type and provenance of the observation that supports it.

## New priority: qualify the financial and action boundaries

Selective source tracing found three consequential seams the earlier reports had not elevated. They do not erase the sound double-entry and Form 1098 work; they demonstrate why a coherent customer journey matters more than an inventory of implemented finance features.

### A. Tax output currently reverses who paid whom

At server/services/bookkeeping.ts, generateAnnualInterestReport groups borrower payments received by the organization, sets requires1099 at $600 interest received on a note, and generate1099IntForms casts the organization as payer and borrower as recipient of that income. form1099Batch.ts turns that into per-borrower PDFs, a 1096 summary and an electronic filing file. The authenticated routes-bookkeeping.ts exposes form data; routes-accounting.ts allows an org owner/admin to generate a batch. These are source-level paths, not proof that anyone has filed one.

The IRS Form 1099-INT instructions describe reporting interest paid to a recipient, with threshold and exemptions depending on the type of payment. Its Form 1098 description addresses qualifying mortgage interest received from an individual in the course of business at $600 or more. AcreOS has a separate form1098Batch.ts explicitly recognizing the organization as receiver. The two paths therefore contradict each other on the same direction of money. The applicability of either form to a given vacant-land instrument, taxpayer and transaction still requires competent tax review.

Immediate product posture: do not represent the borrower-payment 1099 generator as a qualified filing output. At implementation HEAD, verify whether the route remains reachable and whether legitimate organizational interest paid out has a separate use case. If reproduced, restrict/refuse the unsupported export and ask a tax professional to define payer, recipient, instrument, thresholds, exemptions, tax year and filing workflow before restoring it. Preserve the existing 1098 path for independent qualification; do not assume every land note requires Form 1098. tests/unit/bookkeeping1099.test.ts currently asserts the $600 borrower 1099 interpretation and must be corrected rather than treated as proof of legal correctness.

### B. Portfolio annual totals are computed as zero

In generateAnnualInterestReport, per-note principal, interest and fees accumulate in cents, but the three initialized portfolio-total counters are never incremented before conversion and return. A note can show $1,500 of interest while totalInterestIncome, totalPrincipalReceived and totalLateFeesCollected return zero. getPortfolioAnnualSummary derives gross revenue and estimated tax from those zero totals; authenticated /api/bookkeeping/annual-report and /portfolio-summary expose them. The form batch independently sums generated forms, so do not claim its batch total is necessarily zero.

A single focused fixture with two notes, a reversal/correction and nonzero fees should demonstrate the current failure and its repair, with per-note and portfolio reconciliation. Investigate the payment-status semantics before calling a completed row cleared money. The bookkeeping.test.ts suite contains an inline copy of calculateDealPnL rather than exercising the service, and no test referencing generateAnnualInterestReport appeared in the repository's test code search. This limits the meaning of a green suite; it does not prove no other test indirectly covers the function.

### C. P&L and tax labels exceed their inputs

calculateDealPnL receives a selling price, acquisition price, expenses and dates, labels a seller-financed transaction installment_sale automatically, and labels other transactions short/long capital gain by holding days. Its cashOnCashReturn uses projected net profit divided by purchase plus expenses minus down payment, without an upstream loan, collection timing or risk; if expenses also include the purchase category, the purchase may be counted twice. The route accepts these arguments from the request body. IRS Publication 537 describes conditions and exceptions for installment reporting, including dealer property held for sale to customers; the property's legal/tax characterization cannot be inferred from dealType and a date alone.

Retain deterministic scenario economics as an investor planning aid, but rename projected margins as projections with explicit scope. Do not emit tax-treatment certainty or a cash-on-cash return without the required investment and cash timing model. Give a tax adviser export of source transactions, contract terms and user-established classifications, with unresolved eligibility visible. The test that says "seller_finance always gets installment_sale treatment" is a counterexample to correct product semantics, even if it passes.

### D. Decision memory can freeze no evidence for real opportunity/deal choices

decisionStore.ts correctly gathers property and parcel claims and rejects foreign scenario IDs. Its opportunity and deal branches deliberately freeze zero claims: opportunity has a ParcelRef rather than a typed property link; deal evidence lives on a property with no canonical typed edge. This avoids the earlier, worse bug of attaching an unrelated same-number property, but "empty unknowns" can look like there were no unresolved questions when evidence was not evaluated.

The mature contract needs not_evaluated, evaluated_none and evaluated_with_claims to be distinct. For the first land journey, freeze decisions against the supported property subject, or explicitly mark the opportunity/deal evidence coverage and require a verified typed relation before promising a sourced decision there. Do not restore integer-ID guessing. A deeper relationship model is justified when a real journey needs it, and must preserve historical decisions as originally known.

### E. An unavailable autonomy check is treated as full confidence

autopilot/act.ts allows the supplementary risk assessment to throw and fall through as null; a pre-mortem failure similarly becomes null. Its caller continuousLoop.ts initializes loopConfidence=1 and leaves it there if reading calibration pairs fails. Yet forecast.ts deliberately maps unproven to 0.4, which tightens the risk gate. This is an actual fallback contradiction. The main policy gate may still block a move; the finding is that a higher-risk pass may lack the promised supplementary check.

Exercise a policy-pass, medium-risk move with calibration read failure and a risk-assessor failure. The outcome should preserve the default safe authority level or produce an explicit hold/retry, with one durable incident. Do not turn all internal jobs off because a pre-mortem model is unavailable; decide for each action class whether that optional model was ever a required gate. Also note that planAndAct returns acted when a dispatch is enqueued; continuousLoop then marks a growth target dispatched. This is not an externally observed action receipt. The founder and customer surfaces must display queued, executed, confirmed, failed and unknown as separate states.

These findings are selective code readings at a pinned commit. They have not been run against the database or production. Tax-form correctness requires a qualified review of the actual business and filing context; the IRS primary sources establish the payer/recipient direction and explain why a blanket dealType tax label is not justified.

### F. A due-date signal can become an unearned collection task

notePaymentDueDetector.ts runs daily at 11:00 UTC through expiryDetectorJobs.ts. It classifies nextPaymentDate.getTime() < now.getTime() as overdue and emits payment.missed. The originated notes.nextPaymentDate is a timestamp, while acquired notes use a calendar date and an explicit gracePeriodDays field. A due date represented at midnight will be called overdue at the 11:00 UTC scan on that calendar day. The scanner does not inspect the payment record or the contractual grace period when choosing its classification. This is a source-level conditional defect; the actual stored timestamps and installed customer workflows were not observed.

The installed workflow-engine.ts template can notify a team and create a high-priority task to collect an "overdue" payment. Its borrower email was removed, a sound refusal that limits direct harm. Yet an incorrect collection cue still wastes the customer's attention and can prompt a mistaken manual contact. Define separate due today, unpaid after contractual due day, within grace, and actionably delinquent states from a schedule plus payment observation; the governing contract and applicable rules determine the transitions. Do not assume a grace period universally forbids a reminder or equates to a legal delinquency cure. Test midnight UTC, local due day, DST, just-after-due, grace boundary, late payment received before scan, and an unknown schedule. A due-date alert must not become a collection workflow without the evidence that workflow claims.

There is a second handoff flaw: the scanner publishes a durable mesh event, then calls emitPaymentMissedForFinding. That helper calls an in-memory workflowEngine.emit without waiting for a durable workflow run; the engine's processQueue catches and logs trigger failures internally. If it fails or the process dies after mesh publication, later scans see the dedupe key and skip the workflow forever. The comment's "exactly once" claim covers publication, not downstream completion. A reproducible crash/failure between those steps should leave a retryable, visible pending effect; reconcile against workflow-run receipts using existing event/outbox facilities before adding a new queue. The current test double checks publication and dedupe but does not exercise this failure boundary.

### G. Founder runway is a projection on unverified cash and contracted revenue

finance/runwayModel.ts calls the sum of three internal ledger reserve buckets cashOnHandUsd and substitutes a founder-declared environment value if it is larger. It offsets current costs with tier-priced MRR from active organization rows, rather than observed invoice payments or bank deposits. routes-founder-money.ts and the founder Money page expose the result as modeled cash on hand and runway; financeAudit.ts flags a crunch only when the modeled months fall below a threshold, returning no finding when the cash basis is absent. The source's transparency inputs are a strength, but they do not transform internal allocations into a reconciled account balance. Stripe's subscription object describes active subscription status; it is not a record of each cleared invoice or deposit.

Show two separately named founder views: observed liquidity (last reconciled bank/account balance, pending payouts, reserved duties, date and coverage; unavailable when no account feed exists) and planning runway (declared/model cash and revenue assumptions, with sensitivity to collection failure and infrastructure spend). If no observed liquidity exists, the absence/financial audit should say unknown, not quietly green. Do not block a private founder from using a clearly labeled planning estimate; prevent the estimate from authorizing spend, owner distributions or a "safe to step away" claim. A no-customer, one-active-but-unpaid, one-paid-and-refunded, and a founder-declared-cash case would distinguish the models.

### H. Keep the regulated servicing scaffold distinct from a qualified rail

The pure paymentApplication/index.ts has thoughtful suspense-account tests, but repository code search at this commit found applyPayment and applyAcquiredNotePayment called from tests, not a payment-posting production path. If it is later wired, its writer reads the suspense balance, inserts an application, then updates a JSON ledger and balance in separate transactions. A failure after insert makes the retry return created:false without completing the balance write; concurrent payments can both read the same balance. The read and unique balance index key only on loanId, omitting organization and note type. The reg-z.ts schema itself uses note type in a different late-fee key because ID spaces can collide. This is a pre-wiring qualification defect, not proof that current customers suffered a suspense error. Require org/type/loan identity, atomic application and balance update, serial behavior for two payments, and retry after each write boundary before calling it operative. Scope any Regulation Z assertion to the actual consumer/dwelling/servicer facts; the CFPB regulation is not a universal land-note rule.

Likewise, servicerRemittance.ts creates draft remittances through an authenticated route and stubs the PDF. It selects only current, nonsuperseded owners and allocates every payment in the month to them, without checking when ownership became effective or whether a payment was reversed or settled. That is not a payment remittance rail. Qualify historical ownership, payment status, fee agreement and actual transfer/receipt before graduating this feature; defer a general multi-owner servicing platform until customers need it.

Strongest counterarguments and falsifiers: A production note may encode due time at the end of its local day, and no customer may have installed the dunning template; that would lower the immediate exposure of the due-day defect. A reliably synchronized subscription status and ledger could be adequate for a planning runway; they still do not prove a bank balance. A currently unwired payment-application scaffold poses no present posting risk. Claude should first inspect actual note rows, installed workflows, money labels and call sites at its newer HEAD, then narrow or remove these recommendations if those observations disprove the path. The acceptance cases above are designed to make that disagreement empirical.

### I. An estimated agricultural value becomes a "comparable sale" and a letter price

The blindOfferCalculator.ts refusal for an empty comparison set is a valuable recent correction. Its upstream buildCompDataset, however, appends nassData.pasturePerAcre and a prior-year land-trend value as CompData alongside actual sales. analyzeComps then treats every entry as a sale, uses the minimum as the "lowest comparable sale," counts these entries toward "county validated," and the offer engine prices tiers, projected resale and letterVariables.offerAmount. There is no distinction between a property transaction, an agricultural survey estimate and a modeled trend in the operative comparison set. USDA's Agricultural Land Values series is an average value estimate based mainly on operator survey data, published by broad geography and land category; it is useful market context, not a closed sale of a similar vacant lot.

The deeper failure path is in usdaNassService.ts. When a trend has no API observations, computeLandValueTrend generates five years from a state default with 5% assumed annual appreciation and marks those records source: "estimate". Its returned LandValueTrend.years drops that source; buildCompDataset inserts the prior-year value as source: "usda_nass". Thus no manual sales and no USDA API data can still yield one supposed comp and an ok offer report through the fallback trend. The snapshot separately returns pasturePerAcre: 0 for a no-data result rather than a missing value. In the wizard, that can display "$0/ac" as a USDA pasture benchmark while the synthetic trend is labeled "USDA CAGR." The existing offerAndRankHonesty.test.ts tests a null mocked snapshot, not this real no-key branch. This is a source-traced path, not a claim that a customer received or mailed such an offer. A direct behavioral test must confirm it at Claude's actual HEAD.

The authenticated compute route returns the report. The blind-offer wizard can display its recommended amount, produce a downloadable "firm offer" letter and commit a property decision. Its local OfferReport type omits the server's status: "insufficient_data" variant; calculateOffer simply assigns any response to the report. Correcting the server refusal therefore also requires a clear insufficient evidence mobile state, not a crashed or blank letter step. The commit route accepts client-supplied positive offerAmount and salePrice and recomputes scenario arithmetic, but it does not bind those inputs to the earlier sourced offer report; a server-side decision record could freeze unsupported numbers as if they arose from the wizard. The route does not itself mail a letter, so external sending is not established here.

The tier copy claims roughly one, three or four acceptances in five and calls 25% of the lowest comp a "proven industry standard," without a denominator, cohort, sales funnel or cited evidence. Those numbers are predictions in the customer UI, not measured AcreOS outcomes. Preserve the configurable 20/25/33% pricing heuristics as editable scenarios if useful, but remove the claimed acceptance frequencies until a cohort with mailed, delivered, responded, accepted, closed and profitable outcomes supports them. Acceptance alone is not the objective: a higher response rate can buy worse parcels or consume more diligence and capital. Even five genuine comparable sales do not establish a parcel's access, title, utilities, legal constraints or resale likelihood.

Minimum repair contract: keep survey averages and synthetic trends in distinct typed context; only qualified, dated transaction comps enter the sale-comp statistic, with similarity and rights/access caveats shown. That supports a transparent, owner-edited scenario with the prior labeled; it does not justify naming the prior a sale, presenting a synthetic 5% trend as an observed USDA growth rate, or attaching an unsupported acceptance probability. If a newer head carries provenance end-to-end or the wizard already gates letter creation on genuine transactions, classify this finding fixed there instead of recreating a parallel pricing engine.

## 1. A more precise mature architecture

The supplied handoff's "digital twin" is inspiring but potentially overbroad. AcreOS cannot and need not know all property reality. Its mature unit is a bounded decision representation: enough state to decide, act and steward a particular asset or obligation, with explicit blind spots. The formal object is a decision episode, not a perpetual score.

For each consequential decision, preserve:

subject identity + decision question + feasible alternatives + evidence set/as-of times + unresolved states + economic scenarios + constraints + chosen action/defer + authorizing principal + effect receipt + obligations + observed outcome.

The semantic rules are the product:

1. Identity is provisional when the world is. A county/APN, legal description, geometry, provider ID, ownership chain and parcel split/merge can disagree. Two candidate parcels should not silently merge. A user-visible "same parcel?" decision must be reversible with history.
2. Evidence standing is claim-specific. "County assessor says owner X" is authoritative as to that record on that date, not proof of marketable title. An assessor value is an assessment, not fair market value. A provider's flood map may cover one kind of flood hazard and vintage, not the total risk of water on the lot. This refines the existing Evidence Fabric rather than replacing it.
3. Derived decisions have a reproducible dependency set. If a claim, policy or economic assumption changes, mark the prior decision as made under earlier information; calculate a new version. An AI summary may cite the record but never become the canonical record.
4. Intent is separate from permission. A natural-language request compiles into a preview of candidate effects and open questions. Existing approval/outbox paths should enforce actor, object, recipient, channel, cost, time and idempotency. "Action certificate" is a useful logical contract, not necessarily a new table or service.
5. Attempt is separate from external success. Persist a command and external correlation before retrying. A timeout is unknown outcome until a provider lookup, webhook or human observation resolves it. "Exactly once" cannot be promised across an arbitrary provider; idempotency plus reconciliation is the attainable goal. Temporal's activity guidance and AWS durable execution guidance emphasize repeatable activities and stable external idempotency keys.
6. A receipt creates or resolves a duty only when its semantics warrant it. "Mailer accepted by provider" does not prove delivery. "Payment scheduled" does not prove funds cleared. "Contract sent" does not prove signature. After action, derive the next obligation and exception clock from the actual receipt type.
7. Attention is a projection of consequential state. Today and the founder Letter show what changed and why it matters. Each condition keeps a stable identity so retries update one case rather than minting decisions. Expand for source lineage and history without forcing routine operators into logs.

This can be implemented through existing canonical records and a single end-to-end trace before designing a generic ontology. If there is no consumer of a proposed new field, leave it out.

### Where deeper mathematics actually earns its place

The natural mature design is a constrained sequential decision system, but building a universal optimizer now would encode guessed probabilities as authority. For a particular customer decision at time $t$, AcreOS can represent: a versioned set of credible world states $S_t$ (including explicit unknown and conflict), feasible actions $A_t$ limited by owner permission and cash obligations, observations $O_t$ that could be bought or requested, and consequences $U(a,s)$ across a horizon. The data model need not store a complete probability distribution. A practical rule is: if an action is unsafe across plausible states, hold it; if one option dominates the others across the range, decide without more data; if the choice flips, identify the least costly observation capable of changing it. Howard's information-value theory anchors the last step in decision consequences, not data volume. The customer sees "access verification can change the offer by about this much," not a mathematically ornate confidence badge.

| Value of information | Decide whether to pay for title/access, a comparable sale or a field visit before an offer. | A real decision boundary, a bounded acquisition cost and possible outcomes; otherwise rank by plausible loss avoided. |
| Calibrated prediction or conformal ranges | Repeated, comparable properties or payment cohorts with follow-up and stable definitions. | Enough outcomes, censored cases tracked, regional/time shift checked. Conformal guarantees depend on data assumptions and must not be exported as universal land-market coverage. |
| Controlled experiments and policy learning | Improving a low-risk customer journey with genuine retained customers and observable outcomes. | A defined unit, eligible population, guardrails, independent treatment, observation horizon and actual customer metric. |

The last row is especially concrete. For the lost payment.missed handoff, the safety rule is "never tell the customer a task completed before its effect receipt"; the progress rule is "a persisted unresolved missed-payment condition eventually produces a completed workflow, a durable refusal, or an owner-visible exception." A five-state model—observed → queued → attempted → confirmed/failed/unknown—plus a crash after each transition can reveal the bug without a new orchestration platform. AWS's account of formal methods supports using small specifications to find subtle distributed-system failures; it does not imply AcreOS needs TLA+ across every route. These checks belong at the boundaries where the current source has already demonstrated ambiguity.

### One illustrative contract

An investor asks Pax, "Would an offer of $4,000 on this lot leave room for terms resale?" ## 2. Intelligence: decide what to observe, what to compute and when to defer

The original handoff proposes value-of-information. That becomes powerful only with an explicit choice and consequence model. Let $a \in A$ be a feasible action, $s$ an uncertain world state, $U(a,s)$ the outcome after costs and obligations, $o$ a possible observation, and $C_o$ its acquisition cost. A conceptual expected value of sample information is

[
\operatorname{EVSI}(o)=\sum_y P(y)\max_a\mathbb{E}[U(a,s)\mid y]
-\max_a\mathbb{E}[U(a,s)]-C_o.
]

Ronald Howard's Information Value Theory makes the essential point: uncertainty has value only through the decision and its consequences. In AcreOS, a title/access confirmation may flip "buy" to "reject"; another similarly sourced comp may scarcely move the margin. The interface should show "what would change my answer?" and the cheapest legitimate way to learn it.

But the $P$ and $U$ terms will rarely be estimable accurately at Customer #1. Begin with sensitivity and dominance rules: show whether the decision flips across plausible ranges; identify hard blockers; rank missing facts by potential loss avoided and cost/time to resolve. Do not print a false $137.24 EVSI from thin data. Only later use calibrated probabilities and bounded acquisition budgets. A stopping rule matters: research until the expected information gain falls below its cost or the decision deadline arrives, then decide, defer or decline explicitly.

AI should be selectively useful. A model can extract a deed's candidate legal description, propose questions, compare scenarios or explain a contradiction. It should abstain from certifying access, legal rights, title or compliance on weak evidence. A referral to a person must explain what that person can resolve, how urgent it is, and what the system has already assembled. Research on human–AI selective prediction finds that how deferral is communicated affects the combined team's accuracy. A silent "ask a professional" footer is not a functional handoff.

The learning trap is selective labels. If AcreOS recommends 20 parcels, the investor buys two and later sells one, the system has only one observed return. Neither the rejected 18 nor the unsold one has the comparable outcome. Training a buy/no-buy policy on recorded profits would overestimate or mischaracterize performance. Keep the full candidate funnel, policy recommendation, human override and reason, observe intermediate labels for declines where possible, and label final outcomes as censored or unobserved. Compare recommendations against a prospective expert/concierge baseline; do not claim causal deal profit lift without a defensible design. The selective-labels research and unmeasured-confounding study explain why historical decision logs alone cannot identify counterfactual outcomes.

The current decisionExperiments.ts is a useful assignment and diagnostic scaffold, not yet a business-outcome experiment. It assigns one sticky variant per organization and item type. recordExperimentOutcome writes the first scored decision for that organization into its single assignment row, while analyzeExperiment calls the variant with the highest mean internal outcome score (at least three scores) a leader and may suggest promotion after six outcomes. It does not evaluate the declared successMetric against an actual event such as profitable closed deal, customer task completion or borrower recovery. autonomyHealth.ts can award a score because a similar inbox item did not recur, which does not prove the customer's underlying problem was solved. The example of 7- versus 10-day dunning is especially unsuitable for casual experimentation without counsel and customer authorization. A trusted experiment names its eligible population, randomization unit, treatment, guardrails, observable primary outcome, observation window, missing data and stopping rule before analysis; Kohavi et al. show why assignment and analysis details matter. At early customer scale, small prospective comparisons and falsifiable case studies are more honest than a "winner" badge. Preserve the scaffold and rename its present result as an internal score comparison until those conditions are met.

For forecasts that do have outcomes, preserve the repo's metric-specific calibration. Use prediction intervals or scenario ranges, not one generic confidence percent; score probability questions with suitable proper scores and monitor drift by geography, data provider, strategy and customer cohort. User acceptance is a measure of willingness to act, not truth. Google Research's uncertainty review discusses calibration under dataset shift. With tiny samples, present cases and uncertainty rather than a performance badge.

The supplied Hoffman/Trace material offers useful analogies: the visible interface is a lossy, action-oriented projection; attention is finite; a state estimate must not stand in for unobserved reality. Its consciousness or metaphysical claims do not validate any database, AI or UX mechanism. In AcreOS, the test is whether a compressed view preserves the invariants that matter to a specific decision and allows the owner to inspect the underlying source.

### How improvement earns deployment and additional authority

A further source pass at the same confirmed branch head a2dc971 found useful evaluation machinery with a narrower meaning than general agent competence. evals/run-gate.ts uses curated prompts, a locally mirrored Pax system prompt, shape/topic heuristics and a model tone judge. It enforces a score threshold for live evaluation and explicitly records judgeMode=stub and skips enforcement without an Anthropic key. That explicit skip is sound. It does not execute the production tool-bearing customer journey or inspect its final business records. evals/run-eval.ts similarly evaluates generated responses and optionally conversations. Preserve these as communication regression tests; do not present their passing score as proof that Pax applied a payment, retained tenant isolation or completed an authorized send. This does not establish that no other repository test covers those behaviors; it establishes the scope of these inspected runners.

The τ-bench paper evaluates tool-using conversations using resulting database state and repeated-trial reliability. Its retail/airline tasks and 2024 model scores do not estimate AcreOS performance. The transferable method is to evaluate a task's actual end state, including forbidden changes, over varied conversations. For AcreOS, a model upgrade should be judged on a small set of meaningful tasks through the real orchestration boundary with controlled providers, alongside the existing prose evaluation:

| Task family | Required result | Failure that prose scoring could miss |
|---|---|---|
| Review one parcel | Correct parcel and evidence version; useful uncertainty and next observation | A polished answer about a similarly named parcel or stale access claim |
| Prepare one offer | Exact authorized price, party, parcel set and scope; no send before authority | Friendly prose accompanies the wrong recipient or an unauthorized send |
| Reconcile a buyer payment | Correct contract, allocation and duty; repeat observation causes no duplicate | Assistant says "done" while the balance or follow-up is wrong |
| Recover a provider timeout | Unknown effect retained; reconciliation or safe retry; one unresolved case | Agent sends twice, silently abandons the task, or announces success |
| Resume after an absence | Earlier duties and changed evidence survive session/model replacement | Conversation forgets a deadline or treats its prior summary as fact |

Vary user phrasing, ordering, corrections, cancellation and tool failure; check both normal and adversarial cases. Start with a few repeated runs of the highest-consequence path, with a fixed spend ceiling and cached fixtures. Report counts and failure types, not a premature "99% reliable" badge. Repeated model calls on one fixture are not independent real customers. A critical unauthorized effect or cross-tenant disclosure is not averaged away by excellent tone scores. Tests that never invoke a provider remain laboratory proof; qualification for a real external operation still needs its own supported, authorized observation.

server/utils/sanitizePrompt.ts already consolidates prior duplicate defenses, escapes known patterns, wraps untrusted text and documents false-positive tradeoffs. Keep that work. AgentDojo shows why agents must also be evaluated when instructions arrive inside tool-returned data. Its benchmark is not evidence of an AcreOS exploit. The AcreOS-specific test should put a benign simulated redirection inside a seller note, imported property description or retrieved document and verify that it cannot alter approved recipients, budgets, tenant scope or permissions. Enforce those invariants in the server's typed action path even when the model proposes a bad call. Never add another regex list and call the problem solved. Preserve the original document as evidence; a sanitized model view is a transformation, not a substitute legal record. Truncation/redaction should be visible to the extraction pipeline so a missing clause does not become "none exists."

The mature learning loop has three separate stores of meaning, which may reuse current tables: what happened (versioned observations and outcomes), what we currently predict (models/scenarios with cohorts and limitations), and what is permitted (owner-approved policy). New observations may update predictions and propose a policy change; neither a high model score nor a run of successful tasks grants new authority by itself. For a candidate model/prompt/tool change, compare old and new on frozen cases; add fresh held-out cases derived from real failures; inspect the critical disagreements; run a scoped shadow comparison with no duplicate external effects; then release only within the existing grant. Customer actions and sensitive data are not a free training corpus: cross-customer learning requires a lawful, consented, privacy-preserving design and adequate data rights. No universal shared memory should be created merely because multiple organizations use Pax.

Measure the whole burden before calling intelligence an improvement. For the customer: elapsed time to a trustworthy decision, active minutes, corrections, preventable rework, unresolved obligations and repeat use for the same job. For the founder: support minutes per retained customer, provider/model cost per completed task, incidents and recovery work. Keep satisfaction separate from observed task time. METR's early-2025 randomized study found perceived time savings alongside measured slowdown in its experienced-developer setting; that does not predict today's models or land investors, but it demonstrates why perceived ease alone is insufficient evidence. Instrument only the few journeys being qualified, and compare to the customer's real spreadsheet/manual process rather than an imaginary zero-effort baseline.

The mobile outcome is a brief completion receipt: what changed, which record supports it, whether an external party confirmed it, and the next obligation. Corrections reopen the same case. A task that the system cannot safely finish should arrive with the relevant documents and a precise remaining question, preserving work already done. The founder sees whether the new version reduced interventions without increasing unowned duties; the customer sees a calmer, more dependable task flow. These are the experience and economic returns the learning architecture must earn.

Counterargument: richer evaluation can consume scarce model credits and delay a useful release. Keep prose tests for inexpensive communication checks; use deterministic assertions for money, permissions and records; spend repeated model calls on the few paths whose behavior actually depends on model reasoning. Do not impose a new platform-wide eval campaign. The first useful extension is one existing customer journey with a known failure, a small variation set and an explicit result contract. If the stronger model costs more but reduces review and recovery enough to lower total completion cost, it wins; if an ordinary form or deterministic calculation does the job reliably, no agent needs to intervene.

## 3. The economic object AcreOS uniquely needs to get right

The first investor segment may acquire lots on an upstream payment obligation and resell on downstream installment terms. A conventional CRM's "monthly revenue" and a simple amortization chart can create a dangerous illusion. The same asset has independent obligations on both sides, plus marketing, tax, servicing, refunds, late payments and default risk.

For each asset group, model separately:

• Upstream liability: contractual balance, scheduled due dates, actual cleared payments, payoff rights, title/transfer dependencies, and recourse if downstream proceeds stop.
• Downstream receivable: customer-specific payment terms, principal/interest or other legally characterized amounts, charges, actual collected payments, arrears, cure/notice steps, payoff and servicing provider records.
• Cash: bank-cleared funds, pending processor payout, expected payments, reserved amounts and required future cash; never label a forecast "available."
• Portfolio exposure: several buyer notes can depend on one upstream contract, one county, one platform, one marketing channel, or one owner cash reserve. Bundle economics should track parcel-level allocation and shared upstream duty without double-counting the same obligation.
• Customer duties: accurate statement, transfer/recording promise, taxes/maintenance responsibilities, dispute/refund, permissible communication, and post-payoff fulfillment. Applicable duties vary by instrument and jurisdiction; AcreOS should route review to qualified specialists rather than compile a universal law table from prose.

An example with explicitly hypothetical numbers: four downstream buyers owe $390 a month in aggregate while one upstream seller receives $100. Calling the difference $290 "profit" ignores collection failure, prior acquisition payments, taxes, postage, payment fees, reserves, legal costs and timing. If two buyers stop paying, upstream $100 remains due. A strong Finance surface shows cleared current-period net cash, future contractual inflows/outflows, a downside runway and which facts still need reconciliation. It never markets the projection as guaranteed passive income.

This is also a compelling commercial differentiator if a cohort proves it: decision intelligence and note/obligation continuity for land investors with seller financing. It is narrower and more defensible than "AI real-estate operating system," provided the product does not assume a regulated lender/servicer role merely because it stores schedules. The CFPB has documented serious consumer harm around some contract-for-deed arrangements. Its report focuses on home transactions; applicability to a specific vacant-land, deed or installment structure needs jurisdiction- and instrument-specific counsel. Do not turn this reference into a blanket classification of all land deals.

Product test: import or enter one upstream/downstream pair with a missed downstream payment, a corrected payment, early payoff and a disputed fee. The schedule, cleared cash, account entries, customer-facing statement, due task and founder/customer attention must agree. This likely has greater economic value than another agent or map layer. It also tests the existing double-entry foundation without prematurely advertising a full accounting package.

Source-level shape at the inspected commit: originated notes and acquired acquiredNotes each have a nullable single propertyId; deals has one required property. I did not find a note-to-many-parcels association in the repository code search. financialOSService.calculateDealPnL accepts one loanAmount and total interestPaid, but no upstream due-date series or lender obligation; it cannot answer whether this month's promised buyer collections cover next week's purchase installment. This is evidence of limits in these inspected records, not proof that no separate spreadsheet or contract record exists in production.

There is also a narrow arithmetic defect in that reachable financial P&L route: the owner-finance projection computes totalInterestEarned = totalCollected - (salePrice - downPaymentReceived). Because totalCollected already includes the down payment, that adds it twice relative to totalCollected - salePrice. With a hypothetical $10,000 financed sale, $2,000 down and $9,000 in later installments, the function labels $3,000 as interest rather than the $1,000 difference between $11,000 collected and $10,000 price. Actual contractual interest may require a separate schedule, fees and principal allocation; the example demonstrates the formula's inconsistency, not a tax treatment. The dealPnlSingleOwner.test.ts suite tests agreement on gross/net/ROI, but not this owner-finance projection. No customer use of the route was observed.

Minimal mature object: a versioned asset group binds one acquisition agreement to the parcels it covers and to its upstream payable schedule; separate disposition agreements bind subsets of those parcels to downstream buyer notes; a cash observation belongs to a specified payment and financial account; rights and deed-transfer milestones attach to the relevant agreement. This is a typed relation among existing deal/property/note records plus the missing obligation, not an abstract knowledge graph. It permits multiple parcels on one note and multiple notes under one purchase without pretending either arrangement is a single-property sale. The instrument determines whether payments are principal, interest, fee or something else, with qualified review where necessary.

The decision-relevant cash test is about timing and shortfall. For each plausible scenario $s$ and due date $t$, show

[
C_t(s)=C_0+\sum_{\tau\le t}\bigl(D^{\mathrm{cleared}}{\tau}(s)-U^{\mathrm{due}}{\tau}-T_{\tau}-F_{\tau}-O_{\tau}\bigr),
]

where $D$ is observed or explicitly scenario-dependent downstream collections, $U$ mandatory upstream payments, $T$ taxes, $F$ provider/servicing costs and $O$ other operating cash. The investor chooses a reserve floor $R$; the app reports the earliest date and scenario where $C_t(s)<R$, and which obligation causes it. It does not estimate a precise default probability from a handful of notes. A prospect's offer consumes liquidity even if its modeled ROI is high. The iPhone answer to "Can I buy this parcel?" should show cash required before the first possible resale, next binding due date, downside reserve gap and obligations that remain if a buyer stops paying, with links to source terms. This is the economical center of a land stewardship product; sophisticated optimization can wait for calibrated histories.

### The missing right-to-offer and transfer boundary

A multi-parcel relationship is also about whether AcreOS can truthfully show an asset as available to offer, whose interest is being conveyed, which parcels remain subject to an upstream agreement, and what must happen after a buyer finishes paying. These are separate states and sources: proposed seller terms; executed acquisition agreement; recorded instrument; current title/encumbrance evidence; authority to resell; signed disposition; received payment; contractual payoff; recorded release/deed. A tax-roll owner name, a contract PDF, a provider's "payment succeeded" event and a recorded deed answer different questions. The evidence model should preserve each observation and its effective date. This is a design inference from the asset-group gap, not a claim that AcreOS currently misstates any particular customer's title.

The Arizona Department of Real Estate says a public report can be required before advertising or offering six or more lots in a subdivision, including lots in an existing subdivision. Its FAQ discusses title or equitable interest in six or more lots, including certain lots owned in the prior ten years, and a possible subsequent-owner exemption that must be granted. Its raw-land buyer checklist points to public reports, utility/access disclosures and inspection rights. The owner's earlier five-, seven- and nine-lot Arizona possibilities make this a high-value diligence question, not proof that any particular offer violates the rule. Which lots are in one subdivision, who holds the interest, prior sales, exemptions and the applicable law need case-specific review. Do not encode "five safe, six illegal" from the count alone; nor should a UI invite advertising seven lots with no qualification.

The CFPB's contract-for-deed opinion discusses home sales and further protections when credit is secured by a dwelling. Regulation Z §1026.36 has particular scope and conditional seller-financer provisions for dwelling-secured credit. That does not establish that the same mortgage rules apply to a vacant off-grid lot, or that all vacant-land financing is exempt from other consumer-credit, state contract, disclosure, advertising or collection requirements. Attach a reviewed instrument and use classification to each consequential agreement: jurisdiction, parcel set, property/use facts, party roles, security/title structure, consumer or business purpose where relevant, servicing actor, source documents and review date. Changes to those facts reopen the question. Never infer legal permission from a product recipe or an LLM summary.

Smallest useful transition: at "ready to advertise/send contract," show a transaction-specific qualification state—not assessed, facts incomplete, review needed, cleared for this transaction, or restriction identified—with facts, reviewer and date. A customer can model an incomplete deal; AcreOS should withhold a send-ready assurance until the right and disclosure questions are resolved under a professionally reviewed workflow. Existing listings/contracts need a retrospective, non-destructive exception path. Start with the first real jurisdiction and instrument, not a nationwide legal rules engine.

After a buyer payoff, show what was received, how it was applied, what remains, and who will execute/record the required transfer or release under the actual agreement. The investor needs a due-date case with owner, document, receipt and completion evidence. An expected-schedule "paid in full" cannot become "deed recorded." A servicing transfer or acquired note also changes who receives payments and who owes statements and release duties; mortgage-specific notices apply only when their scope is established. Buyer and investor screens should project the same versioned facts with appropriate privacy separation.

Counterargument: qualification can burden users and create false confidence in stale clearance. Tie it only to consequential offer/contract transitions, show the missing fact rather than legal prose, allow research scenarios before clearance, and expire review when the lot set, instrument or jurisdiction changes. If customers already use qualified title/escrow counsel and never ask AcreOS for send-ready contracts, link their reviewed documents and status rather than adjudicating eligibility inside the app.

## 4. A less burdensome customer and founder experience

### Customer: a decision workspace, not five silos

Keep the present Today · Map · Deals · Finance · Pax navigation as a hypothesis. The binding test is whether the user can move among them without losing the same asset and decision. On an iPhone, a parcel's persistent identity and next step should follow the user: search on Map, open a concise decision sheet, inspect evidence and downside in Deals, see funding impact in Finance, ask Pax "what changed?", then return to the pending action. Inbox is the exceptional queue, not another source of tasks; Settings handles connections and permissions.

The parcel sheet has a small, consistent grammar:

1. Decision line: "Investigate access before making an offer" or "Ready for your decision," with alternative action and consequence. Avoid a pseudo-objective health score.
2. Known / uncertain / disputed: only factors that can change this offer, each with source, vintage and impact. "County tax roll reports X" is different from "Title confirmed."
3. Economics: cash and terms scenarios, downside, capital tied up, cost of next observation, future obligations. A slider is a sensitivity tool only if all assumptions are visible and not silently recomputed.
4. Next step: prepare, approve, wait, decline or seek evidence. Show cost and recipient before effect.
5. History: earlier decision as it was then, new information, approval, external receipt and outcome.

For high-consequence choices, ease should mean the right thought at the right time, not zero friction. Before an offer, AcreOS can ask the investor to confirm the one assumption most likely to reverse the decision ("Is legal access verified, or are we pricing the risk?"), then show the downside if it is false. Routine reversible actions need no such interruption. Research on cognitive forcing in human–AI decisions found reduced overreliance but lower subjective ratings for the most effortful interventions. That tradeoff argues for friction proportional to irreversible consequence, tested with actual land investors; explanatory prose alone is not a safety mechanism.

Pax should be ambient and object-aware, but not the only place to find a consequential result. Any AI answer that creates a duty or decision becomes a durable object on Today/Deal/Finance. Chat history is conversational continuity; it is not the asset's ledger. The command palette can compile typed intents for experienced desktop use, while the mobile surface offers the same context-specific actions directly.

For a terms-resale customer, the asset-group view should feel simpler than the underlying contracts. Its phone-sized opening card says, for example, "Two buyer payments expected this month; the upstream $85 is due on the 12th; one buyer payment is unobserved; minimum reserve under the missed-payment scenario is $X below your chosen floor." The $X is computed only from actual recorded terms and labeled scenario assumptions. One tap reveals which parcels and agreements share that upstream duty, which buyer payment was actually cleared, and the next document or service deadline. A different tap answers "If buyer A stops, what must I still pay?" This uses Deals and Finance as one contextual journey, not a sixth navigation destination. A borrower-facing statement, if AcreOS generates one, must use the same payment event and contract version, while keeping investor-only cost and risk analysis private.

Offline capability should be selective: recently opened parcel facts, captured field notes/photos pending upload and a read-only duty list can help in weak coverage. External sends, payments, authorization and "verified current" assertions require fresh server/provider state. The local-first research motivates data ownership and responsive offline work, but does not require converting AcreOS's financial and authority state into a conflict-free replicated system.

### Founder: two kinds of readiness

"Can I step away?" needs two separate answers:

• Platform readiness: release, database restore, provider connections, cost limits, paging, jobs and incidents.
• Customer-duty readiness: unresolved commitments, pending external effects of unknown status, payment/notification deadlines, disputes, unserved requests and a person or fallback who can intervene.

The current stepAwayReadiness is a promising platform reader; it must not become a global absence certificate by inference. A failed DR drill should not be ready, and no-drill/unknown provider/customer duty should not produce "every system is armed." A concise founder Letter can say: "Platform operating; recovery cutover unproven. Two customer duties need coverage before Friday. Your absence runway is limited by the earlier of these deadlines." Deeper checks stay inspectable. The owner decides whether to delegate, pause new actions, or attend to the duty.

Founder attention is a scarce economic input. Measure minutes spent per week on true irreversible decisions, avoidable technical administration, repeated alerts and routine customer support. The supplied report's four attention classes are useful if a durable condition key makes them causal rather than a classification of every event. The Google SRE error-budget policy shows how measured reliability can change release behavior; AcreOS should first instrument a few customer journeys, not copy a large SRE program before customer traffic exists.

The founder's customer-duty view should aggregate exceptions by accountable customer and deadline, not expose the customer's private asset details by default. "One customer statement failed to send; provider accepted none; deadline Thursday" is useful. "Every customer is green" from a skipped job, an internal ledger estimate or a synthetic decision score is not. The founder needs to know whether the product delivered its promise, what intervention is actually required and whether a reversible pause of new commitments would protect existing customers. The customer needs the parallel view of their own contract, money, next action and evidence. Those are two projections of the same operational facts, with different permissions and purposes.

## 5. Trust boundaries and proof without a proof bureaucracy

The earlier report proposes a Proof Lattice service. That may be premature. A lighter contract is a proof receipt attached to each critical claim: capability name; version/commit; environment; population; method; mock/sandbox/live provider; run/skipped/fail/pass/inconclusive; timestamp; artifact; expiry; owner. A release UI can aggregate existing CI and provider receipts. It should say "unconfigured" for the skipped browser job, not turn GitHub green into a journey pass. Introduce a service only if multiple existing consumers require one.

The same distinction governs release: a green typecheck, a simulated E2E run, an authenticated staging journey, a production canary and a retained customer are different claims. Migrations should be tested through the production migration chain, not only schema push. A restore receipt should distinguish a scratch DB restore from a full cutover meeting RTO/RPO. Database row-level security can add a second isolation boundary, but PostgreSQL documentation notes owner and BYPASSRLS behavior; a poorly configured policy is not a substitute for tenant-scoped queries. Begin with a real two-tenant golden journey and the sensitive tables it touches.

For every AI and provider operation, record the business trace from UI intent to receipt with privacy-minimal metadata. A request ID alone does not prove an external effect. A model grader alone does not prove factuality. NIST AI RMF recommends task-context risk mapping and evaluation informed by affected actors; here that means testing whether an investor can identify a wrong parcel claim, avoid an unsafe action and resolve a due obligation, not merely whether Pax sounds polished.

Immediate source-level correction candidates, if unchanged at implementation head:

1. Scope or disable semantic reuse for dynamic/high-consequence tasks. Preserve exact cache for demonstrably immutable low-risk tasks, with explicit state fingerprints. Test two similar wordings that differ in task type, parcel evidence, policy version, authorization and time. Show a red test before repair.
2. Fix the DR drill state mapping: a recent recorded passedRtoTarget=false is failed/attention, not ready. Test the headline and overall verdict's precise scope; do not claim the entire step-away reader is fixed by one boolean.
3. Reclassify scheduled browser job conclusions as skipped/unconfigured in the founder proof view and, if customer launch depends on it, the release admission gate. Use the existing audit-unconfigured action; avoid a new proof subsystem.
4. Revisit source standing for parcel.owner_name and parcel.legal_description only where the customer UI or Pax currently treats those claims as present-day title truth. Introduce predicate-specific qualification and a source-backed correction path, not a wholesale evidence migration.

## 6. Economic positioning and why complexity must earn its keep

There are three viable strategic futures:

| Future | When it wins | When to narrow or stop |
|---|---|---|
| **Focused land decision tool** | Parcels become decision-ready faster than maps/spreadsheets, data spend stays low, customers pay and return. | If accuracy/licensing cost overwhelms value or data products already meet the need, integrate with them instead. |
| **Land operating and note-stewardship system** | Continuity from evidence to terms resale and obligations prevents costly omissions and reduces administration over months. | If customers only use lookup, or servicing/legal/support burden dwarfs subscription price, sell a focused wedge with integrations. |
| **Universal strategy kernel** | A second strategy reuses identity, evidence, economics, authority and obligations at low marginal cost and wins independent customers. | If each vertical needs a parallel service, bespoke UI, provider contracts and counsel, diversification is really fifteen businesses. |

The second is my current favorite, entered through the first; the third is a contingent expansion. Existing marketplace/data/CRM specialists make "we have every feature" a poor differentiation. What matters is an observable improvement to one investor's full workflow and retained willingness to pay. The founder can run a concierge comparator with existing tools to learn what AcreOS uniquely improves. Customer acquisition and onboarding cost, not model token cost alone, may dominate the economics.

The right unit is contribution per retained customer over a cohort: cash collected less refunds, payments/data/AI/postage costs, direct support/onboarding, and a reasonable allocation for shared infrastructure and founder attention. Deal returns belong to the customer, not AcreOS platform revenue. The app can help the customer assess economic outcomes without claiming it caused those returns. A platform that costs the founder more time than it saves customers is not yet an autonomous business.

For the investor, the mature allocator should consider capital at risk, obligations and option value across the portfolio, not maximize modeled deal ROI one parcel at a time. A minimal monthly stress table can show cleared opening cash; mandatory upstream payments, taxes, insurance and servicing; plausible buyer collections under late/default/prepay scenarios; available reserve; and months until a shortfall. The binding constraint is often the worst due-date overlap, not the average monthly spread. Correlation matters: several notes tied to one acquisition contract or one county are fewer independent risks than their note count suggests. A prospective offer consumes both cash and the ability to pursue a better opportunity later. Do not publish an optimal portfolio weight from a handful of transactions. Use explicit owner limits on minimum reserve, maximum county/provider exposure and tolerated shortfall, then compare scenarios and actual outcomes. Only add statistical optimization when longitudinal data can calibrate its assumptions.

## 7. Development sequence: five decisive gates, no ten-campaign prerequisite

Each gate is a customer or founder truth that can fail. Work on one highest-consequence case at a time and re-evaluate head/production before action.

| Gate | Minimum proof and stop condition |
|---|---|
| **1. Honest admission and financial outputs** | Reconcile the reachable 1099 direction and zero annual totals at current HEAD; any tax-form output must be qualified for the actual payer/recipient/instrument or refused. A land customer can pay only for operative access; entitlement and Beta claims agree; two organizations cannot cross-read critical deal, document or finance data. Block real charging/onboarding or tax exports on any reproduced material failure of the corresponding promise. |
| **2. Trustworthy parcel decision** | One real parcel and one rejected parcel: stable identity; transaction comps separate from agricultural estimates and synthetic trends; sourced facts; visible conflict/unknown; downside economics; versioned go/no-go; evidence or policy changes do not silently rewrite the earlier decision. With no qualified sale comps, the blind-offer path cannot produce a firm recommended amount or letter price. A truthful conditional answer is still a pass. |
| **3. Authorized effect and continuing duty** | One consented, permitted external action: typed preview, recipient/cost/budget, effective grant, stable idempotency key, provider receipt/unknown outcome, retry/reconcile, and next obligation. A refused provider, a lost workflow handoff and an owner cancellation do not appear as success. |
| **4. Paired financing and stewardship** | One upstream/downstream portfolio case with partial/late/early payments and one dispute: schedules, actual deposits, ledger, dues, reserves, customer statement and attention agree. The due-date scanner cannot label a payment overdue on its due day or silently lose a payment-missed workflow. Jurisdiction/instrument review is explicit where needed. |
| **5. Repeatable business and founder absence** | Independent retained customers complete and return to the loop; support/cost/attention measured; mobile Safari cold/warm journey measured; DR restore and release proof receipt current; founder can leave for a bounded period without unowned duties. Liquidity observed in an account is distinguished from modeled runway, and unknown is not green. If not, narrow the promise before building Strategy Pack #2. |

These are not a claim that every gate must be a giant project. A contained customer case can expose one missing seam. The old campaign's router zero-bypass, universal certificate and generalized meta-policy are valuable end states only when the paths they govern exist and need consolidation. The direct-call ratchet already prevents growth; migrating 82 paths before Customer #1 has weaker priority than a proven semantic-cache error or an inert paid pack.

## 8. Continuation update: from a capable platform to a complete operating environment

The later repository pass changed the emphasis again. AcreOS's remaining problem is no longer best described as a missing-feature problem. It is a continuity, completeness and convergence problem.

The repository already contains far more machinery than a normal land-investing CRM: property intelligence, campaign and direct-mail systems, communications, offers, contracts, e-sign primitives, title-partner rails, seller-finance notes, accounting, provider intelligence, mobile shells, offline machinery, founder autonomy, Pax, Solene and a large body of self-auditing code. The next leap is to make those parts behave as one operating environment in which the user thinks about the property, counterparty, deal, money or objective rather than about AcreOS's internal module boundaries.

The practical product test is now:

> **Can a new investor begin with only an objective, remain inside AcreOS through market selection, sourcing, outreach, negotiation, diligence, closing, disposition, financing and servicing, and always understand what the system knows, what it does not know, what happened and what should happen next?**

For the founder, the parallel test is:

> **Can the company run for a bounded period with the founder seeing only the few irreducible decisions that genuinely require owner judgment, while every important claim remains inspectable through evidence and receipts?**

This section records the latest verified gaps and the architecture that best closes them without turning the codebase into another generation of parallel systems.

### 8.1 The first major continuity gap is the top of the funnel

AcreOS is strong once a real lead/property/deal exists. The customer can research, communicate, mail, model terms, create offers, contract, order title, originate notes and service finance. But the zero-to-first-deal path still has a major break at the point where a first-time operator asks:

> **Where do my first real candidate parcels come from?**

The current Acquisition Sprint playbook still assumes a customer can identify a county and then pull/download a tax-delinquent or similar source list. The apparent automated alternative in server/jobs/countyAssessorIngest.ts is not a current solution. The worker code defines a BullMQ queue and nightly registration function, but repository search found no caller that creates/registers/runs that queue. More importantly, the tax-delinquent acquisition function is still a stub that returns an empty list when no real source is configured. The repository's own public-claims tests and landing-page truth notes already acknowledge that the old "define the buy-box and the first list pulls overnight" promise describes an engine that does not exist.

This is not a reason to invent tax-delinquency data. It is a reason to make sourcing itself a first-class, evidence-aware journey.

#### Market Launchpad

The better entry point is a Market Launchpad inside Map, not another top-level lead-generator product.

A beginner should be able to tell Pax something like:

> "I have $5,000, I want to begin with rural land, and I prefer recurring seller-finance income to pure cash flips."

AcreOS turns that into an inspectable Operating Mandate, then helps the customer establish a market using data it can actually source and classify:

• parcel and owner-record coverage;
• recent land transaction evidence;
• typical parcel sizes and assessed values;
• population / permit / demand indicators where properly sourced;
• environmental and terrain coverage;
• contactability / mailing-address availability;
• county GIS reliability and license posture;
• likely provider cost per investigated parcel;
• explicit gaps such as unavailable tax-delinquency data.

The output should not be an opaque county score. It should be a decision-readiness explanation:

> "AcreOS can currently build a general absentee-owner acreage universe here. It cannot honestly build a tax-delinquent universe because no verified delinquency source is connected for this county."

That gives the beginner a truthful path forward rather than a synthetic answer.

#### Acquisition Universe / List Definition

"Build my first list" should become a core business verb. A natural-language request such as:

> "5–20 acres, absentee owner, owned 10+ years, under $25k assessed value, prioritize out-of-state owners, and exclude material flood conflicts."

should compile to a deterministic, inspectable List Definition rather than direct LLM-generated database logic. The List Definition should preserve the query, geography, filter semantics, evidence requirements, source versions, exclusions, ranking strategy and timestamp. It should be possible to answer months later:

> "Why was this seller ever contacted?"

by tracing the contact back to the exact acquisition thesis, source evidence and campaign that selected the parcel.

This also implies a cleaner funnel ontology:

Market → Candidate Parcel → Prospect → Contactable Opportunity → Engaged Counterparty → Qualified Opportunity → Deal.

A parcel should not become a CRM "lead" merely because it matched a broad screen. That distinction keeps Deals useful even when AcreOS evaluates tens of thousands of candidate parcels.

### 8.2 Onboarding should remain short; business commissioning should become intelligent

The current onboarding-v2.tsx is deliberately optimized for roughly 45–60 seconds to first workspace. It asks for workspace/business type, then sample data or import, and reveals the app. It is tailored, skippable and re-runnable. That is good account onboarding.

It is not yet sufficient as the full experience for a person who knows little about property investing and expects AcreOS to start the business with them.

Do not solve this by turning signup into a twenty-question wizard. Split the problem:

Account onboarding stays extremely short.

Business commissioning happens progressively after entry.

The commissioning system establishes an Operating Mandate over time: objective, available capital, preferred strategy, initial geography, time commitment, reserve constraints, cash-versus-cash-flow preference, communication identity and desired level of assistance. It asks only the next question whose answer materially changes the next recommendation or action.

This is the same "next best observation" principle used for parcel due diligence, applied to the operator.

Sample data should also be explicitly treated as Demo Mode, not business progress. Demo data can teach the interface, but commercial activation should be tied to real operating milestones such as first real parcel examined, first real decision frozen, first real outreach approved, first real seller response, first offer, first contract, first closing, first financed sale and first collected payment.

### 8.3 The Journey Compiler should become the intelligence layer above Strategy Packs

The repository already contains playbooks such as Acquisition Sprint, Due Diligence and Disposition Launch. Today many steps are still page/checklist oriented: navigate somewhere, manually mark a task complete, move to the next page.

The mature form is a Journey Graph whose nodes are defined by business state, not page visits. Each node should declare:

• objective;
• preconditions;
• required evidence;
• deterministic satisfaction predicate;
• available native/provider/manual actions;
• authority requirements;
• failure/degradation path;
• next-node conditions.

A diligence step such as "verify legal access" should not complete because the user checks a box. It completes because the relevant Requirement is satisfied by sufficient Evidence under the active Strategy Pack or is explicitly waived by an authorized person.

The Journey Compiler combines:

Operating Mandate + Strategy Pack + current canonical state + Evidence Fabric + authority + provider availability

and answers:

> **What should happen next for this particular operator?**

That is how a beginner and an experienced land operator can use the same product without separate "basic" and "advanced" applications. The beginner gets explanation at the moment of work; the experienced operator gets compression.

### 8.4 "Never leave AcreOS" should mean continuity, not provider reinvention

The strongest interpretation of the product promise is:

> **Every important business objective begins inside AcreOS, remains observable inside AcreOS, and returns its result to AcreOS.**

It does not mean AcreOS must become a title company, bank, e-signature trust service, email host or accounting package.

Every journey step should be classified as exactly one of:

• native — AcreOS executes the work itself;
• provider-orchestrated — AcreOS invokes a verified external provider and tracks the result;
• guided external — an irreducible human/professional step happens elsewhere, but AcreOS explains the task and waits for evidence;
• unavailable — AcreOS cannot currently perform or establish the step and says so.

This produces a measurable internal No Dead Ends contract. A normal journey should never collapse into "go Google this, maintain a spreadsheet, remember to come back."

### 8.5 The Connector Fabric needs to become canonical infrastructure

The current connector surface contains a concrete trust defect. The Pax connector catalog describes OAuth-style connections for Google services, but the generic connector connect path can create a row with status: connected even when no OAuth authorization/token exists. The later executor then looks for an access token and cannot actually use the supposed connection. A separate connection-test path can report unverified despite adapters existing elsewhere.

Meanwhile the native mailbox rail is materially better: it normalizes Gmail and Outlook behind one server abstraction, uses fresh OAuth access from Clerk rather than storing provider tokens in AcreOS, and handles provider-specific pagination/safety differences.

The correct convergence is a shared Connector Fabric, not separate "Pax connectors," mailbox connectors and ad hoc provider integrations.

A connector should move through explicit states such as:

planned → connectable → authorizing → connected_unverified → verified → degraded → reauthorization_required → revoked.

"Connected" should mean AcreOS authenticated successfully, knows the external account identity/scopes and has exercised a harmless provider capability. Pax gets tools from verified capabilities, not from the presence of a row.

For providers that support synchronization, use the provider's native incremental model rather than repeatedly polling whole collections. Google Calendar documents initial + sync-token incremental synchronization; Gmail documents full/partial synchronization using history IDs and push-triggered partial sync; Microsoft Graph documents delta queries and change notifications. A mature connector therefore owns:

initial snapshot → provider cursor → push/change hint → incremental reconciliation → periodic repair → health/proof state.

A minimal External Mirror can relate important provider objects back to canonical AcreOS objects without copying the entire external service: provider, external ID, revision/etag, cursor, last synchronization, status, local relationship and content fingerprint where useful.

### 8.6 The rails that most directly close business exits

The next integrations should be prioritized by journey continuity, not logo count.

Communication Rail. Gmail/Outlook + SMS + direct mail + eventual phone should project into one counterparty relationship. The seller should not "live" in four channel apps.

Calendar Rail. Google/Microsoft events should become external mirrors related to closings, follow-ups and inspections.

Document Rail. Drive/OneDrive can remain external content owners while AcreOS tracks canonical document relationships, versions and relevant extracted evidence.

Signature Rail. AcreOS prepares/version-locks documents and orchestrates a qualified signing provider where appropriate. The current native HMAC signing primitives remain useful for bounded cases, but provider ceremony should not be reinvented merely to avoid integration work.

Closing Rail. The existing title-partner infrastructure is substantive and should be hardened rather than replaced. Title-order sends should become durable outbox work, and title-specific events should use title-specific semantics rather than reusing a "deal closed" event because it is nearby.

Accounting / Bank Reconciliation Rail. AcreOS's double-entry ledger should remain operational truth. QuickBooks or a bank-feed provider can be a synchronization/reconciliation rail, not the source of truth. Imported transactions become reconciliation candidates; they do not silently create accounting truth.

Listing Rail. Listing syndication should be based on a canonical Listing Package translated by verified provider adapters. If a provider only supports a feed, portal upload or manual handoff, AcreOS should say so. Unverified assumed REST endpoints must not be marketed as automatic integrations.

### 8.7 E-sign is structurally careful but still has a continuity escape

The current signing path has several good properties: document preview, frozen review hash, explicit review confirmation and bounded signing links. But requesting signatures can still end with the operator receiving raw signer links that must be copied into an external email/SMS tool.

The mature flow is:

generate links → choose Communication Rail → preview exact recipients/message → human approval where required → send → receipt → open/sign events → completed document becomes canonical evidence.

This preserves the human approval boundary while eliminating needless app-switching.

### 8.8 Voice is a real operating gap; rebuild only the professional rail

The old AI voice/call-routing pipeline has been removed; former endpoints now return Gone. Current repository search found no live outbound twilio.calls.create equivalent. That means a customer can email, text and mail from AcreOS but still needs another system for an ordinary seller call.

For a true all-day operating environment, voice belongs on the roadmap, but the first version should be intentionally conservative:

• tap seller → call;
• customer-owned phone identity;
• inbound call routing/matching;
• provider receipt and call start/end log;
• optional recording under appropriate configuration/consent;
• post-call transcript/summary;
• proposed follow-up extraction;
• unmatched inbound calls routed to Inbox.

Pax can prepare and summarize. Do not begin by resurrecting autonomous voice negotiation.

### 8.9 Communication and operating history should converge into a Business Chronicle

dealTrack.ts already demonstrates the right instinct: it assembles offers, inbound seller communications, campaign responses and mail events into one story rather than forcing the customer to inspect source tables.

This should mature into a Business Chronicle: a common event envelope for meaningful occurrences without rewriting AcreOS as a fully event-sourced application.

An event can carry:

• event identity/type;
• occurred-at and recorded-at time;
• actor;
• organization;
• related objects;
• cause/correlation/journey;
• source system/record;
• policy/version;
• receipt/evidence references.

This is especially compatible with object-centric process thinking. OCEL 2.0 is useful as a conceptual reference because an event can relate to multiple objects and object-to-object relationships can be explicit. AcreOS need not adopt OCEL wholesale; the relevant insight is that a seller response may simultaneously involve a Counterparty, Lead/Opportunity, Parcel, Campaign, Mail Piece and Deal rather than being forced into one "case."

Once this Chronicle is sufficiently complete, AcreOS can add Process Intelligence rather than another dashboard: actual median time between mail delivery and response, where offers stall, which title partners create delay, which steps are repeatedly skipped, and where real workflow diverges from the Strategy Pack. These observations must remain descriptive unless causal assumptions support stronger claims.

### 8.10 Customer information architecture should become more object-centric and less route-centric

The five customer doors remain the correct compression boundary:

Today · Map · Deals · Finance · Pax, with Inbox and Settings outside the primary bottom-navigation model.

The current mobile implementation renders the five doors plus a visually equivalent More item. That weakens the doctrine perceptually. More/search/settings are actions/utilities rather than a sixth conceptual area. Apple's current HIG distinguishes top-level navigation from tool/action controls; this supports keeping the primary bar literally limited to top-level areas and moving overflow/search to toolbar/chrome.

The same principle applies inside objects. Desktop Parcel detail still exposes implementation-oriented peer tabs such as Due Diligence, Financial, Land Status, Subdivision, ARV and Actions. A user who is not subdividing should not see Subdivision as an equal architectural concept. A raw-land investor should not have to understand why ARV exists because a different vertical needs it.

The more mature object workspace is stable at a higher conceptual level:

Summary — what this thing is and what matters now.

Evidence — what is known, unknown, conflicting, stale or sufficient.

Work — decisions, tasks, documents, communications and authorized actions.

Strategy-specific depth appears contextually without changing the basic mental model.

Mobile already points toward this calmer pattern, but some actions still escape to external/navigation surfaces—for example opening Apple Maps as a primary parcel action. "View on AcreOS Map" should be primary; "Open in Apple Maps" belongs in overflow.

### 8.11 False completeness is now a cross-cutting architecture defect class

Three separate scale findings turned out to be one problem.

Map currently requests only the first 100 properties/deals in the working branch. The user can therefore see a normal-looking map that silently omits the rest of the organization.

Today composes parts of its active queue by reading whole organization populations through storage methods that are deliberately capped at 5,000. The cap logs when truncated, but the operator does not see that the supposedly complete decision queue was computed from only part of the population.

Pax get_system_context uses the same style: all leads/properties/deals/notes/tasks/campaigns are loaded and aggregated in Node, subject to the same whole-list caps; the result is cached for 60 seconds in a per-process in-memory Map.

These are not independent performance bugs. They violate a missing semantic contract.

#### Population Completeness Contract

Every collection/projection should explicitly be one of:

• complete — all qualifying records represented;
• page — one explicit page with continuation;
• viewport — complete for a stated spatial viewport/zoom;
• top_n — explicitly ranked/truncated;
• sample — deliberate sample;
• unknown/incomplete — completeness cannot be established.

No screen or AI context should visually/semantically imply "complete" merely because a silent LIMIT prevented an expensive query.

Map should move to viewport/bounds queries and scale the representation with zoom: aggregates/clusters at wide scale, points closer in, authoritative parcel geometry when selected. MapLibre's current large-data guidance explicitly recommends clustering for large GeoJSON point sets; vector tiles become the later scale step when measurement justifies them.

### 8.12 Today, Pax and Solene need shared Operational Projections

The fix for the 5,000-row problem is not a larger limit. It is a better read model.

Today should query only bounded decision-relevant populations: pending approvals, overdue tasks, delinquent notes, stalled qualified opportunities, unresolved material evidence, failed/ambiguous outward actions, provider degradation that affects active work, and newly measurable outcomes.

Pax should not receive 5,000 raw leads to answer "who should I follow up with?" It should call an inspectable deterministic query or receive a BusinessProjection.

The mature read layer can expose derived projections such as:

• BusinessProjection — current operating condition;
• AttentionProjection — what warrants human attention;
• ParcelProjection — decision-relevant parcel view;
• DealProjection — transaction state and obligations;
• CounterpartyProjection — relationship + communication state;
• FinanceProjection — money/ledger/obligation state;
• FounderProjection — company condition.

These are not new truth stores. They are versioned, bounded projections over canonical truth. Each should carry asOf, completeness/proof state, material unknowns and source revision where practical.

Pax's current client-side rail context is too thin to support mature reasoning, but the answer is not to send browser state into prompts. The browser identifies the object; the server resolves an authoritative Context Capsule from the same projections the UI uses. Solene should have a parallel Company Context Capsule.

This creates a crucial consistency property:

> **The interface and the AI cannot disagree merely because each assembled a different version of reality.**

### 8.13 Search is already moving in the right direction; its index contract needs to catch up

The latest working branch is more mature than older audit notes suggest. The command palette now prefers server /api/search results when available and uses client-side filtering only as an in-flight fallback. That means the earlier claim "the FTS service is completely unwired" is stale for this branch.

However, the query/index seam still deserves verification. Current fullTextSearch.ts constructs to_tsvector('simple', unaccent(...)) expressions and includes fields such as property APN and joined deal/property text. The original GIN migration indexed older/different to_tsvector('english', ...) expressions. PostgreSQL expression indexes are useful when the indexed expression corresponds to the query expression, and PostgreSQL continues to document GIN as the preferred index type for full-text search.

The next step is therefore not Elasticsearch. It is:

1. define one canonical/versioned Search Document expression per searchable entity;
2. make index and query use the same expression;
3. prove the query plan with EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS) at realistic tenant sizes;
4. use pg_trgm/normalized phone matching where FTS is a poor fit;
5. expand Search to more canonical objects as needed.

Search/⌘K can then become AcreOS's invisible expert interface: find any seller, parcel, note, payment, document, title order or decision and immediately invoke a contextual action without adding navigation.

A narrower failure must be repaired before this becomes the customer's universal entrance. At the pinned branch, fullTextSearch.search catches any failure in its three SQL reads and calls fallbackSearch. That fallback refers to quoted "organizationId", "firstName" and "lastName", whereas the leads schema names the physical columns organization_id, first_name and last_name; the first fallback query can fail and an empty catch then returns []. The authenticated /api/search route emits that as an ordinary 200 {results: [], total: 0}, so the palette cannot distinguish a genuinely absent parcel from failed search. This is a source-derived failure path, not a measured production incident. It joins the index mismatch above: migration 0010 indexes english with different fields; the service searches simple with unaccent and joined text. PostgreSQL documents that a text-search expression index needs a corresponding query expression and configuration (tables and indexes); merely having a GIN index does not prove this query uses it. Do not blindly wrap unaccent() in a new expression index: the index expression's function-volatility requirements and the chosen normalization scheme need explicit design (CREATE INDEX).

Bounded proof: preserve the existing endpoint and palette. Seed two organizations and a realistic lead/parcel/deal population; confirm a known APN, accented name, phone and deal can be found with tenant isolation, and an actually missing identifier returns a true empty state. Inject a primary SQL error and a fallback error separately: the API must report search unavailable with a retry path and an observable incident when it cannot answer, never total: 0 as though it searched. If a fallback is retained, use actual physical columns and decide which entity types it covers; otherwise fail clearly. Measure the route and iPhone palette at a representative larger tenant with EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS), bounded result count, p50/p95 latency and an explicit timeout budget before index changes. A partial local cache can say "recent items" but not "no matching record." This search slice becomes urgent if a real operator cannot find a known record or the latency budget fails; it does not outrank an already reachable money, borrower or notice defect. Counterargument: replacing the service with a separate search platform could make broad entity search easier. Until real volume, relevance or availability evidence defeats a repaired PostgreSQL search, that increases synchronization and operating cost without proving more customer value.

### 8.14 Offline support should converge into one local-first field contract

AcreOS has more offline infrastructure than a casual review suggests. The service worker maintains an IndexedDB-backed mutation queue; a separate offline hook also contains queueing, retry/idempotency and cached entity logic. Repository search suggests the hook's queue path is largely unwired while service-worker interception is the live path.

Do not maintain two synchronization architectures. Converge on one offline mutation envelope containing organization, actor, operation, canonical object reference, payload, stable idempotency key, base revision/precondition, retry state and sync result.

Then create explicit Field Packs:

> "Download this parcel/deal for field use."

A pack should contain the minimum useful offline context: geometry, key evidence, diligence questions/tasks, selected documents, contact details and working notes. Photos, GPS points, voice notes and observations recorded offline should show transparent state:

on_device → queued → uploaded → incorporated → verified/conflict.

The goal is not "every feature works offline." It is resilient, trustworthy field work in rural conditions.

### 8.15 Long-running and bulk external work must leave the request/response cycle

Some AcreOS subsystems, especially direct mail, already use durable queued work. That pattern should become universal for anything expensive, high-volume or externally consequential.

A buyer blast that sends hundreds of messages inside the initiating HTTP request can create ambiguous user experience: many effects may succeed even if the 30-second request times out. Retrying can then duplicate work.

Use one universal bulk pattern:

preview → commit immutable work intent → return accepted → worker claims bounded batches → per-item idempotency/action record → provider receipt → progress stream → reconciliation → terminal summary.

This should govern large campaigns, buyer blasts, bulk enrichment, listing syndication, document sends, large imports and batch AI analysis. A user should never stare at a spinner while AcreOS performs hundreds of irreversible external effects.

#### A scale cycle: a filtered lead list must describe the whole result set

The current branch has already repaired an older unbounded list defect: routes-leads.ts now performs stage-filtered pagination and cursor reads in SQL. Do not revive the older audit's assertion that every /api/leads/paginated request loads all leads. The remaining mismatch is in the leads page: it sends page, size and text query to useLeadsPaginated, then applies stage, assignee and GIS filters and score sorting only to that page, while it still displays the server's unfiltered total. The hook and route already support stage and assignedTo parameters, but this page does not pass them. Thus a matching lead on a later page can be absent from the filtered current page, and the displayed count can refer to a different universe. A page-level score sort is not the highest-score ordering of all matches. This is source-level behavior, not a measured customer session. GIS predicates may need separate qualification before being moved into SQL; until then, label their scope honestly rather than saying "no matches" for the full portfolio.

There is a second, related ceiling in useLeads. The legacy compatibility hook requests 250 leads per cursor page and stops after 40 pages, describing that as a 10,000-lead bound. The current /api/leads/paginated route caps limit at 100, so that walk can contain at most 4,000 leads. It also sends up to 40 sequential client requests to build a flat array for older consumers. Search, dashboard or decision logic deriving organization-wide answers from that array can become incomplete or slow without knowing it. Do not solve this by lifting the server cap to 250 across the application; that could increase payload, scoring and database cost. Replace organization-wide claims with dedicated bounded count/aggregate/search queries at the consumers that need them, and treat a capped client cache as a recent or partial view.

One controlled acceptance journey: seed an organization with 4,001 leads, with the only matching hot/unassigned lead and highest score beyond the first page; include another organization with an identical name. On desktop and iPhone, text + stage + assignee must find the correct organization-wide result, the count and ordering must describe the same filtered set, and a page that is empty while later matches exist must never say "no matching leads." A GIS filter either has an appropriately scoped server predicate or explicitly says "filtering this page" and offers a route to a complete query. A downstream Today/focus/palette count that cannot inspect all 4,001 must disclose partial coverage or use a bounded server projection. Record request count, payload, p95 navigation time and database plan at a representative larger tenant; do not declare a universal 10,000-lead capability from the hook's comment. First qualify the actual design partner's volume and workflow, then repair the reachable screen/reader. This is a correctness and latency slice, not a new pagination framework.

### 8.16 Founder experience should remain the same architecture at company scale

The four founder doors are already real and more mature than the early reports imply:

Letter · Decisions · Controls · Story.

The later repository pass confirmed the Letter reads from a Solene brief endpoint; founder Decisions has active decision/ask infrastructure; Controls has real enforcement surfaces; Story/receipts are part of the documented founder experience. The founder UI should therefore be improved through these four doors rather than adding another cockpit.

The same logic used for customers should apply to the company:

• company reality and proof feed a FounderProjection;
• Solene compresses it into the Letter;
• Decisions contains only irreducible owner judgment;
• Controls defines the operating envelope;
• Story proves causality and shows receipts.

The optimization target is not "how many autonomous actions occurred." It is how little founder attention is needed without creating unowned obligations or unverifiable claims.

A green day should legitimately say "Nothing needs you." An unavailable release proof, provider uncertainty or unowned obligation must never be silently converted to green.

### 8.18 Five invariants should guide the next development campaign

Instead of organizing the next wave around another feature inventory, organize it around defect classes that cut across the entire product.

No false completeness. A capped/page/sample/viewport result never masquerades as the full population.

No dead-end journeys. Every Strategy Pack step is native, provider-orchestrated, guided external or explicitly unavailable.

No unverified capability. A connector, source or provider adapter cannot be presented as working until the corresponding external capability has been verified.

No durable work inside a fragile request. Long-running/bulk/external work is queued, idempotent, observable and reconcilable.

No duplicate representation of truth. UI, Pax and Solene consume shared canonical projections/context rather than independently rebuilding state.

These invariants are more valuable than another wave of route creation because they remove whole families of future defects.

### 13.1 The atomic product promise is a completed business move

The first decisive proof should be one ordinary, sourced path: choose a supported market → create a candidate universe from a qualified source → inspect one property and unresolved facts → model terms → authorize one permitted action → capture the external result → schedule the next obligation. Mark unavailable data and provider steps as such. A guided external step may be necessary; AcreOS must retain the task, requirements, return path, evidence, and follow-up inside the workspace. "Never leave the app" is a continuity promise, not a promise that AcreOS becomes a bank, title company, telephone carrier, marketplace, or universal identity provider.

Do not turn that path into a required setup wizard. An empty Today needs one meaningful Start with a market action, an understandable example of the intended outcome, and a way to defer optional configuration. ### 13.2 Two source-level performance and truth defects to verify before scaling

A single request has conflicting clocks. The shared client queryClient.ts uses a 30-second AbortSignal.timeout for its API helper; security.ts allows 90 seconds for /api/ai/, /api/pax/, and /api/atlas/. Any such call made through that shared helper may be abandoned by the client while the server still considers it active. Trace one actual caller before generalizing. Reproduce with a controlled delayed response and determine whether server work/effects continue after client abort. Align endpoint-specific latency and cancellation semantics; return a durable operation ID and progress/lookup for long or externally consequential work. A timeout after an attempted external action must be unknown result, not "failed, try again." Preserve the existing worker/outbox patterns and avoid an additional job framework.

The performance display confuses an aggregate with a percentile. apiTelemetry.ts calculates p95 inside minute/request windows, then uses MAX(p95Ms) across windows while exposing the result as p95 for the larger period. It also sums each window's distinctOrgs, which counts the same organization again in another window. The former is a worst window p95, useful for detecting bad intervals but mathematically different from period p95; the latter is an organization-window count. Rename those values honestly now. If period p95 or unique organizations actually informs capacity decisions, compute them from mergeable distribution/cardinality data or an appropriately bounded raw sample. A fixture with one slow window and many fast requests, and one organization in two windows, should show the distinction. Do not build an elaborate telemetry warehouse to fix labels.

The aim is a responsive experience through bounded work, not a universal sub-second SLA. Measure actual mobile cold entry, time to the first usable action, object-open latency, interaction response, long-work acceptance/progress, queue age, provider failures, and result acknowledgment at realistic tenant sizes. Segment demo versus real data, slow network versus server work, and cold versus warm paths. Use Google's Core Web Vitals guidance as a general browser reference, then set AcreOS's own journey budgets from observed devices and users. A Map that instantly displays 100 of 100,000 parcels without saying so fails regardless of its render speed.

### 13.3 A connector is a specific operating capability, not a green badge

## 14. A consequential new seam: one logical business action must survive retry and provider failover

Source baseline: The branch claude/acreos-canonical-implementation-1asgvc was checked again and still points to a2dc971809df64e2e10d8c3e1f1d3f1cc2496a31. The following are source-level findings at that commit, not claims that a production shipment has duplicated. No live send or test was run in this investigation.

### 14.1 The physical-mail path has three different protection levels

withOutwardAction has a valuable design: an atomic insert claims a unique organization/action/key, payload hash mismatch refuses, a success replays, an ambiguous provider result refuses, and a provably pre-provider failure can retry. Preserve that distinction and its existing direct-mail accounting. But the guarantee currently weakens at composition boundaries:

1. The retry claim is not single-winner. When an existing row is failed, two workers can both read it, both get execute from classifyExisting, and both run an UPDATE constrained only by the row ID before calling exec(). The initial unique insert protects the first claim, not this transition. The pure outwardActionIdempotency tests check classification and source presence but do not exercise the two-worker database race. This is a code-derived reachable interleaving; reproduce it against a test database with both retries paused after reading failed, then released together.
2. The guarded send is optional. directMailService.sendLetter goes directly to performLetterSend when idempotencyKey is absent. The Lob mail-router adapter passes no key to sendLetter or sendPostcard for its pieces. That specific path therefore does not gain the outward-action claim. The higher-level shipment claim prevents two workers from taking the same queued shipment concurrently, but it does not supply a provider-level per-piece key or determine what the provider accepted after an ambiguous response.
3. Failover does not distinguish no contact from unknown effect. MailRouter.route catches any adapter send error and tries the next viable provider with the same entire shipment. The MailShipment interface has no durable logical shipment/piece identifier or per-piece outcome. If provider A accepts piece 1 and throws on piece 2, provider B can receive the full shipment, including piece 1. If A accepted everything but its response times out, B can receive everything. This is a concrete risk implied by the control flow, not an observed customer incident. A cheap failover is appropriate only when A can establish it never accepted the logical pieces.

The small repair is a logical piece contract across providers, built on existing claim/receipt and queued-mail records, not a new mail platform. Give each intended piece a durable identity independent of provider selection. Before attempting it, bind organization, approved content, recipient, cost envelope and stable key. Record provider attempts and per-piece receipts. Permit automatic failover only after a proven not contacted or provider rejection before acceptance; hold an unknown result for provider lookup, webhook or operator reconciliation. A batch result must report accepted, rejected and unknown pieces separately. An unknown piece is one owner-visible case, not an excuse to repeat the entire batch or a reason to assign the customer routine provider administration.

For the failed-row race, replace the read-then-unconditional-update with a conditional transition such as UPDATE ... WHERE id = ? AND status = 'failed' AND request_hash = ? RETURNING id. Only the caller receiving the row may execute; the loser rereads and returns in-flight/refusal or a known outcome. PostgreSQL's Read Committed behavior rechecks an UPDATE condition after waiting for a concurrent updater, and RETURNING reports rows actually changed; this makes the single-winner property testable at the database boundary. The conditional claim does not resolve a crash after provider acceptance; stale in-flight work still needs an explicit age, reconciliation and safe resolution path. PostgreSQL concurrency documentation; UPDATE RETURNING.

Reachability and the additional accounting consequence. POST /api/outreach/mail/queue creates customer shipment and per-piece rows; the registered mailFlusher atomically claims due queued shipments using FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, then calls MailRouter.route. That claim is a genuine protection against duplicate concurrent flusher claims. But flushOne catches a router exception or subsequent per-piece database writeback error, marks every piece and the shipment failed, and refunds the full queue debit. If one physical piece was accepted before a later error, response loss or status-write failure, the stored status and refund can both contradict the external event. The router's optional second-provider fallback compounds this when multiple providers are enabled. With only Lob enabled there is no cross-provider fallback, yet the accepted-but-unknown → failed/refunded path remains. A later manual resend could then duplicate the piece. Source establishes the path and risk, not an actual mailed duplicate or the behavior of a particular provider under a particular error.

Controlled proof: two retries after a pre-provider failed claim produce one provider call; one unknown outcome produces zero automatic fallback calls; a two-piece shipment with first piece accepted and second unknown never resends the first; a proven pre-provider refusal may choose B; retries after process restart preserve these results. Inject a database writeback failure after the provider returns a successful piece ID and verify it cannot convert that piece into "failed and refunded." Assert queue debit/refund, piece statuses and external piece IDs alongside provider call counts. An unknown provider outcome must not be refunded as though no mail was sent; maintain an unresolved financial exposure until reconciliation. No real Lob/PostGrid send is needed for the failing fixture; authorized sandbox qualification remains a later, separate proof.

### 14.2 The same closed-loop test should govern customer obligations

A second source seam shows why a business action cannot end at a database update. acquiredNoteAging.ts updates each note's aging status, increments transitioned, then calls emitPaymentEvent without awaiting a durable handoff; exceptions in the wrapper are swallowed. workflow-engine.ts appends events to an in-memory queue, starts asynchronous processing and logs workflow errors internally. A process exit between status commit and workflow execution can leave the note changed with no corresponding follow-up. A later sweep need not emit the same transition again because the status has already changed. The prior report found a related seam for ordinary note due detection; this acquired-note path is a separate caller and must not be assumed fixed by that work.

For an obligation that depends on aging, make the status change and durable work intent one transaction, or use an existing transactional outbox with a recoverable scan. The worker's acknowledgment and the customer's visible due/late state must have separate meanings. transitioned=1 is not follow-up completed=1. At portfolio scale the job's current SELECT of every nonterminal acquired note and per-row updates should be measured and bounded by relevant due dates, organization and keyset batches, with a persisted cursor or replayable query; avoid premature partitioning without measured load. Keep case-specific legal/contract terms and missing schedule facts explicit rather than using a universal delinquency clock.

Controlled proof: crash after status write and before workflow dispatch; restart; exactly one durable obligation becomes visible, and its completed state requires the actual workflow result. One note with missing schedule facts remains unknown, not current. An injected failure for one organization cannot yield an all-green sweep merely because the outer loop finished; its error count and customer/founder exception remain inspectable. Test a growing synthetic book for elapsed sweep time and missed due records before optimizing the query.

### 14.3 What this changes for the mature product

The most intelligent AcreOS is not the one that performs the most actions. It is the one that keeps a closed loop for each consequential move: bounded intent → one logical effect → provider observation or explicit unknown → reconciliation → continuing duty → outcome. The customer sees a concise status on the parcel, campaign or note: "Prepared," "Sent to provider," "Provider accepted 18 of 20; two unresolved," "Due for follow-up," or "Completed with evidence." A duplicate click resumes the same operation rather than starting another. Pax can explain and propose the next step from that record, but cannot declare success from a timeout or a model-generated sentence.

The founder Letter should aggregate stable unresolved cases by harm and deadline: how many customer duties are unowned, how many external effects are ambiguous, whether recovery is automatic, and the small number that require a real human decision. Routine retries and reconciliation belong to the system under its existing authority. This is the common operating protocol that can later make mail, offers, e-sign, payments and listing channels feel like one coherent AcreOS without replacing each working subsystem now.

Preserve the shipment's SKIP LOCKED protection, provider credential authority, ledger, queue and receipts already in place. ## 17. A second concrete commissioning seam: the same buyer payment has competing meanings

This investigation is read-only at the same pinned commit. It is a source-level divergence, not evidence that a borrower has been charged incorrectly in production. It sharpens the finance and outward-effect gates in Sections 1, 3, 13 and 14. If a paying customer uses AcreOS to steward seller-financed notes, it should take priority over speculative agents and, where that path is live, over the Luna County sourcing exercise.

### 17.1 One Stripe Checkout Session can be posted by three paths

The preferred borrower endpoint in routes-borrower.ts accepts an optional customer-entered amount, creates a card Checkout Session as a direct charge to the lender's connected account, and includes note, organization and access-token metadata through customerMoneyRouting.ts. The browser-return /api/borrower/verify-payment path reads the session from that connected account, requires payment_status === "paid", uses the cents-based splitPaymentCents, applies a conditional late-fee calculation and inserts the row and balance change transactionally with a transaction_id conflict guard. It emits payment.received after commit. This is substantial existing work worth preserving.

The same session can arrive through the signed connected-account webhook. stripeConnect.ts calls WebhookHandlers.processBorrowerPortalPayment. That method still takes principal and interest from the next amortization schedule row using a floating-point ratio, stores lateFeeAmount: "0", then independently marks the next schedule row paid and advances the next due date. The deprecated token return path is a third writer with the newer cents split but a separate storage.createPayment and follow-up schedule update. Idempotency can suppress a second row; it does not make the first writer's allocation, fee, schedule or resulting communications equivalent.

For a falsifiable example, take a hypothetical $1,000 current principal at 12% APR, a $100 payment and a stale schedule row allocating $20 interest/$80 principal. The browser path's fixed-month calculation allocates $10 interest/$90 principal; the webhook's schedule ratio allocates $20/$80. The $10 difference propagates to principal, future interest and payoff. An after-grace payment may add a conditional late-fee value in the browser route while the webhook records zero. The fixture deliberately makes the schedule differ from the current balance after an adjustment; whether either split is contractually right also depends on the actual instrument and accrual policy. The demonstrated defect is event-order-dependent accounting for one payment, not proof that the cents helper correctly implements every land contract.

The first path to write also changes side effects. The browser route emits a payment.received workflow event; the inspected webhook method does not emit that event. Conversely, that webhook attempts a direct borrower receipt email after posting, but returns early without one when it finds the session was already recorded by the browser route. Another receipt workflow may exist, so do not claim no borrower ever receives one. There is no single durable, inspectable guarantee here that a posted payment produced its required receipt/follow-up exactly once. A failed email is logged while the already-posted payment remains, correctly avoiding a financial rollback, but a log alone does not steward an unsatisfied duty. Stripe documents that its Checkout landing page alone is unreliable for fulfillment, and its webhooks may be duplicated, retried and delivered without guaranteed event ordering (fulfillment, webhook delivery). Browser-first, webhook-first and retry-after-crash are normal cases to qualify, not exotic faults.

### 17.2 An authorized partial payment can make the next installment appear paid

The preferred checkout route accepts an amount other than the scheduled monthly payment. After a paid session is posted, routes-borrower.ts unconditionally marks the first pending amortization row paid and advances nextPaymentDate one month. The webhook does the same. The event builder, meanwhile, labels a payment below the scheduled amount isPartial: true. Thus a $50 payment against a $100 due installment can coexist with "next installment paid" and a next-month due date. Depending on the note, the $50 might be applied to interest/principal, held unapplied, or satisfy a modified installment; the code must not silently choose "fully paid" without a contractual/payment-application policy and remaining-amount calculation. A payment above the payoff can also leave residueCents from splitPaymentCents; the inspected posting routes do not visibly steward that residue as a payable/refund/unapplied balance. Verify call-site behavior at current HEAD before concluding how overpayments are handled elsewhere.

This is a particularly costly false-completeness defect: a borrower may see no amount due while the lender's due detector skips the period, or the lender may mistakenly rely on a projected receipt. It also creates contradictory documents if a payoff quote reads the principal balance while the schedule calls the installment settled. Do not fix it by deciding every partial payment is delinquent; concessions, prepayments, holiday periods, fee priorities and instrument terms can change the answer. Separate money observed, money finally settled, allocation under the instrument, installment satisfaction, and next required date.

### 17.3 The mature operating contract, kept narrow enough to ship

## 19. Outreach intelligence begins with purpose, recipient identity and a recoverable effect

The present smsService.ts has real protective work: a central sender, consent/quiet-hours checks when a lead matches, STOP ingestion, optional DNC provider integration, a frequency gate and the lender/customer's own connected sending identity. sequenceProcessor.ts also defers rather than falsely completing some blocked steps. ### 19.1 "No matching lead" is not evidence that a text is transactional

tcpaGateForRecipient reads all lead IDs and phone strings for an organization on each send, finds the first number whose last ten digits match, then applies lead consent and quiet hours. When nothing matches, it automatically classifies the destination as transactional: the matched-lead consent, quiet-hours and frequency checks are skipped; the optional DNC scrub still runs with leadMatched:false, and its error posture can allow the send. If DNC_SCRUB_PROVIDER is unset, the module explicitly reports that the scrub is inert; a vendor decision recorded in documentation is not proof of a configured, live scrub. A selected but uncredentialed adapter has a different, explicit error result. The key failure is that a missing CRM row is treated as a different communication purpose.

The AI send_sms tool accepts either a lead_id or a phone_number. Its phone-only branch checks inferred quiet hours and requires a human tap, but it has no lead consent object; sendOrgSMS then takes the unmatched-recipient branch. Human approval of a send is authority from the AcreOS customer, not consent from the recipient. A phone-only message can be a one-to-one response about an existing obligation, a new prospect solicitation, a buyer promotion, or a support reply. The server must know which, rather than guessing from row presence. The exact federal/state rules vary by message, equipment, recipient and transaction; this report does not classify a land-purchase offer as covered telemarketing. FCC rules on consent and revocation and the FTC's scope-specific telemarketing guidance show why a general marketing/transactional shortcut cannot be a legal determination.

The first-match lookup has a second failure mode. Two leads can carry the same normalized number, perhaps one with an opt-out and one with consent, and an unordered .find() chooses whichever database row comes first. The STOP handler tries to revoke all matching leads, a good safeguard, but duplicated imports, late updates or a manually changed number can still make a per-lead permission ambiguous. Consent, revocation and phone ownership need their own dated evidence and relationship to the sending organization and message purpose. Missing, conflicting or stale evidence should yield an explicit refusal/review for an optional solicitation, not an accidental allow. At campaign scale, reading every lead for every recipient grows with lead count × send count and can strain the app when the user most needs predictable send status; use an indexed normalized phone lookup and handle every match. This is a targeted query and identity improvement, not a new general contact graph.

### 19.2 A carrier-accepted text must not become "failed" because bookkeeping failed

After commsRouter.route returns a provider SID, sendOrgSMS awaits a cost-ledger write and then a contact-frequency touch write. All three calls share one try/catch. If either internal write throws after the provider accepted the SMS, the function returns { success:false } without exposing the SID to its caller. A route that refunds a pre-send pool draw or a person who retries may therefore treat a sent message as unsent. This is the same accepted-but-unknown failure class established for physical mail in Section 14, now at a second installed outward-action chokepoint. The local provider response is acceptance for delivery processing, not proof the handset received the text; subsequent delivery/failure callbacks and replies remain separate observations.

The minimal repair uses an existing durable message/send record or a narrow outbox intent with an organization-scoped logical message key, recipient, purpose, approved content, consent/permission evidence version, cost bound and channel. Claim it before provider call. Persist provider attempt/SID and the distinction between not attempted, accepted, rejected, unknown and delivered/failed as observed. After acceptance, a cost or touch write becomes a pending internal reconciliation, never a failed external send eligible for blind retry or a refund as though no expense occurred. If the provider timed out without a SID, reconcile against its supported lookup/callback before re-sending; no global exactly-once promise. Apply the same purpose/evidence check again just before a delayed scheduled send so a new STOP or revoked grant is honored. Reuse the queued mail lessons and provider receipt contracts where they fit; no separate universal communications platform is justified by this one slice.

An opt-out is an enduring relationship event, not a keyword that lives in a single SMS thread. The existing STOP path sets doNotContact for matching leads and records a consent event; keep it. The mature relationship view must distinguish an optional marketing follow-up from a contracted servicing duty that may require a different lawful channel after a text opt-out. Do not reinterpret STOP as permission to keep texting "important" offers, and do not silently abandon a buyer's time-sensitive obligation because the SMS channel was revoked. A reviewed, case-specific policy should say which notice is required, which channel remains permitted and who owns an unresolved delivery duty. FTC guidance also distinguishes federal DNC scope from entity-specific requests and state requirements; a vendor's clean result cannot replace the whole purpose/consent analysis.

## 22. A borrower notice is a time-sensitive obligation, not a static task

The active development branch claude/acreos-canonical-implementation-1asgvc still compared identical to a2dc971809df64e2e10d8c3e1f1d3f1cc2496a31 when this section was researched. This is read-only source analysis, with no live send or customer account observation. The existing financeAgent.ts has improved substantially: deterministic collection text replaced an LLM, the ladder is bounded, each outbound rung waits for a customer tap, an unsent letter is called a document, and sent requires a named accepting rail. The borrowerDunningLadder.ts is actually registered through the finance agent. The following are specific gaps in that working path, not grounds to replace it.

### 22.1 The reminder can outlive its financial premise

ensureLadderRung snapshots the due date, amount, balance, recipient, channel and notice text into a payment_reminders row. dispatchReminder later re-reads the note, but refuses only if it is missing or its status is no longer active; it does not recheck the reminder's due period, current balance, payment allocation, recipient or current notice text. sendManualReminder finds a parked awaiting_approval reminder by organization, note and type, selects its latest creation time and sends the saved content with humanApproved:true. A borrower can pay or partially cure the debt while the note stays active; nextPaymentDate and amount due can change before the investor taps the queued approval. The old "past due" amount or date can then be sent as though still current. A valid approval authorizes the specific present notice, not an unbounded future assertion. The terminal demand_letter branch creates a fresh document from current note fields, but also needs a current debt and eligibility check before suggesting escalation; it does not claim to mail it.

The maturity contract is a pre-send revalidation: bind the parked notice to the due period, note balance/delinquency basis, borrower identity, content version and approving actor. Before an external call, atomically claim that exact reminder, re-read authoritative payment state, and either send precisely the approved, still-true notice or mark it superseded/needs_review with an updated preview. Never quietly rewrite approved collection text and send it under the old tap. A payment posted concurrently with the send still needs a defined ordering boundary and a later correction path; no ordinary database lock can retract a message already accepted outside the database.

### 22.2 Provider acceptance can be erased by later bookkeeping failure

The communications.ts email path awaits recordCommunication after emailService.sendEmail reports success; if that write throws, it does not return the provider messageId to financeAgent. The finance agent then awaits updateNote, recordAutonomousSend and only last recordReminderOutcome(sent, acceptedBy=channel). A failure in any of those steps after a provider accepts the message yields an exception. The scheduled sweep catches it and attempts to mark the reminder failed; the manual/approval path returns success:false, and a still-parked approval may be tried again. recordReminderOutcome stores a channel name, not the returned provider message ID. Thus "sent only with an accepting rail" does not establish a recoverable one-reminder/one-provider-operation relation across a process crash or late write failure. This is a source-level possibility; provider behavior and actual duplicates were not observed.

For email, AWS explicitly warns that a timed-out or failed API response can follow an accepted message and a retry can send another with a new ID; acceptance can also precede later delivery failure. SES sending process and SendEmail response. Twilio similarly distinguishes acceptance, queueing, sent, delivered and failure in its outbound status documentation. The product language must distinguish prepared → attempted/unknown → provider accepted → delivered/failed when witnessed, and never say "the borrower received it" from an API success alone. Keep the existing outward_actions, approval kernel and communications providers; add a durable reminder-to-provider receipt or reuse an existing field if it genuinely carries the logical send key and provider ID. A known accepted operation should finish its local bookkeeping idempotently without another send. An unknown email send should be reconciled or shown for review before reissue; promising exactly-once delivery across SES without provider support would be false.

### 22.3 A blocked queue can starve unrelated customers

getDispatchableReminders selects scheduled and queued across all organizations, orders by oldest scheduledFor and limits to 50. A missing sender identity, paused Pax or reached send envelope leaves a reminder queued, and that same old row remains eligible until the 14-day retry window expires. dispatchDueReminders processes just that returned batch per tick. Fifty persistently blocked reminders from one organization can therefore fill every 30-minute batch while newer due reminders at other organizations wait unseen, potentially until the old rows age out. A global limit is valuable, but it is not fair work selection or a guarantee of deadline coverage. This is a deterministic consequence of the query and status transition, not a measured production backlog.

The narrow remedy is to put nextAttemptAt (with bounded backoff and terminal escalation) on retryable rows or an equivalent persisted queue schedule, and select due work fairly across organizations and deadlines. Exclude parked approvals from automatic dispatch; retain their deadline and owner-visible state. Expose oldest unattempted due age, due eligible count, deferred count by reason and per-org coverage, so a healthy job run cannot mask a missed notice. Avoid increasing the batch size as the sole fix: it moves the saturation point, increases provider load and still lets one blocked tenant dominate. At low volume, the current query is simple and serviceable; this becomes a launch blocker only when reminders are a promised feature and the adverse backlog fixture reproduces.

### 22.4 One controlled customer timeline for Claude

At actual HEAD and deployed reachability, first mark each case fixed/reproduced/unverified/inapplicable. Then exercise one note, one approved reminder, and one additional organization through the existing financeAgent, paxAskExecutors, communications and repository seams:

2. In a separate fault fixture, make a provider accept an email and then fail recordCommunication, updateNote, recordAutonomousSend or reminder outcome persistence. Replay/restart must not silently send a second collection notice. The provider ID and unresolved internal duty stay visible until reconciled. Preserve the fail-closed consent and sender-identity rules.
3. Hold 50 old reminders in queued for one unconnected sender, then create a newly due reminder in another organization. The latter must get an attempt within its promised window, and the old work must neither vanish nor endlessly consume every slot. A queue with no provider access is an honest exception, not a customer-wide healthy dispatch.

These cases extend the earlier Section 20 single-move contract: actual borrower money and truthful notice are one stewardship journey. They should be addressed in consequence order as real reachability and design-partner needs establish it, without starting three parallel subsystems. Counterargument: approval of a notice is an informed human action, and the customer might deliberately want to send an updated reminder after payment. That is possible, but a stale prewritten amount is not the updated action they approved. Offer a current preview and a new tap when the financial premise changes. Counterargument: a 50-item backlog may not occur before launch. ### 23.2 "IRR to date" uses a hypothetical balance at the wrong date

The computeYields function puts actual payment principal and interest into calendar-month buckets, then appends currentBalanceCents as a terminal positive cash flow one month after the latest recorded payment, or at month two when there are no payments. The "Effective net" number subtracts a fixed 25 basis points from that provisional IRR and applies Math.max(0, ...), so a negative modeled return can be flattened to 0% rather than displayed as negative. The panel does disclose the servicing assumption, which is good; the inherited IRR timing/terminal problem and zero floor remain. "Current yield" uses current balance times rate divided by original acquisition price; that is a stated coupon-on-cost approximation, and zero purchase price yields 0 instead of unknown. YTM at acquisition uses termMonths promised payments after acquisition; qualify whether imported notes bought midlife encode original or remaining term before presenting it as a schedule-specific YTM. None of these percentages establishes cash collected, market value or full after-cost return.

A payoff quote is a contractual amount owed, not necessarily a market bid or a clearing deposit. Do not replace this useful planning panel with a new financial engine: separate its projected and observed measures, pass a true asOf, reuse the existing payoff calculator for an expressly labeled payoff scenario, preserve negative outcomes and make insufficiency visible. If there is no trustworthy terminal value, show collected cash and unrecovered cost with an optional scenario slider rather than a definitive "IRR to date."

## 26. Imported contacts are not an acquisition universe

This read-only inspection uses the active branch at a2dc971809df64e2e10d8c3e1f1d3f1cc2496a31; no customer file or live import was touched. AcreOS already has three useful but differently scoped entry paths: the ordinary Leads CSV dialog, a column-mapping CsvImportSheet, and a job-backed migration page. Preserve the bulk-job substrate and the existing strict normalizeParcelRef/parcelKey; the tax-sale importer already demonstrates explicit mapping, (state, county, APN) identity and per-row acceptance/rejection without silent loss.

### 26.1 One file can lose the very field that says which parcel matters

The convenient CsvImportSheet offers APN, state and owner/address mapping, previews five rows and POSTs mapped rows to /api/leads/csv-import. It offers no county target. The matching routes-leads.ts schema neither accepts nor inserts county; its duplicate key is (state, APN). The source comment itself acknowledges that two counties in one state can share an APN and collide. If one such row already exists, the new parcel's row is counted as skippedExisting; if both occur in the uploaded file, the later row is skippedDuplicateInFile. The user sees a successful import count, but cannot trace the omitted parcel or use the correct county for due diligence. The preview's within-file duplicate warning uses APN alone, while the server uses (state, APN), so even the advance count can disagree across states. This is a narrowly demonstrated identity flaw, not a general claim that all imported leads are wrong.

The larger importLeads path is appropriate for CRM contacts: it maps name, email, phone and mailing address, with optional tags, assignment and historical creation time. Its lead map and validated insert do not preserve county or APN, even though the property map elsewhere understands those fields. It checks existing duplicates using email, phone or first-and-last name alone, as an OR. An existing same-named person or the same owner with another parcel can therefore cause a new imported row to be skipped without parcel-level review. Conversely, a contact with no parcel is a legitimate CRM record. The product should let the user declare contacts migration versus parcel-owner acquisition list, and must not claim the former has created a qualified parcel universe. A candidate parcel, its owner identity and a contactable prospect are related entities, not interchangeable rows.

### 26.2 The onboarding success state outruns the import job

For a queued 202 job this can happen before any row is inserted, and a later failed job is not followed in onboarding. For a synchronous partial import with successCount>0, the count and completion explanation are still wrong. RFC 9110 defines 202 Accepted as accepted for processing with processing unfinished; a job ID is a tracking receipt, not a completed-import receipt. The separate data-import.tsx page already polls import jobs and shows results. On its small synchronous branch, however, it always says "Import queued" and expects a job below, which that branch never creates. These are UI contract errors over an existing dual-mode API, not evidence that the worker fails in production.

## 27. "Import complete" must include recoverable downstream work

Section 26 examined what an imported row means. This section asks whether accepted work survives normal operation at scale. ### 27.1 The durable row points to a temporary file on one machine

For a 50,000-row import, the source comment correctly observes that 50,000 events can be queued; "off the request path" protects response latency but does not prove memory, queue-age or completion reliability. ## 28. Portfolio intelligence must know what money event happened

### 28.1 A closed acquisition can become an invented sale

The deal schema explicitly represents a deal as an acquisition or disposition through type; offerAmount and acceptedAmount are offer details, with a separate closingDate and closingCosts. The deal calculator treats the deal's accepted amount, falling back to offer amount, as a possible purchase price. Yet getPortfolioPnl selects every closed deal without type, aliases offerAmount as purchase price and acceptedAmount as sale price, and counts each positive accepted amount as a sale. For a hypothetical closed acquisition offered at $5,000 and accepted at $4,500, its report would add $5,000 of acquisition cost, $4,500 of sale proceeds, one acquired deal, one sold deal and a $500 loss without a disposition record. Closing a negotiation also does not, by itself, evidence that any amount cleared a financial account. This is a deterministic source consequence of that data shape, not a claim that this exact transaction exists in production.

The portfolio page presents these totals as annual "profit and loss," "Net profit," "Cash-on-cash return" and sold-deal averages. Its earlier payload-shape repair and provenance chip are useful, but neither changes what the service measures. The service subtracts only the offer-derived acquisition cost from sale-derived and interest-derived revenue: it omits closing costs, ordinary expenses, upstream financing payments, principal movements and outstanding obligations. Accordingly, this is neither an auditable realized profit nor a complete cash position. A useful deal-spread scenario could deliberately compare an accepted purchase price with an expected sale price, but that is a different, explicitly projected measure with separate inputs.

The note side introduces another type error: interestPortion ?? amount counts an entire completed note payment as interest whenever the split is absent. Contracted notes.monthlyPayment is summed under notesReceivable.monthlyIncome; that is expected scheduled cash, not observed collection. Principal payments are selected but never fed into the cash-flow series, even though they matter to investor capital recovery. "Completed" is a software payment state; whether it means settled funds must be established against the relevant payment rail. None of these observations establishes that the existing accounting ledger is unusable. They establish that this particular projection does not reconcile to it.

### 28.2 An undated sequence cannot be called an annualized return

calculateIrr discounts each entry by its array index. The caller pushes negative offer cost and positive accepted amount adjacent to each other for a closed deal, then pushes interest payments after all deals. The selected deal/payment rows have no explicit chronological order; the annual report filters deals by closing date inside the selected year, excluding earlier acquisitions from that year's series. A pair of -$10,000 and +$11,000 yields the same approximately 10% per array slot whether the true elapsed time is a month or a decade. Such a sequence cannot support an annualized money-weighted return. Microsoft's IRR documentation distinguishes periodic entries interpreted by sequence from its XIRR, which takes the dates of irregular cash flows. This does not prescribe Excel as the implementation; it states the mathematical inputs a comparable claim requires.

Return calculations also need a valuation or explicit realization boundary for capital still tied up at the reporting date. A report covering one calendar year cannot compute the return on an ongoing land portfolio by considering only transactions closed that year and treating outstanding assets as nothing. Multiple sign changes may admit ambiguous IRRs; a solver should report "not determinable" with its reason rather than choose an unstable number. The simplest correct first release is often no portfolio IRR until transaction identities, dates, actual cash flows, financing and ending asset values are trustworthy.

### 28.3 A small economic ontology, not a new finance platform

### 28.5 One upstream promise can cover many downstream sales

## 30. Mature autonomy needs a cancellable action boundary and an honest unknown state

### 30.1 The current "cancelled" tap has no winning condition

beginActionPreview inserts pending, then waits in the executor process by polling every 500 ms if a founder-configured window is positive. The setting defaults to zero, so this is an audit record by default, with no practical advance-cancellation interval. At the end the executor calls shouldProceed(), a SELECT that checks status==='pending', then invokes the action in a separate step. The founder can call cancelPreview after that SELECT but before the effect. Its UPDATE will mark the still-pending row cancelled; the route returns {ok:true}. The action can then happen, and recordResult updates by ID with no status predicate, overwriting cancelled with committed. This is a source-level race with a misleading successful cancellation response, not proof that a human has hit that interval in production.

There is a second ambiguity: an executor crash during its in-process wait leaves pending until the hourly sweeper marks it failed; no durable timer resumes it. A crash after an effect but before recordResult can produce the same eventual failed: orphaned label even though the effect occurred. One executor posts a support reply and resolves the ticket; its canonical reply writer inserts the message and updates ticket status as two database statements. A crash between them can leave a posted answer on an open ticket; blind retry can post the answer twice. Churn intervention takes a different, safer governed-email proposal path, so the scope of this failure is the actual selected action, not a claim that every AI send bypasses approval.

### 30.2 Give the founder an exact, legible promise

The action record should distinguish planned, cancelled_before_claim, claimed/executing, effect_confirmed, failed_before_effect, and effect_unknown_needs_reconciliation. A losing cancel gets "Already started; inspect the result" with the current state, rather than ok:true. Once a provider or database has accepted the effect, a cancellation is a new compensating request, if possible, never a rewrite of history.

For a bounded delay, store the due time and use a small durable claim worker on the existing database and job runtime, or reuse an existing qualified durable scheduler. A process-local sleep is suitable for a demonstration, not a guarantee that an action survives deploy. A claimed action needs a stable logical operation ID and an idempotent target writer/provider key. Separate the intent to act from the effect receipt; on crash, query or reconcile the target when possible before retrying. Where a provider cannot be queried or offers no idempotency, keep the result unknown and give the founder a recoverable case. Durable-timer systems such as Temporal offer one possible implementation at greater scale; their existence does not justify installing a second workflow platform for one AcreOS preview window.
