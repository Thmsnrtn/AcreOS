## The intended product

AcreOS should let a new property investor start with a credible first decision and let an existing operator bring in a real book, run the recurring business, see obligations and economics, and recover from failures without leaving the app for routine work. It must be equally intelligible to the founder on an iPhone: what customers can actually accomplish, which ones are stuck, what an agent or provider did, what money is real, and what decision requires the founder. A route, fixture or polished card is only a component of that job.

Preserve the five-door customer shell, the 15 business-type registry and three note roles, the sample seeder, rent and note posting engines, provider registry, app-intent/approval kernel, founder four-door concept, and other working code. Refine existing boundaries. Do not replace the current campaign with a wholesale rewrite, create another profile registry, ledger, inbox, activity feed or general-purpose agent framework, or turn every potential improvement into a ticket.

## What the mature first-run and return experience must prove

### First-customer trace added at `457e6e1`: county coverage and consequential mail

**Priority correction: qualify the customer-facing Outreach composer before the legacy campaign route below.** A beginner can select a named marketing list or enter counties in `client/src/pages/outreach/mail/compose.tsx:156–185,270–344`, then queue mail with a quoted count and cost. `server/routes-outreach-mail.ts:151–237` applies state, but does not apply requested counties, acreage, saved views, or actual membership of selected list IDs. It reads selected marketing lists' **states** and adds that predicate, or adds none for a list with no states. A selected Hidalgo County list with Texas state can therefore address other Texas leads; a list without states can address the whole organization's eligible CRM, up to a silent 50,000-row cap. An unknown/deleted list ID also gives no narrowing. The preview button calls `/api/outreach/mail/preview`, for which no route was found; `PreviewList` catches the failure and substitutes a placeholder (`compose.tsx:821–852`). The first shipment must use an inspectable, immutable recipient/copy/price snapshot whose size and exclusions agree at quote, confirmation and dispatch. If list membership is not represented today, refuse the list option or qualify it through the existing lead-list model; do not infer membership from state. Test a two-county, two-list organization with a missing list ID and 50,001 eligible leads, and assert that *only* intended recipients enter the persisted piece set. Test a real preview of recipients and rendered text against that same frozen set. Refuse an unsupported filter instead of silently mailing a broader audience.

**Queue once, then steward the hold.** `routes-outreach-mail.ts:392–592` has no `idempotencyMiddleware` or other stable request-to-shipment identity although the UI supplies a fresh `Idempotency-Key` (`use-outreach-mail.ts:137–151`). The 30-minute hold stores recipient addresses, but `mailFlusher.ts:200–240` reads pending pieces without rechecking a subsequent do-not-contact/opt-out on the linked lead. Use one persisted operation and recipient snapshot for quote, debit and shipment, recover a lost response by that identity, and check mutable suppression immediately before provider handoff; suppress/refund affected unsent pieces with an auditable reason. Test duplicate/concurrent requests, response loss, a lead added between reads, and opt-out during the hold. Preserve `DEFECT-0105`'s accepted-piece and refund rules. ## Product and founder acceptance standard

- Every number names its meaning: forecast, asking price, accepted contract, operator-recorded receipt, provider acceptance, settled deposit, liability, trailing revenue, recurring run rate and net profit are distinct. Model suggestions cite source/date/coverage/rights/assumptions and can say “insufficient evidence.”
- Every external action names the acting account and authority, shows the intended recipient/cost, and ends as pending, accepted, confirmed, failed or unknown based on evidence. Unknown is not automatically retried. Customer obligations survive a provider or process outage.
- An investor's asserted deal stage remains useful as an assertion. Activation, contract-dependent automation, financial recognition, model training and cross-customer market signals require the distinct evidence each claim needs. Every shared training signal has a correction/withdrawal path and respects customer data rights.
- Every profile sees relevant first work and a relevant return briefing, while a multi-strategy investor can see all owned assets. The interface remains navigable on an iPhone, with one primary action, visible save/result feedback and progressive disclosure of the trace.
- Founder health shows real customer task completion, cohort denominators, unresolved duties, contribution after provider/support costs, failed connectors and safe controls. It can distinguish “no observed activity” from “we could not read activity.”
- No production claim is upgraded from a unit double, static code, demo fixture, or accessible route. No live customer message, charge, import, provider connection or deployment is authorized by this research document.

**Strongest counterargument:** fixing these seams may defer visible new features. That is justified where a current promise can misstate a send, money, identity or authority. If deployed usage shows a feature is dormant, contain it and prioritize the actually used path; if production telemetry contradicts a source-level risk, record the evidence and re-rank. The aim is a reliable investor operating loop and an exceptionally manageable solo-founder business, not a larger catalogue of screens.
