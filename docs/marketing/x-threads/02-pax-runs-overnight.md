# Thread 2 — Pax runs overnight

## Tweet 1 (hook — 230 chars, stands alone)

> Pax is the AI assistant inside AcreOS. It runs overnight.
>
> By the time the operator opens the app in the morning, Pax has pulled new county lists against the saved buy-box, run comparable-sales on the parcels that passed, drafted reply emails, and queued tasks.

## Tweet 2 (251 chars)

> Every draft cites the comps it used. Every action shows the data trace behind it. Nothing happens behind the operator's back.
>
> A drafted mail piece lists the parcels it priced against. A scored lead shows which buy-box filters it passed and which it almost failed.

## Tweet 3 (244 chars)

> The skip list is also shown:
>
> · matched an owned parcel
> · recipient was mailed in the last 90 days
> · address came back returned-to-sender
> · do-not-contact flagged
>
> Each skipped lead has a reason. Nothing silently disappears.

## Tweet 4 (240 chars)

> What Pax does not do:
>
> · give investment advice (Constitution §12; code-enforced)
> · auto-send anything that touches a third party (mail, email, calls — all require approval)
> · hide what it can't do (failed comp pulls flagged in the morning queue)

## Tweet 5 (192 chars)

> The operator handles judgment calls. The system handles the busy work.
>
> That contract is in the AcreOS Constitution and on the landing page. It is the design, not a tagline.

## Tweet 6 (217 chars)

> A Tuesday morning operator on AcreOS:
>
> · 3 seller replies to approve
> · 11 mail drafts to approve in bulk
> · 2 missed-note-payment workflows ready to send
>
> Twenty minutes of judgment, not three hours of tab-switching.
