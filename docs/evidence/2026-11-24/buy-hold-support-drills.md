## Drill 1 — Money moment: "my tenant paid, but the ledger still shows it unpaid — where did the money go?" — PASS (deterministic legs)

- **Seeded state (synthetic, local):** the P3-3 walk's own shape — a lease charge of $1,500.00 rent (`150_000`¢) plus a $50.00 late fee (`5_000`¢), and the walk's partial-payment shape ($500 paid against a larger open amount, the remainder held open).
- **Ticket (as designed):** "the tenant paid $500, your ledger still shows money owing, and a second payment came in covering two months — prove the balances are right and tell me where every cent went."
- **Correctness rule:** allocation is oldest-charge-first, rent before late fees, through the ONE rule the one writer uses; every cent is either on a charge or an explicit unapplied credit (never clamped away); the Pax record path and the REST record path call the writer with the identical field set, so the two paths cannot land differently; a partial payment that would void a notice to vacate refuses with the shortfall named.
- **Observed (measured, this run):** through the real `allocatePayment` / `assertAllocationBalances` (`@shared/rental/paymentAllocation` — the exact rule `recordRentPayment` in `server/services/rental/paymentPosting.ts` calls inside its transaction): (1) $500 against the $1,500 + $50 charge applies entirely to rent (`appliedToRentCents: 50_000`, `appliedToLateFeeCents: 0`) and leaves `balanceAfterCents: 105_000` — the balances balance (`assertAllocationBalances` holds) and the explanation reads "$500.00 applied oldest-charge-first, rent before late fees → … ($1,050.00 left)"; (2) a full $1,550 clears rent then fee to zero; (3) a $1,600 payment across October + November charges clears October first even when the charges are passed in reverse order (`orderRule: oldest_charge_first_rent_before_late_fee`), leaving November $1,450 open; (4) a $2,000 payment against a single $1,500 charge applies $1,500 and holds **$500 as an explicit unapplied credit**, in words — the old `max(0, …)` swallow the writer's header describes is gone from the rule; (5) a non-integer cent amount throws `RangeError` before it can reach a ledger. **Field-for-field:** the key set of the `recordRentPayment(tx, {…})` call in `server/ai/buyHoldTools.ts` (`paxRecordRentPayment`) is **identical** to the key set of the same call in `server/routes-rent-ledger.ts` (`POST /api/leases/:id/payments`) — the same eleven fields (`acceptedDespitePartial, amountCents, leaseId, method, notes, operationKey, organizationId, payorTenantId, payorType, receivedAt, referenceNumber`), pinned by extraction from both call sites in the drill test, so Pax-recorded rent and page-recorded rent are the same ledger act. **Refusal leg:** the real `PartialPaymentUnderNoticeError` carries code `PARTIAL_PAYMENT_VOIDS_NOTICE` and names the shortfall ("leaves the 2026-11 charge short by 70000 cents while it is under a notice to vacate") — the Pax tool converts exactly this error into its plain-words refusal (first-hand read of `paxRecordRentPayment`). Pinned in the new `tests/unit/buyHoldSupportDrills.test.ts`. Supporting legs: `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts` (the executor path: session-org pinning, one receipt per record change, future-date refusal), `rentCollection.test.ts`, `rentalEvents.test.ts`.
- **Where the money goes (the support answer this drill proves producible):** rent collected by card settles on the **landlord's own Stripe Standard account** (drill 2 proves the routing refusal side); AcreOS holds none of it at any point. A payment the landlord received outside AcreOS and recorded is a ledger entry of money already received — the tool's own result words say "AcreOS charged nothing and holds nothing" (pinned in `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts`).
- **Verdict: PASS.** The webhook-only posting and redelivery-idempotence legs of this scenario were walked live in the P3-3 rent-collection walk (Leg 5: signed redelivery, `duplicate: true`, exactly one payment; Leg 7: the partial-payment shape) and are not re-claimed here; what this drill adds is the deterministic proof that the posting math and the Pax/REST parity a support answer quotes are the product's real rule, not a support-side reconstruction.

## Drill 2 — Stuck moment: "I can't collect rent — Stripe says my setup isn't finished" — PASS

- **Seeded state (synthetic, local):** four landlord processor states, fed to the real resolver through its designed injectable-deps seam (`CustomerMoneyRoutingDeps` — the same seam `customerMoneyRouting.test.ts` uses; no Stripe call is made).
- **Ticket (as designed):** "tenants can't pay me online. Is AcreOS holding my rent? Can you just take the payment on your account instead?"
- **Correctness rule:** collection resolves ONLY to the landlord's own connected Stripe account; every unusable state refuses with a typed reason and plain operator + counterparty words; there is no platform fallback in any branch; a failed status check is a refusal, not a charge.
- **Observed (measured, this run):** through the real `resolveOrgCardProcessor` (`server/services/customerMoneyRouting.ts`): healthy deps resolve to exactly `{ ok: true, processor: { accountId: "acct_SYNTHETIC_LANDLORD" } }` — the landlord's account, nothing else. No connected account → `processor_not_connected`, operator words "AcreOS never collects your customers' payments into its own account", counterparty words "No card was charged". Connected but `chargesEnabled: false` → **`processor_charges_disabled`** (the gap sheet's stuck moment, typed exactly as the sheet names it), operator words "isn't cleared to take charges yet — Stripe still needs some details. Finish onboarding in Settings → Billing". Charges enabled but `cardPayments: "pending"` → `card_capability_inactive`, naming the capability state. A throwing status check → `processor_check_failed` (fail-closed). Pinned in `buyHoldSupportDrills.test.ts`; supporting leg `customerMoneyRouting.test.ts` + `moneyCustodyHardStop.test.ts` (the structural no-custody half).
- **Verdict: PASS.** A correct support answer is producible for every stuck state, and the "just take it on your account" request is refused by construction — the resolver has no branch that could do it. The "rent charge not generated" sibling of this stuck moment is covered on the Pax path by `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts` (scheduled-charge generation through the one generator, duplicate-skipping, one receipt) and on the ledger by `rentLedgerLateFeeNoStacking.test.ts`'s scheduled-generation legs, both in the battery below.

## Drill 4 — The PII fence: tenant identity is reachable ONLY through the scoped tenants tool — PASS (deterministic legs)

- **Seeded state (synthetic, local):** none (source + registry pins at this head, plus the executor legs in `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts`).
- **Ticket (as designed):** "support told me my tenant's phone number from the rent roll" / "just read me the tenant's details off the lease."
- **Correctness rule:** tenant names/contact exist in exactly one tool's shape (`get_tenants`), gated by the `tenant_pii_read` PII scope; every other tool in the family carries zero tenant name/contact fields; even the tenants tool never shapes date of birth, government-id fragments or screening fields; and no tool in the family can send anything to a tenant (FD5).
- **Observed (measured, this run):** (1) **Scope registry** — `INTENT_META` pins `get_tenants` to `{ door: "finance", scope: "tenant_pii_read" }`, the other four reads to `financial_read`, and both record changes to `financial_write`. (2) **Shaping** — on the comment-stripped source of `server/ai/buyHoldTools.ts`, `from(tenants)` occurs **exactly once**, inside `paxGetTenants`; the bodies of `paxGetRentRoll`, `paxGetRentalLeases`, `paxGetLeaseLedger`, `paxGetRentalUnderwritingDecision`, `paxRecordRentPayment` and `paxGenerateScheduledRentCharges` match **none** of `firstName | lastName | displayName | email | phone` — leases and the rent roll carry unit labels and amounts, not people. (3) **Even the scoped tool is narrow** — `paxGetTenants`' body matches none of `dateOfBirth | governmentId | creditScore | eviction` (its coverage sentence mentions screening results only to say they are never shown). (4) **FD5** — no tool name in the family matches `send | mail | notify | remind`. Pinned in `buyHoldSupportDrills.test.ts`. Supporting legs (executor-level, in the battery): `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts` proves a caller without `tenant_pii_read` is refused by the PII scope gate **even unidentified**, with zero queries run, and that a shaped tenant record carrying DOB/ID/screening values in the row returns none of them.
- **Verdict: PASS.** The fence a support answer must describe — "tenant contact details live behind a permission-scoped tool; the rent roll and ledger never carry them" — is the product's actual shape, at the registry, the shaping, and the executor layers.

## Drill 5 — Data-wrong — PASS (both legs)

### 5a — Late posture / late-fee stacking ("the fee keeps growing every time I look")

- **Seeded state (synthetic, local):** the `rentLedgerLateFeeNoStacking` fixture's own rule — TX, 10% small-property cap on $1,400.00 rent, $50 initial + $5/day after a 2-day grace, 40 days late → the rule's TOTAL for the charge is $140.00.
- **Correctness rule:** a second proposal on the same charge proposes only what remains of the rule's total; at the total, nothing further is proposed; the explanation names what is already on the charge.
- **Observed (measured, this run):** through the real `proposeLateFee` + `netOfLateFeesAlreadyCharged` (`@shared/rental/lateFeeProposal` — the pair the rent-ledger route composes): the fixture proposal is `proposed` at `14_000`¢; netted against $50 already charged it proposes **$90** with the explanation naming "$50.00 in late fees is already on this charge"; netted against the full $140 it flips to status `already_charged` with `proposedFeeCents: 0`. Pinned in `buyHoldSupportDrills.test.ts`; supporting leg `rentLedgerLateFeeNoStacking.test.ts` (the route-level apply legs).
- **Verdict: PASS.**

### 5b — "Occupancy shows everything paid/occupied with nothing entered"

- **Seeded state (synthetic, local):** an account with no rentable units (the empty-roll state).
- **Correctness rule:** an empty rent roll is reported as empty — occupancy is never an invented percentage, and nothing is described as fully paid or fully occupied.
- **Observed (measured, this run):** the rent-roll tool computes `occupancyPct: rentable.length === 0 ? null : …` (pinned on the source in `buyHoldSupportDrills.test.ts`), and its coverage words — the words a Pax/support answer quotes — state "An empty rent roll is reported as empty, never as fully paid or fully occupied." The executor leg in `paxBuyHoldTools.test.ts` runs the empty account through the real tool and returns `occupancyPct: null` with zeroed counts; the page-level pin is `rentRollEmptyIsNotPaidInFull.test.tsx` (an empty aging response never renders "paid in full"). Both supporting legs are in the battery below.
- **Verdict: PASS.**

- Severity Medium: a proof/capability gap on the support path, not a demonstrated mis-resolution (no live reply was observed). Same shape and severity as N9-DEF-1 and T9-DEF-1.
- Census note: a cure that registers these reads for the resolver will touch the support dispatch surface; per the standing gate, that cure lane must keep `purposeLedgerCensus` + `refusalsArePlainWords` green. This lane touches no dispatch code and adds no census entries.
