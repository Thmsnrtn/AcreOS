# Competitor Profile: Pebble REI

**Last updated:** 2026-04-15
**Category:** Direct land-investment SaaS (CRM + operations platform)
**Website:** pebblerei.com (formerly pebbleland.com / joinpebble.com)

---

## Company Overview

Pebble REI is a vertically integrated CRM and operations platform built exclusively for land investors. It was created by land investors who experienced the operational pain of managing deals across spreadsheets and disconnected tools, and turned that frustration into a product.

- **Founded:** ~2020 (announced early 2020, launched mid-2020; evolved from REI Conversion, a website agency for REI professionals)
- **Headquarters:** Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada
- **Founders:** Jessey Kwong (design/marketing background, previously founded REI Conversion) and Kevin Rockwood (developer and active land investor based in South Korea)
- **Team size:** 11-50 employees (per Crunchbase/LinkedIn)
- **Funding:** Appears bootstrapped; no disclosed venture rounds. Co-founders built it during the pandemic while running their own land deals.
- **Revenue:** Estimated $11M-$100M range (Crunchbase estimate; likely mid-to-low end given team size and niche)
- **Growth claim:** Described as "one of real estate's fastest-growing CRM platforms" with a "7-figure" land investing operation behind the founders

---

## Target Customer

- **Primary:** Established land investors who want to systematize and scale acquisitions (not beginners on a tight budget)
- **Secondary:** Small land investing teams (2-6 people) including virtual assistants
- **Persona:** Someone doing 5+ deals/month who has outgrown spreadsheets and generic CRMs, running direct mail campaigns, and managing a pipeline of acquisition-to-disposition deals
- **Not ideal for:** Side hustlers or early-stage investors with limited working capital (pricing is a barrier)

## Core Features (Detailed)

### Property Data Management
- Property-centric architecture (everything organized around the parcel, not the contact)
- Bulk import from any data provider without formatting requirements
- Handles 100,000+ property records without performance degradation
- Additional parcel data enrichment at $0.05/record
- Instant address verification
- Duplicate owner grouping
- Partial address support (city, zip, or APN only)

### Direct Mail Engine
- Native print-and-mail integration (no external mail house needed)
- Letter and postcard template library with document editor
- Dynamic placeholders that merge property-specific data
- Drip campaigns with configurable daily send rates
- Satellite imagery auto-included in mailers (from GPS coordinates, no extra cost)
- Campaign ROI tracking (cost per lead, which mailer worked)
- Neighbor letter marketing
- Do Not Mail filters

### Communication (Unified Inbox)
- Built-in VoIP calling and SMS from desktop and mobile
- Call recording with AI transcription and summaries
- Voicemail management
- Email integration
- Unified inbox across all channels (calls, texts, emails, direct mail)
- Multiple phone numbers per plan (1-6+ depending on tier)

### AI / Pebble Intelligence
- **Intake Agent:** AI-powered call handler that greets first-time callers, asks qualifying questions, and auto-creates deals when selling interest is detected. $0.99 per deal created. Supports multiple simultaneous calls. Provides transcripts, recordings, and handoff summaries.
- AI-generated call summaries
- Voice option customization for the Intake Agent (added Dec 2025)

### Deal Management
- Visual drag-and-drop deal board with customizable stages
- Seller lead inbox and deal board
- Buyer lead inbox and deal board (Growth+ plans)
- Inventory board (Growth+ plans)
- Acquisition manager board (Scale plan)
- Deal automations and checklists
- Threaded deal notes with pinning, filtering by author, keyword search
- Separate activity feed (stage changes, system events) from conversation notes
- Due diligence tabs within deals
- Document generation (letters, offers, deeds, contracts) with one-click creation
- Custom due diligence workflows

### Automation & Follow-Up
- Customizable action plans (sequences mixing emails, texts, mailers)
- Auto follow-up templates
- Task assignment with deadlines and team member routing
- Overdue task filtering
- Zapier integration for extending automation beyond native capabilities

### Websites (Add-Ons)
- **LandLeads** ($59-69/mo): Seller lead capture website; submissions auto-register in Pebble
- **LandList** ($59-69/mo): Buyer listing website; one-click property push from Pebble, payment processor integration for deposits

### Mobile Apps
- iOS app (requires iOS 15.1+) with native calling interface
- Android app (launched July 2025)
- Call/receive from mobile devices

### Integrations
- DataTree (property data)
- HubSpot
- MailerLite
- Zapier
- Chrome Extension ("Pebble Pricer") for finding comps on real estate marketplaces

### Team & Collaboration
- Multi-user access with role-based permissions
- VA management capabilities
- Individual notifications
- Project boards for complex activities (subdividing, developing, marketing)

## Strengths

1. **Purpose-built for land:** Property-centric architecture mirrors how land investors actually think (parcel-first, not contact-first). Every feature is designed around the land deal lifecycle.
2. **All-in-one value proposition:** Direct mail, VoIP, SMS, email, deal management, document generation, and websites in one platform. Competitors require 3-5 separate tools to match.
3. **Direct mail excellence:** Native print-and-mail with satellite imagery, drip campaigns, ROI tracking, and template library is best-in-class for the niche.
4. **Founders are practitioners:** Built by active land investors (Kevin still invests from South Korea), so product decisions reflect real workflows.
5. **Strong onboarding and support:** Live chat, email, Zoom calls, 1:1 onboarding, weekly Q&A office hours. Users consistently praise support quality.
6. **Active development cadence:** Monthly/quarterly feature releases (Q1 2026 shipped threaded notes, satellite mail images, campaign import simplification, action plan search, etc.).
7. **AI Intake Agent:** Novel feature that auto-qualifies inbound seller calls and creates deals at $0.99/deal -- solves a real pain point for investors who miss calls.
8. **Community and brand loyalty:** Strong word-of-mouth in the land investing community; endorsed by prominent educators and podcasters.
9. **Mobile apps:** Both iOS and Android coverage for field work.
10. **Clean, modern UI:** Repeatedly praised as beautifully designed and intuitive compared to clunky competitors like Investment Dominator.

---

## Weaknesses

1. **Price barrier for beginners:** Growth plan at $227-248/mo (the first plan with deal automations and Zapier) is expensive for new investors. The Free tier is extremely limited (10 deals, 2K records, no phone). Starter at $147-160/mo still caps deals at 50.
2. **No native seller financing / note management:** No amortization schedules, payment tracking, note portfolio management, or loan servicing tools. Seller financing is the dominant exit strategy in land, yet Pebble treats it as a generic "deal closed" event.
3. **No built-in skip tracing or deep data enrichment:** Relies on users importing data from external providers (DataTree, etc.). No native property enrichment pipeline, no multi-provider fallback, no credit-based lookup system.
4. **Limited comps / valuation tooling:** The "Pebble Pricer" is just a Chrome extension for manually browsing marketplaces. No automated comp analysis, no algorithmic pricing, no blind offer calculation engine.
5. **Template rigidity:** Cannot upload or use document templates created in third-party tools. All templates must be built within Pebble's editor, frustrating users with existing workflows.
6. **No native follow-up sequences (until Growth plan):** Starter users must rely on Zapier workarounds for automated follow-ups, which adds cost and complexity.
7. **Disposition is an afterthought:** Buyer management and the inventory board are only available on Growth+. The company itself recommends pairing Pebble with a separate disposition tool (e.g., Landydandy) for the selling half of the business.
8. **No built-in marketplace or listing syndication:** LandList is an add-on website builder, not a marketplace. No syndication to Zillow, LandWatch, Lands of America, etc.
9. **No regulatory intelligence:** No zoning data, no wetlands/flood risk analysis beyond basic mapping, no entitlement tracking.
10. **AI is narrow:** Intake Agent is clever but limited to inbound call qualification. No AI-powered deal underwriting, offer calculation, negotiation, or market prediction.
11. **Scaling costs add up:** A team of 6 on Scale ($387/mo) + both websites ($118/mo) + phone overages + data enrichment = $500+/mo before mail costs.

## Key Differentiator to Watch

**Pebble's AI trajectory.** Today, Pebble Intelligence is limited to the Intake Agent (inbound call qualification). But the "growing suite of tools" language and active monthly releases signal they are investing in AI expansion. If Pebble adds AI-powered offer pricing, automated comp analysis, or seller financing management, it would erode AcreOS's most important advantages. Their December 2025 and Q1 2026 updates show a team shipping fast.

**The seller financing gap is our biggest moat.** Pebble is built for the "buy low, flip fast" land investor. It has no tools for structuring notes, tracking payments, managing a portfolio of seller-financed deals, or servicing loans. This is an enormous blind spot given that seller financing is the primary profit driver for a large segment of the land investing market. AcreOS's Financial OS (amortization, payment schedules, note portfolio) addresses a workflow that Pebble users currently manage in spreadsheets or separate accounting software. Every month Pebble ignores this gap is a month we can acquire their most sophisticated customers.

**Secondary differentiator: data intelligence.** Pebble is a workflow tool that depends on users bringing their own data. AcreOS's multi-provider enrichment pipeline, automated valuations, and AI-driven lead scoring turn raw property records into actionable intelligence inside the platform. As land investing gets more competitive, the investors who win will be the ones with better data -- not just better mail campaigns.
