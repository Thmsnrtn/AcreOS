# Competitive Analysis: Privy (privy.pro)

**Category:** Real estate deal-finding and investment analysis platform
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

---

## Company Overview

Privy (officially Privy Pro, operated by RE Data Labs Inc.) is a real estate investment software platform headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Founded by Scott Fahl, the company has positioned itself as the only platform giving non-agent investors direct access to first-party MLS data -- data traditionally reserved for licensed real estate agents.

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | 2019 (some sources cite earlier roots to 2011-2014) |
| **Founder/CEO** | Scott Fahl |
| **Headquarters** | Denver, CO |
| **Team size** | ~11-50 employees (estimated 9-15 core team) |
| **Funding** | $4M non-dilutive growth capital from Cypress Growth Capital (February 2024) |
| **Funding type** | Royalty-based (preserves founder equity and control) |
| **Notable team** | Brittany Carcova (Dir. of Marketing), Natalie Bernacchi (Dir. of Customer Success) |

Privy claims 1,000%+ customer base growth over a two-year period leading up to its 2024 funding round. The company operates as a bootstrapped-then-revenue-funded business with a single known capital infusion.

---

## Target Customer

Privy serves three primary audiences:

1. **Real estate investors** -- Wholesalers, fix-and-flip operators, and buy-and-hold investors who need fast deal analysis and comps. This is the core audience.
2. **Licensed real estate agents** -- Agents who work with investor clients and want to be matched with active investors in their market.
3. **Lenders** -- Private and mortgage lenders who want to automate borrower acquisition and retention by integrating deal data into the lending workflow.

The platform skews toward **experienced investors** who already understand deal analysis fundamentals. Beginners are often warned that Privy's feature set can be overwhelming without foundational knowledge.

**Geographic focus:** U.S. residential real estate markets. Direct MLS feeds in 150+ major metros covering ~80% of all listings. Third-party data fills gaps for ~98% total U.S. coverage.

## Core Features

### Deal Finding and Analysis
- **Live MLS data** -- Real-time feeds updated every 15 minutes in supported markets (direct MLS agreements in 150+ markets)
- **LiveCMA** -- Proprietary tool that analyzes investment potential using before-and-after data, photos, and timelines on comps and historical flips
- **Automated deal analysis** -- Calculates ARV (after-repair value), profit margins, and fix-and-flip potential automatically against preset investment criteria
- **Investment filters** -- Custom searches by equity, price, property type, investor activity, and deal type (flip, LTR, STR)
- **Deal alerts** -- Real-time notifications when properties matching user criteria hit the market

### Comping and Valuation
- **Investor comps** -- Side-by-side before/after valuations from completed investor deals (not just standard MLS comps)
- **CMA Selector** -- Analyze MLS and off-market listings as teardown deals, flips, or rentals side-by-side
- **Rental analysis** -- Integrated LTR calculator computing cap rate, cash-on-cash return, and net cash flow
- **Public records integration** -- Liens, auctions, tax assessments, deeds, and ownership history

### Investor Intelligence
- **Investor activity heatmap** -- Visual mapping of where successful investors are buying
- **Strategy replication** -- Track deals from successful investors and surface similar opportunities automatically
- **Lead scoring** -- Proprietary algorithm to rank leads by deal quality

### CRM and Pipeline
- **Lead tracking CRM** -- Basic pipeline management with follow-up reminders
- **Client activity tracking** -- For agents managing investor client relationships
- **Bulk list uploads** -- Import and analyze external property lists

### Agent and Lender Tools
- **Preferred Agent placement** -- Featured listing for agents within MLS coverage areas
- **Lender integration** -- Partnerships with loan origination software (LOS) for seamless deal-to-loan workflows
- **Email blasts and branding** -- Agent Pro plan includes marketing tools

### Education and Support
- **Live training sessions** -- Twice weekly
- **Learning center** -- On-demand demos, guides, and webinars
- **On-demand demo** (no free trial available)

## Pricing

Privy uses a tiered model based on user type and geographic scope. Prices have shifted over time; the most current structure (as of early 2026):

### Investor Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 State Access** | ~$79/mo | ~$53/mo | Single state, MLS + public records |
| **3 State Access** | ~$109/mo | ~$73/mo | Three states |
| **National Access** | ~$149/mo | ~$119/mo | All 50 states, 98% property coverage |

### Agent Plan

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Agent** | ~$37/mo | ~$25/mo | Licensed agents only (requires active MLS ID) |

**Key pricing notes:**
- Annual billing saves ~33% on investor plans
- No free trial -- only an on-demand demo walkthrough
- No money-back guarantee reported
- State selection on 1-state and 3-state plans is locked for the subscription duration
- Promotional codes occasionally available (e.g., 30% off annual, 20% off first month)

## Strengths

1. **Direct MLS access for non-agents** -- This is Privy's primary moat. Unlike PropStream (public records only) or BatchLeads (skip tracing focus), Privy gives investors the same first-party MLS data that agents see. 15-minute update cycles are significantly faster than third-party aggregators.

2. **LiveCMA is genuinely differentiated** -- The before-and-after comparison tool with photos, renovation timelines, and investor comps is unique in the market. It lets users see exactly what successful flippers paid, spent, and sold for.

3. **Investor activity intelligence** -- Heatmaps and strategy replication features give users a "follow the smart money" capability that competitors lack.

4. **Strong comping engine** -- Multiple comp types (standard, investor, rental) with side-by-side analysis streamline underwriting that would otherwise require multiple tools.

5. **Affordable agent plan** -- At $25-37/month, the agent tier is a low-cost entry point that builds a network effect (more agents = more investor matches).

6. **Non-dilutive funding model** -- Cypress Growth Capital's royalty-based structure means the company is not under VC pressure to hyper-grow at the expense of product quality.

7. **150+ direct MLS feeds** -- Broad coverage in major metros makes it viable for most urban and suburban investors.

---

## Weaknesses

1. **No free trial** -- Forcing users to commit without hands-on testing is a significant conversion barrier, especially given the price point. Only an on-demand demo is available.

2. **Billing and cancellation complaints** -- This is the most damaging issue in user reviews. Reports of double charges, difficulty canceling, and continued billing post-cancellation appear across BiggerPockets, Capterra, and Software Advice. Only 33% of users recommend the platform on some review sites, with a 2.3/5 user experience rating cited.

3. **Limited integrations** -- No public API. No confirmed Zapier integration for the real estate product (Zapier results often conflate with the unrelated ecommerce "Privy" product). Limited third-party connectivity forces manual workflows.

4. **Residential-only, house-centric** -- Privy is built around residential homes (SFR, multifamily). There is **no meaningful support for vacant land, raw acreage, or seller-financed note investing**. Land investors are not a target audience.

5. **MLS coverage gaps** -- Rural states (Wyoming, Alaska, etc.) and some secondary markets rely on third-party data with reduced freshness and detail. The "98% coverage" claim uses third-party backfill that is materially lower quality than direct MLS feeds.

6. **State-locked subscriptions** -- The 1-state and 3-state plans lock your geography for the entire subscription period. Investors exploring new markets must upgrade rather than swap.

7. **Steep learning curve** -- Multiple reviews note that the platform is overwhelming for beginners. The feature density requires significant onboarding investment.

8. **Basic CRM** -- The built-in lead tracking is rudimentary compared to dedicated CRM platforms. No workflow automation, no drip campaigns, no multi-channel outreach (direct mail, SMS, email sequences).

9. **No skip tracing or marketing tools** -- Unlike PropStream/BatchLeads, Privy cannot find owner contact info or execute outbound marketing campaigns. It is an analysis tool, not an acquisition tool.

## Key Differentiator to Watch

**Privy's MLS data moat is real but narrow.** Their competitive advantage is built entirely on MLS data access agreements -- something that took years to establish and that competitors struggle to replicate. However, this advantage is limited to residential properties and has no relevance to the land, seller financing, and note-management market that AcreOS serves.

**The strategic risk is adjacency, not direct competition.** Privy could theoretically expand into land or seller financing, but their entire product architecture, data infrastructure, and customer base are house-centric. The more likely competitive threat is if Privy's CRM improves substantially and begins to capture the "full lifecycle" investor who does both houses and land.

**Watch for:** Privy expanding beyond deal-finding into deal execution (outbound marketing, skip tracing, full CRM). If they add these capabilities -- especially through acquisitions or partnerships -- they could become a more complete platform that competes for the same "I need one tool to run my investing business" buyer that AcreOS targets. The PropStream/BatchLeads merger (July 2025) is consolidating the residential REI software market, which may push Privy to differentiate by expanding vertically into underserved niches like land.

**Bottom line:** Privy and AcreOS are not direct competitors today. They serve different property types (residential vs. land) and different workflow stages (analysis vs. full lifecycle CRM). The overlap exists only if an investor does both houses and land, in which case they would likely use both tools. AcreOS should position against Privy by emphasizing its end-to-end land investing workflow, seller financing capabilities, and robust CRM -- none of which Privy offers or is likely to build in the near term.
