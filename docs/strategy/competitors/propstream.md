# PropStream -- Competitive Analysis

**Category:** Real estate data + analytics + lead generation
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

---

## Company Overview

PropStream is an all-in-one real estate data intelligence and lead generation platform covering 160M+ U.S. properties. Originally founded in 2006 (some sources say 2007) by Rob Zahr, the company started selling real estate data on CD-ROMs and evolved into a full SaaS platform.

- **Parent company:** Stewart Information Services Corporation (NYSE: STC), which acquired PropStream in November 2021 for approximately $175 million.
- **Current CEO:** Brian Tepfer (Rob Zahr remains as Founder).
- **Employee count:** Approximately 80 employees across 3 continents (North America, Asia, Europe) as of early 2026. Estimates vary by source (48 per PitchBook, 80 per company claims post-BatchLeads acquisition).
- **Funding:** Bootstrapped prior to acquisition. No external venture funding raised. Now operates as a subsidiary of Stewart (market cap ~$1.8B, ~$2.5B annual revenue).
- **Revenue target:** PropStream has publicly stated ambitions to become a $100M brand; exact current revenue not publicly disclosed.
- **Key acquisition (July 2025):** Acquired BatchLeads and BatchDialer to unify property data, AI-powered lead generation, and outreach dialing into a single platform.
- **Recognition:** HousingWire Tech100 honoree for six consecutive years (2021-2026).

---

## Target Customer

PropStream serves a broad swath of real estate professionals, but its sweet spot is **active real estate investors** who need to find off-market deals at scale:

| Segment | Use Case |
|---------|----------|
| **Wholesalers** | Primary audience. List building, skip tracing, and cold outreach to find motivated sellers. |
| **Fix-and-flip investors** | Property valuation (comps), rehab estimation, identifying distressed properties. |
| **Buy-and-hold investors** | Market analysis, rental comps, absentee owner identification. |
| **Real estate agents** | Prospecting FSBOs, pre-foreclosures, expired listings, investor clients. |
| **Lenders / loan originators** | Filtering by loan type and mortgage history to match lending products. |
| **Appraisers / contractors** | Property data lookup and valuation research. |

**Notable gap:** PropStream does not specifically target or optimize for **land investors**, **seller financing operators**, or **note investors** -- the segments AcreOS is purpose-built for.

## Core Features

### Property Data & Search
- Nationwide database of 160M+ property records (public records, tax assessor data, MLS)
- 165+ search filters organized by property characteristics, MLS info, owner info, financial data, distress indicators
- 20 pre-built lead lists (pre-foreclosures, tax liens, bankruptcies, divorces, high equity, absentee owners, etc.)
- List stacking (layering multiple filters to narrow prospects)
- County-level and ZIP-level search with map-based drawing tools
- MLS integration with active, pending, and sold data (where available)
- Non-disclosure state handling with estimated sale prices

### Analytics & Valuation
- Comparable sales analysis (automated comps from public records + MLS)
- ARV (After Repair Value) estimates
- Rehab/renovation cost calculator (powered by BlueHammer, itemized by project area)
- ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) calculator
- Heat maps for market trends (property values, foreclosure rates, rent prices, demographic data)
- PropStream Intelligence Chat -- AI-powered assistant for property-level Q&A

### Skip Tracing & Contact Data
- Built-in skip tracing for owner phone numbers and emails
- DNC (Do Not Call) flagging and litigator scrubbing for compliance
- Enhanced skip tracing ranks contacts by likelihood of connection
- Free with Pro and Elite plans; per-record cost on Essentials

### Marketing & Outreach
- Direct mail postcards (from $0.48 each)
- Email campaigns ($0.02 per message)
- Ringless voicemail drops
- Custom landing page builder
- Integrated dialer (via BatchDialer acquisition) with campaign management
- Click-to-dial calling
- Marketing campaign tracking

### Team & Automation
- Lead Automator: auto-refreshing lists that update daily based on saved criteria (monitors 50K-120K properties depending on plan)
- Team member seats with customizable permissions (2-9 included depending on plan, additional at $30/mo each)
- Mobile app (iOS and Android)

### AI Features
- PropStream Intelligence Chat for property-level research questions
- Predictive analytics and AI-driven lead scoring (marketed but details sparse)
- BatchLeads AI tools (being integrated post-acquisition)

## Pricing

PropStream restructured pricing in June 2025 into three tiers:

| | Essentials | Pro | Elite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | $99/mo | $199/mo | $699/mo |
| **Annual** | $81/mo ($972/yr) | $165/mo ($1,980/yr) | $583/mo ($6,996/yr) |
| **Saves/Exports** | 25,000/mo | 50,000/mo | 100,000/mo |
| **Team members** | Up to 5 add-on ($30/mo each) | 2 included | 9 included |
| **Skip tracing** | Paid add-on (~$0.12/record) | Free (included) | Free (included) |
| **Lead Automator** | Add-on ($27/mo) | Included (50K properties) | Included (120K properties) |
| **Click-to-dial** | 2 months free trial | Included | Included |
| **Marketing credit** | $100 bonus | $150 bonus | $150 bonus |

**Key add-on costs:**
- Postcards: from $0.48 each
- Email sends: $0.02 each
- Ringless voicemail: ~$0.10 each
- Additional team members: $30/mo each
- BatchDialer: 20% lifetime discount (separate subscription)

**Free trial:** 7 days with 50 free leads on all plans.

**Hidden cost reality:** Active wholesalers report that the base subscription is just the starting point. Skip tracing volume, direct mail, and email sends can push monthly costs well above the base price, especially on the Essentials plan.

## Strengths

1. **Massive data coverage:** 160M+ properties nationwide is among the largest datasets available. Single platform replaces multiple data subscriptions.
2. **Filter depth:** 165+ filters and 20 pre-built lists let users build hyper-targeted lead lists quickly. List stacking is a standout feature for wholesalers.
3. **All-in-one lead gen + outreach:** Data discovery, skip tracing, dialing, email, and direct mail in one platform (especially post-BatchDialer acquisition) reduces tool fragmentation.
4. **Brand recognition:** Six consecutive HousingWire Tech100 awards. Widely recommended in wholesaling communities and YouTube education channels. Strong word-of-mouth.
5. **Stewart backing:** Acquisition by a $2.5B revenue title company provides financial stability, data partnerships, and credibility.
6. **Compliance features:** DNC flagging and litigator scrubbing built into skip tracing reduces legal risk for cold-calling operations.
7. **Heat maps and market analysis:** Visual market intelligence is well-executed and useful for identifying opportunity zones.
8. **Mobile app:** Functional companion app for on-the-go research, which many competitors lack.
9. **Pricing flexibility:** Three tiers allow entry at $99/mo, which is accessible for solo investors.

---

## Weaknesses

1. **Skip tracing data quality:** Consistently the top complaint across Trustpilot, BiggerPockets, and review sites. Users report high rates of disconnected numbers, wrong numbers, and missing contact info. One reviewer: "don't skip trace there -- lots of old numbers, wrong numbers, disconnected numbers."

2. **Not a real CRM:** PropStream is a research and lead generation engine, not a deal management system. There is no pipeline management, no deal stages, no task assignment, no automated follow-up sequences, and no centralized activity logging. Users universally report needing a separate CRM (REsimpli, Podio, etc.) to actually work leads.

3. **No seller-intent scoring:** The platform provides data but does not prioritize leads by likelihood of selling. As one reviewer put it: "You get the data, not the hand-holding." Users must build their own rules for which contacts to call first.

4. **Data accuracy issues:** ARV estimates described as "accurate about half the time." Pre-foreclosure lists sometimes include properties not actually in foreclosure. Public records lag is a known issue, especially in counties with infrequent updates.

5. **Vacant land data limitations:** Tax assessor data for vacant land is updated infrequently (annually in most states, every two years in Colorado). Rural property data is thinner than urban/suburban. Vacant property flags are "extremely unreliable" per industry analysis.

6. **Cost escalation:** Base pricing looks affordable but active users accumulate significant costs from skip tracing, postcards, email sends, and add-ons. The true monthly cost for active wholesalers can easily reach $300-500+.

7. **No seller financing / note management tools:** Zero capabilities for loan origination, payment tracking, amortization schedules, note portfolio management, or borrower communication. PropStream explicitly recommends third-party loan servicers for this.

8. **Learning curve:** Multiple reviewers note the UI can be overwhelming. Help documentation relies on video tutorials without search functionality. Font sizes are small with no adjustment options.

9. **Integration limitations:** No native CRM integrations. Data export is CSV-based (manual import/export to other systems). No API for automated workflows.

10. **Non-disclosure state gaps:** In 12 non-disclosure states (TX, UT, MT, etc.), sale prices are estimated rather than actual, reducing reliability for comps and valuations.

11. **BatchLeads/BatchDialer integration incomplete:** As of early 2026, the acquired products still operate as separate platforms. The unified experience is a roadmap item, not a reality.

## Key Differentiator to Watch

**PropStream's AI investment is the primary threat vector.** The PropStream Intelligence Chat and their stated AI roadmap signal a move toward automated lead scoring and seller-intent prediction -- exactly the capability gap users complain about most. If PropStream successfully ships AI-driven lead prioritization that tells users "call these 50 first," it closes a major weakness and makes the platform stickier.

However, PropStream's fundamental architecture is **horizontal** (serve all real estate professionals) while AcreOS is **vertical** (serve land investors and seller financing operators deeply). PropStream is unlikely to build dedicated seller financing workflows, note management, or land-specific analytics because those features serve a fraction of their user base. This creates a durable moat for AcreOS in its target segments.

**The realistic competitive scenario is not PropStream vs. AcreOS head-to-head, but PropStream + AcreOS coexistence.** Many AcreOS users will also subscribe to PropStream for its data breadth and lead lists, then manage deals and notes in AcreOS. The strategic play is to integrate cleanly with PropStream (CSV import at minimum, API if available) so AcreOS becomes the natural "next step" after PropStream generates the lead.

**Watch for:** PropStream adding basic CRM features (deal stages, follow-up automation) that could reduce users' motivation to adopt a second tool. Any move by PropStream into seller financing or note servicing would be a direct competitive escalation, but this remains unlikely given their horizontal strategy.
