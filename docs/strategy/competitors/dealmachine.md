# Competitor Analysis: DealMachine

**Category:** Real estate lead generation + direct mail
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

---

## Company Overview

DealMachine is a mobile-first real estate investing platform that combines lead generation, skip tracing, direct mail marketing, and CRM into a single subscription. Founded in 2017 by David Lecko in Indianapolis, IN, the company was born from a personal pain point: Lecko, a software developer and part-time investor, was manually writing down addresses while driving for dollars and lost a deal because of missed follow-ups. He built the first version of DealMachine to capture property information, look up owner details, and trigger direct mail — all from his phone.

The company grew rapidly, ranking #36 on the Inc. 5000 list with 8,385% revenue growth between 2017 and 2020. As of mid-2025, DealMachine reports approximately $1.8M in annual revenue, ~28 employees across 3 continents, and over 250,000 registered users. Headquarters are at 456 N Meridian St, Indianapolis, IN.

**Funding:** Seed VC round from Vision Ventures (exact amount undisclosed). The company appears to be largely bootstrapped/revenue-funded beyond the seed round.

**Key leadership:** David Lecko (Founder/CEO).

## Target Customer

DealMachine primarily serves:

- **Wholesalers** — Their core user. Investors who find distressed properties, put them under contract at a discount, and assign or double-close the contract to a cash buyer.
- **House flippers** — Investors looking for off-market distressed houses to rehab and resell.
- **BRRRR investors** — Buy, Rehab, Rent, Refinance, Repeat strategy users looking for below-market acquisitions.
- **Real estate agents** — FSBOs, pre-foreclosures, and expired listing prospecting.
- **Small acquisition teams** — 2-6 person teams running driving-for-dollars operations in multiple markets.

**Notably absent from their target customer profile:** land investors, seller-financing operators, and note holders. DealMachine is built around the house/structure paradigm — distressed houses, rehab calculators, ARV (after-repair value) analysis. Vacant land is an afterthought.

## Core Features

### Lead Generation

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Driving for Dollars (D4D)** | Mobile app with GPS tracking. Point your phone at a property to instantly pull owner info. Tracks routes to prevent duplicate coverage. Supports team route coordination. |
| **Virtual Driving for Dollars** | Desktop-based D4D using satellite imagery to identify distressed properties from home. |
| **List Builder** | 150M+ property database with 700+ filters. Filters include foreclosure status, absentee ownership, equity percentage, owner demographics, and more. |
| **Pre-built lists** | Foreclosures, pre-foreclosures, bank-owned (REO), tax-delinquent, vacant, absentee owner, high equity. |

### Skip Tracing

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Unlimited contact info** | Phone numbers, email addresses, mailing addresses for every property/owner in the US. Included in all plan tiers — no per-lead fee. |
| **Match rate** | Self-reported 87% skip-trace match rate, 95% coverage for single-family homes. |
| **Data freshness** | Updated monthly. Color-coded phone identification (mobile vs. landline vs. VoIP). |
| **Accuracy caveat** | Email accuracy reported at ~10%. Phone accuracy ~80%. Accuracy degrades significantly in rural areas. |

### Outreach & Marketing

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Automated direct mail** | Multi-touch mail sequences with automated follow-ups. Templates include handwritten-style letters. Property photos auto-included on mailers. |
| **Mail pricing** | $0.57–$0.67 per piece depending on plan tier. |
| **AI-Powered Dialer** | Auto-dialer with local presence (caller ID matches recipient's area code). Live call transcription. AI-suggested responses during calls. |
| **AI Voicemail Drop** | Voice cloning — user records voice samples, AI leaves personalized voicemails in the user's cloned voice. |
| **SMS & Email** | Integrated texting and email outreach (added more recently alongside postcards). |
| **Ringless voicemail** | Drops voicemails without ringing the recipient's phone. |

### CRM & Pipeline

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Deal pipeline** | Basic pipeline manager with customizable statuses, notes, and team mentions. |
| **Lead management** | Photos, tasks, reminders, notes per lead. Bulk contact upload. |
| **Marketing calendar** | Visual timeline of outreach sequences and follow-ups. |
| **Custom fields** | Available on Pro Plus (25 fields). Not available on lower tiers. |
| **ROI tracking** | Basic campaign ROI tracking. |

### AI Features

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Alma (AI assistant)** | Powered by OpenAI. Analyzes deals by investment strategy (wholesale/flip/buy-and-hold). Generates cold call scripts. Estimates property values and rental prices. Answers general investing questions. |
| **AI call coaching** | Real-time suggested responses during live calls. Sentiment analysis on call outcomes. |
| **Follow-up prioritization** | AI sorts and prioritizes leads based on call outcomes and engagement signals. |

### Data & Analysis

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **MLS comps** | MLS and county-sourced comparable sales data. |
| **Rehab calculator** | Repair cost estimation and ARV determination. |
| **Property data** | Ownership info, mortgage details, tax records, equity estimates. |
| **Foreclosure data** | Pre-foreclosures, auctions, bank-owned properties. |

### Integrations

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Zapier** | Core integration method. Triggers on new lead or status change. Actions include adding leads, tagging, notes, custom field updates. |
| **API limitation** | Contact info (phone/email) is NOT available via Zapier or API — only property data (addresses, owner names). |
| **CRM forwarding** | Can push leads to external CRMs via Zapier (Follow Up Boss, Podio, etc.). |

## Strengths

1. **Best-in-class Driving for Dollars.** The D4D feature is the product DealMachine was built around and it shows. GPS route tracking, team coordination, one-tap property lookup, and route history are polished and well-integrated. No competitor matches this specific workflow.

2. **Unlimited skip tracing included.** Eliminating per-lead skip tracing fees is a genuine differentiator. Competitors like PropStream and BatchLeads charge $0.10–$0.25 per trace. For high-volume users, this represents hundreds in monthly savings.

3. **Mobile-first design.** The app is built for field work. 4.8/5 stars from 5,000+ App Store reviews. GPS functionality is praised as "naturally intuitive."

4. **Integrated outreach stack.** Mail + dialer + SMS + email in one platform reduces tool sprawl for users who previously cobbled together 3-4 separate services.

5. **AI features are practical.** The AI dialer with local presence, live transcription, and voice cloning voicemail drops are genuinely useful for outbound-heavy operations. Alma provides decent deal analysis.

6. **Strong brand in wholesaling community.** Ranked on Inc. 5000. Large YouTube presence. 250K+ registered users creates network effects in wholesaling communities.

7. **Nationwide data coverage.** 150M+ properties across all 50 states with daily county record updates.

---

## Weaknesses

1. **Not a real CRM.** The CRM is bolted on, not purpose-built. It lacks advanced automation, multi-stage pipelines, custom objects, role-based permissions, and the depth that full CRM platforms offer. Users outgrow it quickly and need to export to a dedicated CRM via Zapier.

2. **No seller financing or note management.** Zero support for amortization schedules, payment tracking, note portfolios, buyer management, or any of the workflows that land investors and seller-financing operators need. This is a houses-only platform.

3. **No land-specific intelligence.** No flood zone data, soil quality, topography analysis, access verification, utility proximity, or environmental screening. The rehab calculator and ARV tools are useless for vacant land.

4. **Data accuracy problems in rural areas.** Multiple reviewers report that skip trace accuracy degrades significantly outside metro areas. Email accuracy is ~10% across the board. This is especially problematic for land investors, who operate heavily in rural counties.

5. **Hidden costs add up fast.** The subscription is the floor, not the ceiling. Direct mail, dialer minutes, voicemail drops, and auto-charged credit refills push real costs to $300-500+/month for active users. Multiple BBB complaints about unexpected charges.

6. **Billing and cancellation complaints.** Recurring pattern of users being charged after cancellation, difficulty getting refunds, yearly subscriptions activated without clear confirmation, and $50 auto-charges for dialer credits. BBB complaints are not resolved satisfactorily.

7. **No phone support.** Customer service is chat-only, staffed by a non-US-based team. Users report "consistent tech issues that often go unresolved" and "strategically avoided phone contact."

8. **API/integration limitations.** Contact information (the most valuable data) is not available through Zapier or the API. Only property addresses and owner names can be exported programmatically. This creates lock-in and makes CRM migration painful.

9. **Limited multi-channel drip campaigns.** Mail sequences exist, but there's no way to coordinate SMS, email, ringless voicemail, and direct mail in a single automated sequence. Each channel operates somewhat independently.

10. **No KPI tracking, accounting, or business operations.** No income/expense tracking, ROI calculation across the business, or operational dashboards beyond basic campaign metrics.

### Summary

DealMachine and AcreOS serve **overlapping but distinct markets**. DealMachine is built for house wholesalers and flippers who drive neighborhoods looking for distressed structures. AcreOS is built for land investors and seller-financing operators who work from data lists, send blind offers, and manage note portfolios.

The overlap is in lead management, outreach/campaigns, and deal pipeline tracking. In that overlap, AcreOS wins on CRM depth, pricing, and AI sophistication. DealMachine wins on outreach execution (dialer, mail fulfillment, skip tracing volume).

## Key Differentiator to Watch

**DealMachine's unlimited skip tracing model and AI dialer could expand into land.** If DealMachine adds land-specific filters to its List Builder (tax-delinquent vacant land, acreage ranges, rural county targeting) and improves its rural data accuracy, it could become a viable lead generation front-end that feeds into AcreOS. Alternatively, it could attempt to add basic seller-financing features and compete more directly.

The strategic risk is not that DealMachine replaces AcreOS — their CRM and financial tooling are too shallow for that. The risk is that DealMachine captures users at the top of the funnel (lead generation) and makes it hard for them to migrate to AcreOS later, thanks to the API restriction that blocks contact info exports. Users locked into DealMachine for lead gen may tolerate its weak CRM rather than manage two platforms.

**AcreOS's counter-move:** Ensure the lead import path from DealMachine (via Zapier) is seamless, and position AcreOS as the "operating system" that DealMachine feeds into — not a replacement for D4D, but the place where leads become deals, deals become notes, and notes become cash flow. The Land Credit Score and seller-financing module are features DealMachine cannot replicate without a fundamental product pivot.
