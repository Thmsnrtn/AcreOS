# Competitor Analysis: BatchLeads / BatchDialer (Batch Suite)

**Category:** Real estate lead generation, skip tracing, CRM, and cold calling
**Last updated:** 2026-04-15

---

## Company Overview

BatchLeads is a real estate investing software platform that combines lead generation, skip tracing, property data, marketing tools, and a lightweight CRM into a single product. It was co-founded in 2018 by Jesse Burrell (known as "the REI Data King"), Ivo Draginov, and Anny Draginova under the parent company BatchService. The founders were real estate investors frustrated by fragmented data and tooling.

BatchDialer is the companion power dialer product built for cold-calling property owners at scale, with AI coaching, sentiment analysis, and compliance features.

**Key facts:**

- **Founded:** 2018 (BatchService parent entity)
- **Headquarters:** Phoenix, AZ
- **Funding:** Bootstrapped -- no known venture funding raised
- **Revenue:** $5M (2019), $16.9M (2020), $30M (2021) per Latka
- **Customers:** 18,000+ as of 2021; current figures not publicly disclosed
- **Team size:** 10-19 employees (per ZoomInfo), though post-acquisition this may be larger under PropStream
- **Acquisition:** PropStream (a Stewart Information Services Corporation subsidiary) acquired BatchLeads and BatchDialer effective July 1, 2025. The products continue operating independently for now, with integration planned over time. BatchData (the API/data layer) remains a separate company.
- **Review ratings:** G2 lead generation score of 9.3/10; quality of support 9.5/10. However, BBB rating is F (failure to respond to complaints).

## Target Customer

BatchLeads primarily serves:

- **Real estate wholesalers** -- their core power user, running high-volume direct mail and cold calling campaigns to find off-market deals
- **Fix-and-flip investors** -- using comp tools and property filters to find undervalued properties
- **Real estate agents/brokers** -- prospecting for listings through distressed property identification
- **Buy-and-hold investors** -- identifying rental acquisition opportunities
- **Mortgage lenders/loan officers** -- using property and equity data to find refinance-ready prospects
- **Home services companies** -- targeting properties for roofing, HVAC, etc.

Customer company size skews heavily toward solopreneurs and small teams (0-9 employees). 90%+ of customers are US-based.

**Notably underserved:** Land investors, seller financing operators, and note investors are not a primary focus. BatchLeads has blog content about land investing and seller financing but no specialized tooling for these niches.

## Core Features

### Lead Generation and Property Data
- Nationwide property data across all 50 states
- 140+ property search filters (distressed, pre-foreclosure, vacant, tax lien, absentee owner, etc.)
- APN (Assessor Parcel Number) search and parcel view
- MLS keyword search for hidden on-market deals
- List stacking (combine multiple distressed indicators to prioritize leads)
- Parcel count filter for multi-parcel land opportunities
- BatchRankAI: AI-based propensity scoring that ranks leads by likelihood to sell

### Skip Tracing
- One-click skip tracing integrated into the platform
- Claims 67% accurate contact rate
- Returns phone numbers, emails, and social media profiles
- Phone verification to improve data quality over time
- Costs included in plan (with overage charges at ~$0.09-0.15/record)

### CRM and Pipeline
- Integrated CRM with lead statuses and tags
- Conversation tracking and follow-up scheduling
- Lead assignment for teams
- No visual/Kanban-style pipeline management -- users needing drag-and-drop deal boards typically integrate external CRMs (Podio, Salesforce, HubSpot, GoHighLevel)

### Marketing and Outreach
- SMS campaigns (text message blasts)
- Direct mail campaigns (postcards, letters) via in-platform partners
- Ringless voicemail drops
- Zapier integration (4,000+ app connections)
- Direct integrations with Podio, Salesforce, HubSpot, GoHighLevel

### Dialer (BatchDialer -- separate product)
- Multi-line power dialer (3-5 simultaneous lines)
- Predictive and preview dialing modes
- AI live coaching: real-time prompts, sentiment analysis, call transcription
- Phone reputation monitoring with automatic number replacement
- DNC list scrubbing and litigator scrubbing (TCPA compliance)
- Whisper coaching and barge-in for managers
- Call recording (90-365 days depending on plan)
- Property and owner data displayed during calls

### Mobile App
- Driving for Dollars: identify distressed/vacant properties while driving
- Photo capture and note-taking in the field
- Reported as buggy with crashes, data sync issues, and tag problems

### AI Features
- BatchRankAI: lead propensity scoring
- Reia AI ($19/mo add-on): instant property analysis, deal calculations, offer strategy
- Dialer AI ($89/mo add-on): real-time call guidance and transcription

## Strengths

1. **Comprehensive lead generation engine** -- 140+ filters, list stacking, and nationwide coverage make it one of the most powerful property search tools available
2. **Integrated skip tracing** -- one-click access with competitive accuracy rates, no need for third-party skip trace services
3. **All-in-one acquisition workflow** -- find leads, skip trace, launch campaigns (SMS, mail, voicemail), and call from one ecosystem
4. **BatchRankAI propensity scoring** -- AI-driven lead prioritization helps investors focus on highest-probability sellers
5. **Power dialer with AI coaching** -- real-time sentiment analysis and call prompts are genuinely differentiated in the market
6. **Scale pricing** -- volume discounts make it cost-effective for high-volume wholesalers running 10K+ leads/month
7. **Driving for Dollars mobile feature** -- useful for boots-on-the-ground prospecting
8. **PropStream backing** -- acquisition by a Stewart Information Services subsidiary provides data depth, financial stability, and access to PropStream's established user base
9. **Zapier + CRM integrations** -- plays well with existing tech stacks

## Weaknesses

1. **CRM is shallow** -- no visual pipeline, no Kanban boards, no deal-level financial tracking. Serious investors still need a separate CRM
2. **No post-acquisition tools** -- no offer generation, no contract management, no e-signatures, no closing workflow
3. **No financial/accounting features** -- cannot track income, expenses, or calculate marketing ROI at the deal level
4. **No seller financing or note management** -- zero tooling for structuring seller-financed deals, tracking payments, amortization schedules, or note portfolios
5. **No land-specific intelligence** -- no USDA/NASS data, no timber/mineral rights analysis, no rural land valuation models, no county-specific regulatory data
6. **Data accuracy complaints** -- users report needing frequent verification, duplicate leads across monthly pulls, and stale skip trace results
7. **Mobile app reliability** -- frequent crash reports, data sync failures between mobile and desktop
8. **Customer support inconsistency** -- BBB F rating, reports of billing issues, difficulty canceling accounts, unresponsive support for smaller customers
9. **Two separate products/subscriptions** -- BatchLeads and BatchDialer are billed independently, creating cost complexity and UX friction
10. **No buyer management or disposition** -- focused entirely on acquisition side; no tools for marketing deals to buyers, managing a buyers list, or tracking dispositions
11. **Steep learning curve** -- 140+ filters and complex interface overwhelm new users

## Key Differentiator to Watch

**The PropStream acquisition reshapes the competitive landscape.** As PropStream integrates BatchLeads and BatchDialer under the Stewart Information Services umbrella, the combined entity will have:
- PropStream's deep property/MLS data + BatchLeads' skip tracing and filters
- A unified lead-to-call pipeline
- Institutional backing and resources to build out CRM and post-acquisition features that BatchLeads lacked independently

**The critical question for AcreOS:** Will PropStream remain focused on high-volume wholesaling and general RE investing, or will they push into land, seller financing, and note management?

**Current signal:** PropStream's roadmap appears focused on consolidating the acquisition and serving their existing wholesaler/flipper/agent base. There is no indication they are building land-specific intelligence, seller financing tools, or note servicing capabilities. This gives AcreOS a meaningful window to own the land investing + seller financing + notes niche before a larger player notices the opportunity.

**Strategic implication:** AcreOS should not try to out-BatchLeads BatchLeads on high-volume wholesaling lead gen. Instead, AcreOS should double down on the full deal lifecycle (lead-to-payments) for land investors and note buyers -- the part of the market BatchLeads/PropStream structurally ignores. The moat is depth of workflow, not breadth of leads.
