**What I'd defer (and why):**

The moment you wire "approved if property_value > 50th percentile of market comps," you've triggered Regulation B (disparate-impact liability). Defer this until a lawyer signs off on the model-card and you've run a disparate-impact audit on historical data. The current manual-review workflows are slow but legally defensible.

**What scares me most (one named risk + mitigation):**
*Class-action jurisdiction arbitrage.* Your first few customers will be Texas-based (Wendell + land-investor base). Texas permissible-purpose law is clear. But the moment a California or New York customer uses BH for tenant screening, they're subject to CA Civ Code §1786 (tenant-specific FCRA rules) or NY Gen Bus Law §527 (licensing + bonding). If your BH product isn't licensed as a CRA in CA/NY, every screening is a per-customer violation — 30+ customers = 30+ claims = class certification. Mitigation: explicitly gate BH to TX/OK at launch (state enum in onboarding); add a legal hold at the product boundary: "Tenant screening available in TX/OK only; CA/NY support coming 2027 pending state licensing." Cost is geographic narrowing; benefit is deferring $5M+ in exposure until after Series B.
