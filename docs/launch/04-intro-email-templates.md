## Sending Rules

1. **Cold outreach:** Personalize [OPERATOR_NAME], [REGION], [CALENDAR_LINK]. Send Tuesday–Thursday 10–11am PT (highest open rate for B2B).

2. **Warm referral:** Thank [REFERRER] by name. Reference one specific detail they told you about [PROSPECT_NAME]. Send same day as the introduction if possible.

3. **Wedge customer:** Research their vertical problems in advance. [VERTICAL], [SPECIFIC_PROBLEM], [FEATURE_1–3] must be specific to them, not generic. Otherwise it reads as mass-mail.

4. **Post-call:** Send within 1 hour. Include [LOGIN_LINK] so they don't have to ask for it. Use their name once (high-trust signal).

5. **Re-engagement (14-day):** Send on day 14–15 (not day 21; too late). Tone is conversational, not sales-y. The "two quick questions" format makes it easy for them to reply even if they haven't tried it.

---

## What NOT to do

- **No "I saw you on LinkedIn."** (Everyone hates this.)
- **No "limited-time offer" or fake scarcity.** (14-day trial is real scarcity; don't manufacture more.)
- **No "I'm impressed by [generic praise]."** (Personalize or don't.)
- **No long case studies in cold email.** (Nobody reads them.)
- **No "as you may know" openings.** (They don't know. Be specific about what you know.)
