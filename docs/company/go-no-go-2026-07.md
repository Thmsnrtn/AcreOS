## First campaign recipe — $5/day, witnessed, mail-first

1. **Prerequisite:** PR #112 merged + deploy verified (`/api/version` SHA,
   one lead created and VISIBLE in the list immediately).
2. **Universe:** one county the platform already has data depth in; pull
   ~50 absentee/tax-delinquent land leads (existing import path).
3. **Channel: direct mail only.** No DNC/TCPA exposure; the scrub seam can
   stay dormant. SMS/voice stay off until the vendor pick.
4. **Witnessed mode on:** every send requires the founder's tap; the
   autopilot drafts, the founder releases. Keep `simulationMode` OFF for
   this org but leave global spend switches at current dials.
5. **Volume:** ~20 letters over week one (≈ $2–3/day at current print+post
   rates — under the $5/day line with headroom).
6. **The watch loop:** replies land in Inbox (inbound-SMS fix is live if
   sellers text the tracking number); lead flips `responded`; cockpit
   wedge tiles (outreach/replies/offers, 7d) show the funnel move —
   that's the scoreboard for the week.
7. **Abort handles (drilled):** panic stop freezes everything in one tap;
   dispatch kill-cap and daily AI backstop are armed and now audible.
8. **Success bar for week one:** the machine survives contact — zero
   customer-visible errors, every reply captured, at least one offer
   drafted. Response-rate economics come later; launch week proves the
   plumbing.
