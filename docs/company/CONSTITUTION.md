# The AcreOS Constitution

*Signed by the founder. Effective 2026-05-31. Version 1.0.*

This document is the spine of how AcreOS operates. It exists so that anyone using the product, working alongside it, or regulating it can read exactly what the company commits to — and what it will never become.

## What we are for

AcreOS exists so that anyone — solo, capital-light, learning as they go — can own property well, build durable wealth honestly, and pass it on cleanly.

## What we will not do

These commitments are code-enforced where possible and self-disciplined where not. They are not policy. They are the company's constitution.

1. We will not lie to a customer about a fact, a price, or what our AI assistant Pax did.
2. We will not use dark patterns — forced opt-ins, hidden cancellation, sneaky upsells, manipulative urgency, fake scarcity, or deceptive UX.
3. We will not collect customer data that isn't immediately useful to that customer.
4. We will make cancellation as easy as signup. Always.
5. We will not sell, share, or use customer data outside of serving the customer.
6. We will not auto-charge without explicit, recent, easily-revoked consent.
7. We will disclose our AI use clearly to every customer at first interaction.
8. We will honor data-deletion requests within 7 days.
9. We will not recommend an action against the customer's interest, even if it benefits us.
10. We will not market to vulnerable populations — anyone in financial distress, anyone identifying as under 18, anyone in disclosed crisis.
11. We will not produce "get rich quick" content. Land investing is hard, slow, real work, and we will market the discipline, not the dream.
12. Our AI assistant Pax will not give advice that crosses into fiduciary territory. Pax surfaces data and offers suggestions; the customer makes every decision about their money.

## How we operate

**AcreOS is autonomously operated by an AI executive team** working on behalf of the founder. The team makes most decisions inside this constitution; the founder retains authority over strategic positioning, capital allocation, and constitutional changes.

**Every public claim with a number or comparison is continuously verified** against the source. We call this our truth engine. If verification fails or the underlying fact degrades, the page updates honestly or stops claiming it.

**A defined percentage of net revenue goes to charity** before reaching the founder. Currently 5% at $1,000 monthly recurring revenue, scaling to 10% at $250,000 MRR. Real numbers are published monthly with linked receipts.

**The AcreOS team adheres to an Autonomy Horizon score** that measures how confidently the company can run without the founder's daily involvement. The score is computed across stability, financial reserves, compliance posture, customer health, and decision velocity. It is the team's measure of its own readiness.

## Founder privacy

The founder operates privately. Their name, photo, and personal details do not appear in customer-facing materials. AcreOS has its own public voice; the founder is not the brand.

## Annual review

This document is reviewed annually. Changes require the founder's explicit approval and are logged publicly. The version history of this document is part of the company's public record.

## Contact

Concerns about how AcreOS is living by this constitution can be sent to ethics@acreos.io. The team reads every message; the founder reviews escalations.
