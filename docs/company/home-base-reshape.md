# The Home-Base Reshape — AcreOS as the OS you connect your business to

_Founder doctrine, 2026-07-15. The company's positioning decision, made
while it is cheapest to make: before a paying base exists to migrate.
Codifies the shift from "AcreOS runs services for you" to "AcreOS is the
home base you connect your own services to." Reduces liability to its
floor, sharpens the operating-system thesis, and aligns price with the
value actually owned. Constitutional anchors: customer immutables #3
(don't collect what isn't useful), #5 (never use customer data outside
serving them), #6 (money is special), #12 (Pax is not a fiduciary);
Sovereign Principle 10 (no self-expanded authority). Companion to
`three-level-boundary.md` and `pax-jarvis.md`._

## The one-line identity

**Connect your services. Find your home base. Work your entire business
with an intelligent, autonomous assistant.** Your tools, your keys, your
data — AcreOS is the intelligent workspace over the top of all of it.

## What AcreOS provides vs. what the customer connects

The company's whole surface splits cleanly along the liability line. We
provide the layers that carry almost no risk and hold all the moat; the
customer connects the layers that carry the risk and were never our
business.

| Layer | Who provides it | Risk it carries |
|---|---|---|
| **Data** — mapping, parcels, county signals, comps, Land Credit Score, due-diligence signals | **AcreOS** (built on unencumbered open data; free early, a paid tier once MRR clears the threshold) | Near-zero — read-only intelligence, disclaimed as informational |
| **Intelligence + autonomy** — Pax, analysis, workflows, memory, the second brain; opt-in autonomy, per-user fine-tunable, clearly disclosed | **AcreOS** (the paid core) | Managed by the Pax-Jarvis wrapper: consent ledger, attribution, disclaimers, fail-closed compliance, money capped forever |
| **The workspace** — the home base, the connectors hub, native-feeling UI | **AcreOS** | None — it's software |
| **Action rails** — outreach (SMS/mail/email), note servicing, syndication, skip-trace/enrichment | **The customer connects their own** (Twilio, Lob, SendGrid, GeekPay/servicer, portals, skip-trace) under **their** credentials, via guided native wizards | Lands on the customer as the legal actor of record — where it always belonged |

## The rules

1. **AcreOS never becomes the actor of record on an external act.** Every
   send, publish, charge, or servicing action fires through an account
   the customer owns, under credentials they connected. AcreOS
   orchestrates, schedules, records, enforces compliance gates, and
   displays results — it does not act in its own name.
2. **The note vertical dashboards a connected servicer; it does not
   compute servicing law.** Customers connect GeekPay or their servicer;
   AcreOS is the intelligent dashboard over it. Any statutory computation
   we still surface is a labeled worksheet the customer verifies, never a
   determination we render (see the liability audit, Tier 1).
3. **New orgs are BYO-rails by default.** Platform-key sending is not the
   default path. There is no legacy base to migrate — this is decided at
   the cheapest possible moment.
4. **Capability gates on the connector, not on our willingness to
   front.** A send feature lights up when its rail connects. SMS
   additionally gates on the customer's A2P/10DLC registration — the
   compliant answer, not a friction workaround. The forbidden move:
   "we'll send on our keys while your registration processes." That
   reinstates everything the reshape escapes.
5. **The compliance machinery stays exactly as strong — it changes
   jobs.** Consent ledger, quiet hours, STOP handling, DNC-when-keyed:
   built to protect AcreOS-as-sender, they now protect the
   customer-as-sender and demonstrate the platform's good faith as the
   tool. Never weakened by the reshape.
6. **Price reflects owned value, not resold usage.** The paid core is
   data + intelligence + autonomy + the workspace. Usage COGS leaves the
   P&L with the rails. Pricing changes are a permanent hard-stop —
   founder decision only, never machine-set.
7. **Wizards make connecting native and easy.** Every connector an
   investor profile needs, grouped by capability, each with an
   in-product guided flow (Pax-narrated where useful) from account
   creation → key → verification. Easy rails (mail, email) work day one;
   SMS unlocks on registration. The customer never leaves the home base.

## Why this is safer AND more profitable

- **Safer**: the action rails carry the audit's Tier-1/Tier-2 liability
  (sender-of-record, servicing law, stored-value custody). Moving them to
  customer-owned accounts moves the liability with them. The credit pool
  (stored-value), auto-top-up (off-session charging), and refund
  machinery shrink toward deletion.
- **More profitable in the ways that matter**: reselling postage/SMS was
  a thin-margin line carrying disproportionate operational and legal
  weight. Dropping it trades a few points of revenue-per-customer for
  80–90%-margin subscription revenue, a smaller codebase (the
  mature-machine north star), cleaner unit economics, and a stronger
  acquisition/investor story. Vendor referral programs (Twilio, Lob)
  offset a slice of even that.
- **Stronger thesis**: "your entire land business runs here; your vendors
  plug in underneath" is the Shopify posture — the platform doesn't own
  the trucks. It is the most durable position available.

## The honest caveats

- **Not a total liability eraser.** TCPA plaintiffs have named platforms
  that merely facilitate sending. Rule 5 exists because of this — the
  compliance surface is load-bearing, not decorative.
- **Onboarding friction is the real cost.** A2P registration confuses
  professionals. The mitigation is wizards + progressive capability +
  vendor sub-account patterns where they exist — never fronting sends.
- **D4 free-first-send changes form.** "Your first Lob invoice on us"
  credit, or a free-analysis hook (which fits the new identity better).
  A decision card, not an assumption.

## Positioning

Landing page and eventual paid ads lead with the identity line. The
contrast is with point tools (a customer stitches together a servicer, a
mailer, a dialer, a CRM) versus one home base that connects them all and
adds the intelligence none of them have. Positioning only — every public
claim maps to a live, named source (the truth-engine audit enforces
this); no disparaging named-competitor claims.
