If AcreOS doubles in capability but the customer or founder must learn twice as much software, the architecture has failed.

Complexity belongs behind the interface.

## COMMAND EXPERIENCE

## REMOVE HUMAN WORK

For every workflow ask: Why is the human doing this?

If AcreOS already possesses the information, the authority, the deterministic ability, and sufficient confidence — then automate or prefill the routine step.

Examples:

* carry known information forward
* remember preferred defaults
* infer safe selections
* select suitable providers
* draft communications
* prepare documents
* detect anomalies

* generate reports

Do not automate consequential human judgment simply to reduce clicks.

## ERROR EXPERIENCE

Best-in-class software is often revealed by abnormal state.

Design deliberately for: no data, stale data, conflicting evidence, AI unavailable, provider unavailable, partial completion, failed external action, payment failure, authorization failure, offline/network loss, system degradation, autonomy blocked, uncertain outcome.

Tell the human:

* what happened
* what remains true
* what AcreOS is doing
* what they need to do, if anything
* whether anything consequential changed

Never strand them with: Something went wrong.

AcreOS should have a recognizable visual character.

## VISUAL QUALITY BAR

The target emotional quality is: calm intelligence. Not: futuristic AI dashboard.

A live company should still feel calm.
