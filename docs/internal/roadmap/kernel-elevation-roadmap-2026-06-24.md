# AcreOS Kernel — Elevation Roadmap

*A governed-autonomy kernel audited by 30 elite lenses, then adversarially re-examined against the running code. Grounded in `server/services/autopilot/*`, `server/services/solene/continuousLoop.ts`, and `shared/schema.ts`.*

## 1. Verdict: where the kernel actually stands

The kernel is a **genuinely unparalleled *design*** wrapped around a **mostly-unwired *implementation***, operating a business that **has no customers yet**. The architecture — sense → reflex-loop + daily operator → a five-gate stack (compliance → eval → budget → earned-per-domain-autonomy → witnessed-send) → measure → learn (causal world-model, Thompson sampling, policy induction) → hash-chained, principal-attributed, constitution-anchored proof-receipts, all behind a kernel/DomainPack CI boundary — is more disciplined than anything shipping in this category. But the verification is unambiguous: `continuousLoop.ts:478` ranks moves with `rankMoves(senses)` alone and never calls `predictMoveEffect`, `queryIntervention`, `allocateByRoi`, `refineModel`, or `runOperator`; `act.ts:331` earns autonomy on the raw dispatch boolean `d.success`; the consequence columns `deliveryBounced`/`paymentRecovered` exist in `experienceLog.ts` but no writer ever sets them (`recordDispatchSignal`, the only wired writer in `worker.ts:637`, writes only `dispatchSuccess`/`costUsd`/`evalScore`). So the kernel today is a **tasteful, well-gated rules engine + bandit whose three most intelligent organs are decorative, whose reward inflates on cheap completion, and whose self-sharpening moat learns from a column that is always null.** It is "paramount" in governance design and "not yet operating" in intelligence. The honest path to paramount is not more capability — it is **wiring the intelligence that exists, making the reward honest, proving one real consequence signal flows end-to-end, and refusing to gold-plate a multi-tenant future that does not exist.**

## 2. Themes

1. **Wire the dead brain.** The causal oracle (`predictMoveEffect`/`queryIntervention`), the ROI allocator (`allocateByRoi`), the daily Operator (`runOperator`/`planToActions`), and the grounding judge (`runGroundingJudgeEval`) are verified dead code with zero callers in the live decision path. The decision loop narrates the causal model instead of deciding with it. The dominant work class is *wiring*, not building.

2. **Reward hygiene.** Autonomy (`recordCleanCycle` on `d.success`), efficacy play-selection, and causal refinement (`refineModel` on the dispatch-outcome proxy) all feed off mechanical completion rather than a resolved real-consequence vote — and the strongest available "real" signal, `founderVerdict`, is also the thing being optimized away.

3. **The proof-receipt is under-built.** `proofReceipt.ts` carries `gateResults`/`situationHash`/`evalScore` as optional fields that aren't populated at the emit sites; `accountableHumanId` is a bare string with an `"unknown"` escape; the chain is application-level. It attests WHO, not WHAT-was-witnessed / WHICH-gates / WHY.

4. **Tenant-scope the cognition plane while it's free.** `proof_receipts` already has a `scope` column (`schema.ts:2599`); `autopilot_experiences` and the domain-autonomy ledger do not. Re-keying on `(scope, domain)` is a no-op today (one tenant = `platform`) and a ruinous retrofit later — *but only matters at N>1, which does not exist yet.*

5. **Fail closed by construction.** `bindingFor` returns `DEFAULT_BINDING` (`isCustomerFacing: false`, internal) for any unknown move-kind — an unseen action scores SAFE. Gate outages silently no-op. There is no atomic `panicStop`. The drift sentinel alarms but cannot contain.

6. **Calibration must be load-bearing.** The calibration machinery is global, self-grading, and discarded each tick rather than gating promotion or throttling the loop.

7. **Make consequence observable.** The world-model is refined-and-discarded every tick; its per-edge confidence trajectory — the literal heart of THE BET — is never persisted, so "self-improving" is an assertion, not an observable.

The two genuinely paramount, under-ranked items are *prove-the-signal-flows* and *fail-closed*, and the dependency between #4 (wire the oracle) and #7 (learn from consequence) is **reversed in the synthesis and corrected here.**

**#1 — Prove one consequence signal flows end-to-end.** *Current state:* `outcomeOf()` (`experienceLog.ts:47`) reasons over `paymentRecovered` and `deliveryBounced`, ranked above the eval/mechanical proxies — but `recordDispatchSignal` (the only signal writer wired into `worker.ts:637`) sets only `dispatchSuccess`/`costUsd`/`evalScore`, and there is **no `recordPaymentRecovered` or `recordDeliveryBounce` writer anywhere in the tree.** *The elementary gap:* for ~100% of rows, `outcomeOf` falls through to clause 4 (the mechanical dispatch boolean), so the "resolved consequence" the entire learning loop claims to learn from **does not exist in the data.** Every downstream move (#2, #5, #7) builds atop a signal that is structurally null. *What to build:* a `recordPaymentRecovered(dispatchId)` writer fired from the existing Stripe `invoice.paid` webhook for the *specific* dunning dispatch, and a `recordDeliveryBounce(dispatchId)` fired from the SES/SendGrid bounce/complaint webhook; an end-to-end integration test that asserts a real webhook payload mutates the experience row and flips `outcomeOf` off the mechanical clause. Until this is green, *do not refine anything on these columns.* This is the cheapest, highest-truth move in the document and it appears in no original paramount slot.

**#2 — Unify reward on a resolved `outcomeOf()` vote.** *Current state:* `applyAutonomyFeedback` in `act.ts:323-335` calls `recordCleanCycle(domain)` on raw `d.success` (a dispatch boolean), and `efficacy.ts`/`refineModel` consume the dispatch-outcome proxy. *The elementary gap:* autonomy currency inflates on cheap completion; the causal model grows confident on execution noise; the loop is rewarded for *firing*, not for *being right*. *The honest correction the synthesis got wrong:* it wanted to both unify onto `outcomeOf()` (whose top signal is `founderVerdict`) *and* stop rewarding founder approval — a contradiction. In cold-start there are no customers, so `founderVerdict` *is* the only legitimate consequence; stripping it makes autonomy *unlearnable* (every domain sits at OBSERVE forever). *What to build:* route `recordCleanCycle`/`recordAnomaly`/efficacy/`refineModel` through the **same settled `outcomeOf()` vote**, fired from the *resolved* experience after a signal accretes — a `"pending"` vote banks nothing, a clean cycle requires `vote==='success'`. Add a typed `OutcomeChannel` split: an action-quality vote (fine for `efficacy.ts` play selection) vs. a consequence vote that only fires on a real downstream metric. Founder-verdict legitimately drives autonomy *now*; as `#1`'s real signals accrue, the consequence channel takes over — which is why `#2` honestly depends on `#7`'s maturation-gating and is **not independently shippable as the synthesis claimed.** Add an invariant test: no path increments `cleanCycleCount` on a non-resolved-success vote.

**#3 — Fail closed by construction.** *Current state:* `bindingFor(moveKind)` in `act.ts:83` returns `DEFAULT_BINDING` (`isCustomerFacing: false`, `surface: "generic"`, internal) for any kind not in `MOVE_BINDINGS` — *an action the kernel has never seen scores SAFE.* Gate awaits in `policyGate.ts` have no timeout. There is no CI proof of fail-closed behavior. *The elementary gap:* the safe default for a domain-agnostic body is inverted — a foreign Foundry pack ships move-kinds the kernel has never seen, and they currently bind to the cheapest internal tier. *What to build:* make `DEFAULT_BINDING` `reversible:false, isCustomerFacing:true, noveltyN:0`; add explicit `reversible` to `MoveBinding`; feed any Operator-proposed binding as a *floor only* (can raise risk, never lower). Wrap each gate await in `withDeadline(promise, ms, onTimeout)` resolving to the safe terminal verdict (compliance/eval/budget/autonomy → block; witnessed → escalate) — **but selectively**, not blanket, per blind-spot #3 below. Add `tests/unit/autopilotGateChaos.test.ts` injecting throw/reject/hang/malformed per gate, asserting `decision !== 'pass'` and no enqueue; promote it to an invariant ratchet like the truth-immutable one.

**#4 — Complete the proof-receipt.** *Current state:* `proofReceipt.ts` declares `gateResults`, `situationHash`, `evalScore` as optional (`?`) inputs; they are not populated at the `registry.ts`/`approvalKernel.ts` emit sites. `accountableHumanId` is a bare string with an `"unknown"` escape. *The elementary gap:* a receipt that can't show the gate trail or bind the exact witnessed bytes is a legal shield with the shield missing — and it is mostly *plumbing of values that already exist upstream.* *What to build:* thread `PolicyGateDecision.results` + `decidedBy`, the frozen `contentHash` (as `payloadHash`, one canonicalizer for gate *and* receipt), the real `getDomainLevel` autonomy level, `evalScore`/`costUsd`, and `situationHash = hashPayload(senses)` (joinable to the experience reasoning trace) through `pendingHands.approvePendingHand → executeHandWitnessed → recordReceipt`. Replace `accountableHumanId` with a typed attribution quad `{principalScope, deployerOfRecord, witness{humanId,role}, affectedParty?}` (keep `accountableHumanId` as a derived alias). Reject sealing on a missing/`"unknown"` witness. `verifyReceipt` asserts non-empty `gateResults` + a `witnessed_send` entry for customer-facing/broadcast actions and `payloadHash === contentHash`. *Defer* the Ed25519/RFC-3161/Merkle/public-verifier layer (synthesis #8) entirely — see blind-spot #4.

**#5 — Wire the planning oracle into the decision.** *Current state:* `predictMoveEffect`/`queryIntervention` (`worldModel.ts`) and `allocateByRoi` (`economics.ts`) have **zero callers in `continuousLoop.ts`**, which ranks by `rankMoves` (urgency/win-rate) alone. *The elementary gap:* three disconnected organs — the causal model *predicts*, `allocateByRoi` *prices*, an EV gate could *decide* — never meet. *What to build:* within the **discretionary tier only** (never crossing the stabilize > serve > grow safety ladder), for each candidate with a `moveToLever` mapping call `predictMoveEffect` → predicted `deltaPct` × path-confidence; feed as `valuePerSuccess` into `allocateByRoi` (built from real `PlayStats` + `costUsd` + attribution-floor ARPU) and rank by expected value per dollar. **Add a null-action arm** (blind-spot #8): the model currently can only learn "pulling lever X tends to succeed" because `landEvidenceByLever` only tallies moves *taken* — so a do-nothing baseline must be an explicit candidate or the allocator will over-act. **Critical sequencing: ship #5 only after #7**, because acting on confounded edges with an EV allocator *amplifies* the over-action bias. Surface the predicted effect with an honest confidence label in the founder ask, preserving `simulate.ts`'s honesty wall.

**#6 — Close the Operator→act seam under the gate stack.** *Current state:* `runOperator`/`planToActions`/`netNewKinds` are only self-referenced within `operator.ts`; the operator runs in `runScheduledJobs.ts` disconnected from `continuousLoop`, files a daily ask, and never injects net-new moves into the act pipeline. Its "net-new is forced witnessed" safety story lives in the *proposer*, where the next engineer could bypass it. *The elementary gap:* the capability that breaks the fixed-catalog ceiling has its safety in the wrong place. *What to build:* after `rankMoves`/`applyObjectiveWeighting`, when cognition is enabled call `runOperator(briefing, moves, KNOWN_KINDS, {callModel})` and act on reconciled moves; carry `isNetNew` on `RankedMove`; have `moveToPolicyAction` force `requiresWitness:true`/`isCustomerFacing` for net-new kinds, taking the *more restrictive* of (proposed binding, a hard net-new floor). Add an Operator grounding gate — wire the currently-dead `runGroundingJudgeEval` plus a pure `groundedNumbersCheck(plan, briefing)` that fails any numeric assertion absent from the source briefing (rewrite to honest-absence on failure). Unit test: any `isNetNew:true` move reaching `runPolicyGateStack` returns `escalate`.

**#7 — Refine the model from consequence.** *Current state:* `refineModel` (`worldModel.ts`, also called from `cognitionContext.ts` and the land pack) is fed `landEvidenceByLever` — the dispatch proxy, tallying only moves taken. *The elementary gap:* confidence rises on confounded co-occurrence, not measured causality, and there is no representation of "abstain." *What to build:* record the interventional context per episode (concurrent move-set + a single-outward-move "clean attribution" flag carried on the receipt); refine an edge **only** from a cleanly-attributed intervention whose downstream variable subsequently moved, read from the real metric series — multi-move cycles update nothing (honest abstention). Give the DomainPack a per-lever `maturationDays` (a consequence vote counts only once `now - createdAt ≥ maturation`). Edges with no downstream sensor stay frozen and `summarizeModel` flags them `"unmeasured"`. **Until clean attribution exists, disconnect the move-success → confidence wire entirely.** This is the true prerequisite for #2's consequence channel and #5's oracle.

**#8 — Containment + atomic `panicStop`.** *Current state:* `detectDrift` (`safety.ts`) returns findings only; kill-switch logic is split across `founderSettings.ts` and `founder-chat/executor.ts` with no atomic stop. *The elementary gap:* detection without containment, and — per blind-spot #6 — the master switch is a `autopilot_settings` DB row reachable by anything with DB write access, *including the autopilot's own un-sandboxed bash tool.* *What to build:* on a critical `DriftFinding`, auto-trip containment *before* paging (`setDomainLevel(domain,'observe','auto-quarantine')` + dispatch off, re-enable behind an explicit founder tap). A single `panicStop()` that sets dispatch/cognition/publish false, quarantines all domains, records one reason, emits one receipt — surfaced as the most prominent Control Center control. **Make the off-switch live outside anything the agent can reach** (a process-boundary env/secret re-read but never agent-writable), so `panicStop` is atomic safety, not theater. Make the master switch a cooperative cancellation re-read between dispatch tool calls and at the top of `executeHandWitnessed`.

**#9 — Calibration load-bearing.** *Current state:* calibration is computed, global, self-grading (grades the correction it applied), and discarded each tick. *What to build:* persist `predictedRaw` and `predictedCalibrated`, grade on the raw series, build the recalibration map on a train split and validate on holdout (pass-through if it doesn't beat raw out-of-sample); make `getCalibrationPairs` per-domain and recency-weighted; hold autonomy promotion on the per-domain report; derive a scalar `loopConfidence` threaded tighten-only into risk thresholds, the Thompson temperature, and the operator's propose-licence; tie promotion to a convergence/regret gate, not a raw clean-cycle count.

**#10 — Persist consequence + decision provenance.** *Current state:* the world-model is refined-and-discarded each tick; the reasoning trace re-derives a "driver" rather than recording the decisive mechanism; suppressed moves leave no record. *What to build:* `autopilot_worldmodel_snapshots` (per-edge confidence, evidence n, source) each cycle, surfaced as a confidence sparkline + "model maturity" figure on `/founder/autopilot/story`; extend the reasoning trace with the actual `decidedBy` provenance the loop computes; a trace row for every ranked-then-killed move tagged with the suppressing gate; a lightweight SSE stream of considered+chosen+gate-verdict per tick. *Honesty constraint: snapshots show learned-vs-prior badges; unmeasured edges say so.*

**#11–#14** are real but **deliberately deprioritized**: tenant-scoping (#11) is a no-op hygiene migration worth doing while free but worth *zero* until tenant #2; the hierarchical cross-tenant prior (#14) is empirical-Bayes pooling over N=1, i.e. the tenant's own prior; constitution machine-checkability (#12) and exactly-once effects (#13) are sound but should follow the revenue-relevant core. Ranking these as paramount mistook "Foundry-aligned" for "valuable now."

## 4. Blind spots folded in from the adversarial pass

- **The prior question no lens asked: AcreOS has no paying customers.** Every move justified by the multi-tenant Foundry future (#11, #14, the cross-tenant meta-learning in #10) optimizes the learning substrate of a single-tenant system that hasn't acquired one customer. **No lens scored moves on "does this help AcreOS get its first dollar"** — the only thing that makes any of it real. The roadmap above re-weights toward customer-relevant truth (#1, #2, #3, #5) and explicitly demotes the speculative substrate.
- **The consequence signal is most likely never written (the highest-probability *actual* bug).** Verified: `paymentRecovered`/`deliveryBounced` have no writer; `outcomeOf` falls through to the mechanical clause for ~100% of rows. This was in *no* original move and is now #1.
- **The reward fix is self-contradictory and premature.** `outcomeOf` ranks `founderVerdict` first as "human ground truth" (`experienceLog.ts:49`); you cannot both unify onto it and demote it. In cold-start, founder verdict *is* the legitimate reward; the consequence channel is a *transition*, gated on #7's maturation.
- **The gate stack is a synchronous per-candidate choke point with no cognition budget.** `runPolicyGateStack` runs five gates (some LLM calls) per candidate per tick. Wrapping *everything* in `withDeadline` (synthesis #5) and wiring the Operator to propose a dozen net-new candidates can mean ~24 model calls/tick against a $50/mo budget — and the budget gate is *inside the loop it throttles* (a control deadlock). **Add a per-tick cognition token ceiling distinct from the customer-facing cost ceiling, and backpressure, before #6.**
- **The receipt is being gold-plated for a regulator that doesn't apply.** The EU AI Act Art. 50 line is hardcoded but AcreOS is a US land tool with no EU presence; an insurer underwriting $50/mo won't read a hash chain. The receipt's real job *today* is letting Tom reconstruct in plain English why the system did something — a readable timeline (#4 + #10), not Ed25519/RFC-3161/Merkle (synthesis #8, **deferred entirely**).
- **Witnessed-send is the actual scaling ceiling.** Every customer-facing/broadcast action escalates to a human tap forever by design (`policyGate.ts:191`). Foundry's "one kernel, N businesses" pitch is capped by one founder's tap-rate unless witnessed-send becomes delegable/poolable — an unexamined design question with deep liability implications. **No move addresses it; it must be named before Foundry is costed.**
- **Adversarial *customer*, not model:** nobody modeled DoS-by-escalation (mass-replies exhausting founder attention) or attribution gaming (self-referral loops inflating `autopilot_conversions`). Both are cheap to weaponize against the current design.
- **Model-misspecification has no graceful degradation.** THE BET assumes the business is a small DAG of elasticities. There is no story for a regime change or an omitted confounder, at which point a confidently-refined model is *worse* than dumb rules. The `"unmeasured"` badge (#7/#10) is the minimum honesty floor; a "fall back to rules when calibration regresses" path is the missing safety net.
- **Maintenance entropy:** ~71 files in `server/services/autopilot/` for a pre-revenue product, most unwired. The honest move absent from every list: **wire or delete — stop stacking a third intelligence layer on two unwired ones.** Every move here is *wiring or hardening existing code*; none adds a new dead subsystem.

## 5. Dependency-ordered sequencing

**Tranche 0 — Truth & safety floor (do first; revenue-relevant; cheap).**
1. **#1 prove one consequence signal flows** — everything learned is honest or null. Nothing in T1+ may refine on a signal not proven to flow.
2. **#3 fail closed** (unknown-maximally-risky + the chaos ratchet) — structural, independent, and protects every later move. Defer the blanket `withDeadline` until the cognition-budget ceiling (blind-spot #3) exists.
3. **#8 containment + `panicStop` outside the agent's reach** — the off-switch must be real before more autonomy is wired.

**Tranche 1 — Honest reward & legible governance.**
4. **#7 refine from consequence** (maturation-gated, with the move-success wire *disconnected* until clean attribution exists) — prerequisite for #2 and #5.
5. **#2 unify reward** on the resolved vote, founder-verdict-legitimate at cold-start, transitioning as #1's signals accrue.
6. **#4 complete the receipt** (plumb existing values; defer crypto) + **#10 persist provenance** — makes the loop legible and the receipt a readable timeline.

**Tranche 2 — Wire the brain (only after the reward is honest).**
7. **Cognition budget ceiling + backpressure** (blind-spot #3) — gate before any net-new proposing.
8. **#5 planning oracle** with a null-action arm — *strictly after #7*, or the EV allocator amplifies confounded over-action.
9. **#6 Operator→act seam** with the net-new-witnessed floor in the body + the grounding gate.
10. **#9 calibration load-bearing** + **#12 machine-checkable constitution/poisoning resistance** — make "earned" a measured claim.

**Tranche 3 — Foundry substrate (only when a second tenant is real or imminent).**
11. **#11 tenant-scope** (do the no-op migration opportunistically *during* T0–T2 since it's free, but don't let it gate revenue work), **#13 exactly-once effects**, **#14 hierarchical prior**, and the **#8 crypto layer** — all deferred until N>1 justifies the carrying cost.

*Rationale for the ordering inversion vs. the synthesis:* the synthesis put tenant-scoping #1 and treated #2/#5 as independent. Verification shows (a) the learning signal is probably null, so #1-prove-the-signal must precede any reward work; (b) #5-wire-the-oracle is *unsafe before* #7-honest-refinement, not after; and (c) the Foundry substrate is carrying cost until a customer exists.

## 6. How each tranche compounds into Foundry

- **Tranche 0 → the safety contract that travels unchanged.** A proven consequence-signal pipeline, an unknown-is-dangerous binding default, a chaos ratchet, and an out-of-reach `panicStop` are the primitives a foreign pack author relies on *without re-auditing the body.* "Gate outage ⇒ no outward action" and "novel action ⇒ witnessed" become CI-enforced contracts every pack inherits.
- **Tranche 1 → incentive-compatibility encoded once.** Routing autonomy/efficacy/refinement through one lag-correct, do-vs-see consequence vote means no agent can game any pack into trusting it — the single rule that makes every DomainPack safe-by-construction. A complete, legible receipt makes each pack's governance export trustworthy by construction: "here is why this exact action cleared these exact gates under this constitution version."
- **Tranche 2 → the planning claim, not the packaging claim.** `queryIntervention` + `allocateByRoi` are 100% kernel; the `moveToLever` map and edges come from the pack. Wire it once and every vertical inherits an EV-ranked capital allocator over its own causal pack with no new kernel code — and the Operator authoring novel *pack-specific* plays within that pack's gate stack is the difference between an operator and a macro. Per-reference-class calibration lets each pack earn autonomy on its own proven foresight.
- **Tranche 3 → the moat, deferred until it's earned.** Tenant-scoping unlocks per-tenant blast-radius and lawful k-anonymous pooling; the hierarchical prior is the literal statistical object behind the data-co-op (every tenant's witnessed consequence sharpens a shared prior every new tenant inherits, then sharpens to its own truth — honesty-preserving because pooling moves only the prior, never the displayed record); externally-anchored receipts make Foundry a trust *utility* rather than a control plane. **But none of it is worth one dollar until AcreOS has earned its first — which is what Tranche 0–1 are actually for.**

*Sacred invariants preserved throughout: no fabricated numbers (every figure carries its source or says "unmeasured"); attribution is always a lower bound (absence ≠ failure, never fed to the learning loop); the brain never bypasses the body's safety floor (net-new and unknown actions take the more restrictive binding, gates fail to the safe terminal verdict, and the off-switch lives outside the agent's reach).*
